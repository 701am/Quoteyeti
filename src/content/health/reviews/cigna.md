## How to get the best rate from Cigna

The biggest rate drivers at Cigna are subsidy eligibility and wellness programs. Stacking these discounts can reduce premiums by 15–30% depending on eligibility.

For members comparing Cigna against competitors, focus on three things: the rate after all eligible discounts, the actual NAIC complaint ratio (not just J.D. Power's marketing-friendly scores), and the specific add-ons each carrier offers for your situation. The cheapest base rate isn't always the best total value.
