## Who Aetna is best for

Based on QuoteYeti's evaluation, Aetna fits members who want an established carrier with broad coverage options. Customer satisfaction scores below industry leaders is the most important caveat to consider.

## How to get the best rate from Aetna

The biggest rate drivers at Aetna are subsidy eligibility and cvs healthhub discount. Stacking these discounts can reduce premiums by 15–30% depending on eligibility.

For members comparing Aetna against competitors, focus on three things: the rate after all eligible discounts, the actual NAIC complaint ratio (not just J.D. Power's marketing-friendly scores), and the specific add-ons each carrier offers for your situation. The cheapest base rate isn't always the best total value.
