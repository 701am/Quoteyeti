## Who UnitedHealthcare is best for

Based on QuoteYeti's evaluation, UnitedHealthcare fits members who want an established carrier with broad coverage options. Higher base rates than smaller marketplace carriers is the most important caveat to consider.

## How to get the best rate from UnitedHealthcare

The biggest rate drivers at UnitedHealthcare are subsidy eligibility and wellness programs. Stacking these discounts can reduce premiums by 15–30% depending on eligibility.

For members comparing UnitedHealthcare against competitors, focus on three things: the rate after all eligible discounts, the actual NAIC complaint ratio (not just J.D. Power's marketing-friendly scores), and the specific add-ons each carrier offers for your situation. The cheapest base rate isn't always the best total value.
