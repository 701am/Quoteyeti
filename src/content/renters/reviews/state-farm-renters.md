## Who State Farm is best for

Based on QuoteYeti's evaluation, State Farm fits renters who value strong customer service and are willing to pay a modest premium for it. Base rates not the lowest in the renters market is the most important caveat to consider.

## How to get the best rate from State Farm

The biggest rate drivers at State Farm are multi-policy bundle and home alert protection. Stacking these discounts can reduce premiums by 15–30% depending on eligibility.

For renters comparing State Farm against competitors, focus on three things: the rate after all eligible discounts, the actual NAIC complaint ratio (not just J.D. Power's marketing-friendly scores), and the specific add-ons each carrier offers for your situation. The cheapest base rate isn't always the best total value.
