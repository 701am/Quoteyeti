## Who Progressive is best for

Based on QuoteYeti's evaluation, Progressive fits renters who want an established carrier with broad coverage options. Coverage limits can be lower than dedicated renters carriers is the most important caveat to consider.

## How to get the best rate from Progressive

The biggest rate drivers at Progressive are multi-policy bundle and quote and pay online. Stacking these discounts can reduce premiums by 15–30% depending on eligibility.

For renters comparing Progressive against competitors, focus on three things: the rate after all eligible discounts, the actual NAIC complaint ratio (not just J.D. Power's marketing-friendly scores), and the specific add-ons each carrier offers for your situation. The cheapest base rate isn't always the best total value.
