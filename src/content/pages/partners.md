QuoteYeti is an independent insurance research publisher. We work with a network of licensed insurance carriers, agencies, and lead-aggregation partners to connect readers with quotes. This page documents those relationships in full.

## How partnerships work

When you request a quote through QuoteYeti, we route your information to one or more licensed insurance carriers or agents who can quote your situation. We are compensated by these partners on a per-lead or per-acquisition basis. The compensation amount varies by vertical, carrier, and consumer profile.

Compensation is paid to QuoteYeti as a publisher — **not to individual editors, writers, or anyone involved in producing rankings or reviews.**

## What partnerships influence

Partner relationships determine **which carriers can quote you** when you submit a quote request. We work with the carriers in our partner network — we cannot route quote requests to carriers outside the network.

When you see a partner-offers grid on a thank-you page (for example, after submitting an auto quote request), the carriers shown are sponsored placements. This is clearly labeled with an "Advertising disclosure" notice on every grid.

## What partnerships do NOT influence

Partner relationships do **not** influence:

- **Editorial rankings** — Our "Best of" rankings are determined by our scoring methodology. A carrier paying us more does not rank higher. A carrier paying us less does not rank lower.
- **Review scores** — Star ratings and category scores in our reviews are determined by the editorial team using documented criteria.
- **Editorial conclusions** — When we say "Carrier X is our top pick for young drivers," that conclusion is independent of whether Carrier X is in our partner network.
- **Methodology weights** — The factors and weights we use to score carriers are set by editorial and reviewed independently of the partnership team.

If a carrier in our partner network performs poorly in our editorial scoring, we will say so. If a carrier we don't partner with performs well, we will still rank it highly — even though we earn nothing if you choose them.

## Compensation models

We earn compensation through three primary models:

1. **Per-lead compensation** — A fixed fee when a consumer submits a complete quote request that meets the partner's eligibility criteria.
2. **Per-acquisition compensation** — A larger fee when a consumer who came through us purchases a policy.
3. **Flat-rate sponsorship** — A monthly or quarterly fee for sponsored placement on partner-offer grids (always labeled).

We do not accept compensation in exchange for editorial coverage, rank placement, or favorable reviews.

## Why we disclose this

Transparency is a precondition of trust. You should know how a publisher makes money so you can evaluate its content honestly. Our goal is for you to read our reviews knowing exactly where the incentives lie — and to verify, through our published methodology, that the incentives are not driving the editorial output.
