## Our commitment

QuoteYeti is committed to making our website accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We work to ensure compliance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards and applicable provisions of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

## What we do

- **Semantic HTML** — Pages use proper heading hierarchy, landmarks, and ARIA roles to support screen readers and assistive technology.
- **Keyboard navigation** — All interactive elements are reachable and operable via keyboard alone. Focus indicators are visible on all focusable elements.
- **Color contrast** — Text and meaningful visual elements meet WCAG AA contrast ratios against their backgrounds.
- **Resizable text** — Pages render correctly when text is enlarged up to 200%.
- **Alternative text** — Meaningful images include descriptive alt text. Decorative images are marked as such.
- **Form labels** — All form fields have explicit labels and clear error messaging.
- **Reduced motion** — We respect the `prefers-reduced-motion` setting and minimize animations for users who request it.

## Known limitations

We are actively working on:

- Improving keyboard navigation patterns on complex interactive components (multi-step quote flows)
- Expanding screen-reader testing across all 8 vertical sections
- Refining color contrast on partner-offer cards where carrier logos use brand colors we don't control

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on our site, please let us know and we'll work to resolve it.

## Feedback

We welcome accessibility feedback. If you experience difficulty using the site or have suggestions for improvement:

- **Email**: accessibility@quoteyeti.com
- We aim to respond within 2 business days.

## Third-party content

Our site connects users to third-party carriers and agents through partner offers. We do not control the accessibility of partner sites, but we ask our partners to maintain their own accessibility commitments.

## Updates

This statement is reviewed and updated quarterly. We periodically test the site against WCAG 2.1 AA criteria and incorporate findings into our development roadmap.
