Every ranking on QuoteYeti is built from a documented scoring rubric. This page explains the rubric.

## The three universal pillars

Across all eight verticals, we score carriers on three primary dimensions:

1. **Cost** — How affordable is the carrier relative to peers for a representative consumer profile?
2. **Customer experience** — How do real customers rate the carrier? How does it handle claims, service, and policy management?
3. **Coverage breadth** — How comprehensive are the carrier's coverage options and discounts?

The relative weight of these three pillars varies by vertical (a $20/mo renters policy is a different cost calculus than a $1500/yr auto premium).

## Auto insurance

| Pillar | Weight | What we measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost | 60% | Average annual premium for a baseline 40-year-old driver with clean record, good credit, full coverage — across all 50 states |
| Customer experience | 30% | J.D. Power claims and customer-service scores, NAIC complaint index, app-store ratings |
| Coverage breadth | 10% | Available endorsements, discount programs, telematics options |

## Home insurance

| Pillar | Weight | What we measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost | 50% | Average annual premium for a $300K dwelling in moderate-risk markets |
| Customer experience | 35% | J.D. Power home-insurance satisfaction, NAIC complaints, claim-payout reputation |
| Coverage breadth | 15% | Replacement cost terms, perils covered, extended/guaranteed replacement options |

## Life insurance

| Pillar | Weight | What we measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost | 40% | Sample term and whole-life rates across age brackets |
| Underwriting | 25% | Speed, no-exam options, accommodation of common health conditions |
| Financial strength | 20% | AM Best, S&P, Moody's, Fitch ratings |
| Customer experience | 15% | Service, policy administration, claim payout reputation |

## Business insurance

| Pillar | Weight | What we measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coverage relevance | 35% | Industry-specific policy options, exclusions, limits |
| Cost | 30% | Sample pricing for representative business profiles |
| Underwriting accessibility | 20% | Speed, online vs. agent-required, eligibility breadth |
| Customer experience | 15% | Service quality, claims, online policy management |

## Health insurance

| Pillar | Weight | What we measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Network quality | 30% | Provider network size, in-network reputation, hospital tier-1 access |
| Plan design | 25% | Deductibles, OOP maximums, copay structure, prescription coverage |
| Cost | 25% | Premium relative to subsidized and unsubsidized peer plans |
| Customer experience | 20% | CMS star ratings (Medicare Advantage), NCQA accreditation, member satisfaction |

## Renters insurance

| Pillar | Weight | What we measure |

| Customer experience | 20% | App quality, claim speed, customer reviews |

## Pet insurance

| Pillar | Weight | What we measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost | 35% | Sample monthly premium for dogs and cats at multiple age tiers |
| Coverage terms | 35% | Reimbursement %, lifetime caps, pre-existing condition treatment |
| Customer experience | 30% | Claim reimbursement speed, payout consistency, customer reviews |

## Travel insurance

| Pillar | Weight | What we measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coverage breadth | 40% | Trip cancellation reasons covered, medical limits, evacuation coverage |
| Cost | 30% | Sample pricing for representative trips |
| Claims experience | 20% | Documentation requirements, payout speed, denial rates |
| Customer experience | 10% | Support availability, app quality, member reviews |

## Data sources

- State Department of Insurance rate filings
- J.D. Power studies (where applicable to vertical)
- NAIC consumer complaint indices
- AM Best, S&P Global, Moody's, Fitch financial strength ratings
- CMS Star Ratings (Medicare)
- NCQA accreditation (health)
- Carrier policy documents and product pages
- Public regulatory filings

## Updates

Methodology is reviewed annually. When we change weights or measurement criteria, the change is announced and the affected rankings are re-scored. Significant methodology changes are documented in our [editorial updates log](/editorial/).
