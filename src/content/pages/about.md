## About QuoteYeti

QuoteYeti is an insurance comparison marketplace covering eight insurance verticals: auto, home, life, business, health, renters, pet, and travel.

We exist because shopping for insurance is needlessly painful — and the existing comparison sites mostly serve the carriers, not the customer.

## How we work

We analyze rates from top carriers in every state, publish independent guides written by licensed insurance experts and journalists, and connect you with the right coverage for your situation.

## How we make money

Carriers pay us a commission when you purchase a policy through us. Our editorial rankings and recommendations are independent of those partnerships.
