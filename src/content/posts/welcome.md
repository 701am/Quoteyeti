## A bigger Yeti

QuoteYeti started as an auto insurance comparison site. We're now expanding to cover seven more verticals: home, life, business, health, renters, pet, and travel insurance.

The mission hasn't changed: make insurance shopping less painful, less pushy, and more transparent.

## What's coming

Over the next few months we'll be publishing guides, calculators, and provider rankings across every vertical. Each one will follow the same standards we built for auto: independent editorial, verifiable methodology, and zero invented statistics.

Stick around. The Yeti's just getting started.
