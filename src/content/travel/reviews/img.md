## Who IMG is best for

Based on QuoteYeti's evaluation, IMG fits travelers who want an established carrier with broad coverage options. Less focused on short-term domestic travel is the most important caveat to consider.

## How to get the best rate from IMG

The biggest rate drivers at IMG are group plans. Stacking these discounts can reduce premiums by 15–30% depending on eligibility.

For travelers comparing IMG against competitors, focus on three things: the rate after all eligible discounts, the actual NAIC complaint ratio (not just J.D. Power's marketing-friendly scores), and the specific add-ons each carrier offers for your situation. The cheapest base rate isn't always the best total value.
