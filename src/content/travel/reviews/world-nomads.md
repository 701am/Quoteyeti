## Who World Nomads is best for

Based on QuoteYeti's evaluation, World Nomads fits travelers who value strong customer service and are willing to pay a modest premium for it. Higher premiums than non-adventure-focused competitors is the most important caveat to consider.

## How to get the best rate from World Nomads

The biggest rate drivers at World Nomads are bundling and a clean claims history. Bundling with other products is the most reliable way to lower your premium.

For travelers comparing World Nomads against competitors, focus on three things: the rate after all eligible discounts, the actual NAIC complaint ratio (not just J.D. Power's marketing-friendly scores), and the specific add-ons each carrier offers for your situation. The cheapest base rate isn't always the best total value.
