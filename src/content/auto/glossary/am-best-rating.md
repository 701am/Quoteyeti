## What is am best rating?

AM Best ratings measure an insurance company's financial strength — its ability to pay claims now and in the future. Ratings range from A++ (superior) down through B, C, and D (vulnerable).

## Details

Financial strength matters because insurance is a promise to pay claims later. If the insurer is financially weak, that promise is weaker. Most major carriers carry A or A+ ratings. Anything below A- is worth questioning. AM Best is the most widely cited insurance rating agency; S&P, Moody's, and Fitch also rate insurers.
