## What is comprehensive coverage?

Comprehensive coverage pays for damage to your vehicle from events other than collisions: theft, vandalism, severe weather, fire, falling objects, hitting an animal.

## Details

Comprehensive is the second layer of vehicle protection after collision. It typically has a separate deductible. If you live in an area with high theft, severe weather, or wildlife collisions, comprehensive is essential. Most lenders require it on financed vehicles.
