## What is naic complaint index?

The NAIC Complaint Index is a standardized measure of how many consumer complaints an insurer receives relative to its market share. An index of 1.00 is average; below 1.00 is fewer complaints than average, above 1.00 is more.

## Details

The National Association of Insurance Commissioners (NAIC) collects complaint data from every state insurance department. The complaint index is normalized so a small insurer with proportionally few complaints can be compared to a large one with more total complaints. It's one of the most useful third-party measures of customer experience.
