## What is endorsement?

An endorsement is any modification to your standard policy — usually an add-on coverage or limit increase. Examples include rideshare coverage, gap insurance, custom equipment coverage, or accident forgiveness.

## Details

Endorsements customize your policy beyond the base coverages. Each endorsement adds to your premium but provides specific protection that base coverages don't. Common endorsements include rental reimbursement, roadside assistance, OEM parts replacement, and new-car replacement.
