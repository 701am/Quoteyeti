## What is verbal (or descriptive) threshold?

This outlines the sort of significant injuries that must be sustained before a person can bring a bodily injury or damages lawsuit against the motorist who caused the accident. Here are some of the insurance companies you may review: [Allstate](<https://www.allstate.com/>), [Liberty Mutual](<https://www.libertymutual.com/>), [Geico](<https://www.geico.com/>).
