## What is medical payments (medpay)?

MedPay is a low-cost add-on that covers medical expenses for you and your passengers after an accident, regardless of fault. Limits are typically low (\$1,000-\$10,000) but the coverage is broad.

## Details

MedPay overlaps with PIP in no-fault states but is broader in some respects — it can cover you as a pedestrian, in someone else's car, or on public transit. In states with PIP, MedPay is optional supplemental coverage; in other states, it's the primary first-party medical coverage.
