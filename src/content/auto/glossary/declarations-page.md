## What is declarations page?

The declarations page (or 'dec page') is the summary front of your policy. It shows your name, vehicle, coverage limits, deductibles, premium, and effective dates. It's the quickest way to check what you actually have.

## Details

Lenders, employers, and rental car companies often request a copy of your dec page as proof of insurance. Keep a copy somewhere accessible. Most insurers let you download the dec page from their app or website.
