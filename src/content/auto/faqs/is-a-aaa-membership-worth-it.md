### **AAA Membership Fee**

The time has come for the answer to the most frequently asked question: How much does AAA membership cost?

AAA membership is available for either monthly or annual fees. The costs vary by state. You can choose a yearly payment by looking at the below price range:

  * AAA Classic memberships are $38-$74 per annum and $25-$48 for each additional member.
  * AAA Plus membership costs $60-$124 per annum and $34-$80 for each additional member.
  * AAA Premier membership costs $77 to $164 annually and $45 to $109 per additional member.

### **How do I downgrade my AAA membership?**

You can change your AAA membership by either calling AAA Member Services or from your account.
