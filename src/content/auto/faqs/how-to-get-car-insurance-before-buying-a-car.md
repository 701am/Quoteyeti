## Insurance needs to be purchased as soon as the car is purchased.

Before you leave the lot, you should have automobile insurance. However, if you have an existing insurance policy, things are a bit different.

Before your new car is added to your policy, your current auto insurance policy may provide a grace period that extends coverage to the vehicle.

However, this time does not last indefinitely. The majority of grace periods are only valid for a week to 30 days, so be sure to contact your insurance provider as soon as possible.

There may also be restrictions on the length of this grace period. It might only cover replacements and not extras. Depending on your policy, the temporary coverage may or may not include comprehensive and accident coverage.

Before you take delivery of your new automobile, make sure you know what's covered under your existing insurance plan.
