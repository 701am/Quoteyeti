---
title: "How often should I shop my auto insurance?"
question: "How often should I shop my auto insurance?"
description: "When and why to compare auto insurance quotes."
category: "Shopping"
publishDate: 2026-01-08
updatedDate: 2026-05-01
---

**At minimum, every renewal** — typically every 6 or 12 months. Rates can change significantly at renewal, even without claims or violations.

**Definitely shop after**:

- A major life event (move, marriage, new vehicle, teen driver added)
- A claim or ticket falls off your record (typically 3-5 years)
- Significant credit improvement
- A rate increase from your current carrier
- A bundling change (added or dropped home/renters insurance)

Get quotes from at least **3-5 carriers**. The spread between cheapest and most expensive for the same coverage often exceeds 40%, and the carrier that was cheapest 3 years ago may not be cheapest now.
