When it comes to car registration, many people think that it must be done in their own name. However, there may be times when someone wants to register their car in someone else's name, and this is possible under certain circumstances.

## Who can Register a Vehicle in Another Person's Name?

In most cases, it is not possible for an individual to register their car in another person's name. The primary exception to this rule is if the two people are related. So, for example, a parent can register their child's car in their name if they are the legal guardians of the child. Additionally, an individual can register a vehicle in their name as a gift for another person, as long as they can provide the proper paperwork to prove that the car is a gift.

The other common exception is for businesses. Companies can register their vehicles in another person's name, provided that the individual is the owner of the business and the vehicle is used for the purposes of the business. In most cases, companies will register their vehicles in the name of their company or the name of the owner of the company.

## What Are the Laws Around Registering a Vehicle in Another Person's Name?

The laws around registering a vehicle in someone else's name vary from state to state. In some states, it is illegal to do so without the permission of the individual to whom the vehicle belongs. 

Other states allow for it under certain circumstances, but it may require additional paperwork, such as a Power of Attorney or a Court Order.

## Are There Any Benefits to Registering a Vehicle in Someone Else's Name?

There are some benefits to registering a vehicle in someone else's name. For instance, it can be beneficial if the vehicle owner is a minor, since they may not have the legal capacity to register a vehicle in their own name. It can also be helpful if the vehicle owner is elderly or disabled, since it can make it easier for them to register their vehicle. 

Additionally, it can make it easier for businesses to register their vehicles in the name of the company, since it can simplify the paperwork associated with registering a vehicle in a business's name.
