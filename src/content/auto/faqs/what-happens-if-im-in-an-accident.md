Step-by-step after an accident:

1. **Check for injuries**. Call 911 if anyone is hurt.
2. **Move to safety** if vehicles are drivable and you're in traffic.
3. **Call the police** — even minor accidents should have a report on file.
4. **Document the scene**: photos of all vehicles, license plates, damage, surroundings, road conditions.
5. **Exchange information**: name, phone, address, insurance carrier, policy number, license plate.
6. **Get witness contact info** if anyone witnessed the accident.
7. **Don't admit fault** to the other driver or in your statement — let your insurer determine fault.
8. **Report to your insurer** as soon as possible, even if you don't plan to file a claim.

Your insurer will assign an adjuster, investigate, and determine fault and coverage. Keep records of medical visits, repair estimates, and any out-of-pocket expenses.
