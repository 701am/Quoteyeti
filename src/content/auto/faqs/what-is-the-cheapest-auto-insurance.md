There's no single 'cheapest' auto insurance company — the answer depends entirely on your profile.

**Generally most competitive for typical drivers**:

- GEICO and Progressive often quote among the lowest rates nationally
- State Farm wins on customer service but isn't always cheapest
- USAA is consistently cheapest for military members and families

**Cheapest for high-risk drivers**:

- Non-standard carriers like Direct Auto, The General, Dairyland
- Progressive often has more flexible underwriting than other nationals

**Cheapest for young drivers**:

- State Farm with Steer Clear program
- Geico with Good Student discount
- Erie Insurance in eligible states

**Cheapest for safe drivers**:

- Carriers with strong telematics programs: Progressive Snapshot, State Farm Drive Safe & Save, Allstate Drivewise
- Root Insurance (telematics-first underwriting)

**The reliable approach**: get quotes from at least 5 carriers — mix of nationals (GEICO, Progressive, State Farm), regionals (Erie, NJM, Auto-Owners depending on state), and specialized carriers if you're high-risk or military.
