## Do I Have to notify My Insurance That I'm an Uber Eats Driver?

Yes, you must inform your insurance provider of your employment with Uber Eats. Failure to do so may result in serious consequences.

[When you first purchased your policy](<https://www.quoteyeti.com/how-to-buy-car-insurance/>), your insurance provider questioned you about your driving habits and car usage. One of the questions was if you use your automobile for business purposes.

Insurance companies will consider your response to this question when determining your liability. If you are involved in an accident while conducting business but did not disclose that you were doing so, you will not be covered.

If you tell your insurance provider a false story about your driving in order to obtain coverage or submit a claim, you risk getting into far deeper problems. Insurance fraud is described as fabricating information in order to gain coverage or make a claim. If you attempt to circumvent insurance and disclosure regulations in this manner, you risk imprisonment or a fine.

Even if you are not involved in an accident, failing to notify your insurer about your side business could result in severe consequences. If your insurer discovers that you have been using your car for commercial purposes, it has the right to terminate your coverage entirely.

This will significantly complicate the process of obtaining another policy and will almost certainly cost you hundreds of dollars in [premiums](<https://www.quoteyeti.com/glossary/premium/>).
