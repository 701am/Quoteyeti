If you’ve recently been convicted of a DUI, you may be wondering how to find car insurance coverage. It can be difficult to find an insurer willing to cover your vehicle after a DUI, but it is possible. We’re here to help guide you through the process and provide tips for finding the best possible policy for your situation.

## Understanding the Impact of a DUI on Car Insurance

According to Forbes Advisor's analysis, the national average auto insurance rate increase for drivers with a DUI is 70%. A DUI can have a significant impact on your car insurance rates, making them significantly higher than for drivers without a DUI conviction. This is because insurers view drivers with DUIs as higher risk, and therefore charge them more for coverage. 

Additionally, your state's DMV will notify your insurance company when you get a DUI, so it's a good idea to contact your insurance company in advance to discuss the potential rate increase. 

Furthermore, your insurer may add a “surcharge” to your policy, usually a percentage of the premium that you pay each year. It's important to shop around and compare quotes from different auto insurance companies when looking for car insurance after a DUI to ensure you get the best rate possible.

## Researching Auto Insurance Companies After a DUI

After a DUI conviction, it’s important to research auto insurance companies to find the best rates and coverage. Saving money on car insurance after a DUI can be done by checking with trusted providers like Progressive, State Farm, NJM, MAPFRE, and USAA. 

These companies are known for offering the most competitive rates for those with driving offenses. It’s important to make sure you meet the guidelines for coverage in your state and to check if your insurance company accesses your motor vehicle report. 

Depending on the insurance company, they may penalize those with DUIs or DWIs. Understanding how a DUI or DWI will affect your car insurance premium is also essential in order to find the best options for coverage and cost.

## Look for Discounts and Other Options

After researching auto insurance companies, drivers with a DUI should look for discounts and other options. Many companies offer discounts for drivers who have taken a safe-driving course or have gone a certain amount of time without an accident. 

USAA is one company that offers discounts for drivers with a DUI, and State Farm also offers some discount options for customers with DUIs. 

Additionally, Farmers offers up to 15% off their car insurance rates for drivers with a clean driving record. Drivers who are willing to look for discounts and other options can often find ways to save money on car insurance after a DUI conviction.

## Filing an SR-22 Form

[Filing an SR-22 form is a legal requirement after a DUI conviction in most states](<https://www.quoteyeti.com/faqs/sr-22-car-insurance/>). Once you submit it to your insurance company, they will add the insurance endorsement to your policy and notify the state. An SR-22 form is a certificate of financial responsibility that proves a driver has the minimum required auto insurance. 

Your auto insurance provider must file the SR-22 form on your behalf, although not all insurance companies offer this option. In these cases, you will need to look for other options to secure car insurance after a DUI. 

Understanding the impact of a DUI on car insurance, researching auto insurance companies and looking for discounts are all important steps when seeking car insurance after a DUI conviction.
