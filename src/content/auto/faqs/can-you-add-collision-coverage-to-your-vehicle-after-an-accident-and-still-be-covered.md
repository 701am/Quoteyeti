## What is Collision Coverage?

Collision coverage is a type of car insurance coverage that protects you financially in the event of an accident. It pays for damage to your car if you are at fault, or if you are not at fault, it pays for damages to the other driver's car. In the event of a collision, it pays for repair costs to your vehicle, up to the policy's limits.

## When Can You Add Collision Coverage?

You can add collision coverage to your vehicle at any time, provided you are in good standing with your insurance company. If you have an existing policy, you can upgrade it to include collision coverage. If you are shopping around for new insurance, you can easily add collision coverage to your policy.

## Do I Have to Make a Claim to Add Collision Coverage?

No, you do not have to make a claim to add collision coverage. You can add the coverage to your policy without ever making a claim. However, depending on the circumstances of your accident, you may need to make a claim in order to receive compensation.

## What Are the Benefits of Adding Collision Coverage?

Adding collision coverage to your policy has a few key benefits. First, it will help cover the costs of repairs to your vehicle in the event of an accident, even if you are found to be at fault. Second, it can help protect your personal finances from unexpected costs. Lastly, it can help reduce your stress and worry if you are ever involved in an accident and need to make a claim.

Before adding collision coverage, there are a few things to consider. First, you should make sure you understand the details of your policy and what coverage you will receive.
