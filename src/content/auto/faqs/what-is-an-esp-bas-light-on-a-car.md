An ESP BAS (Electronic Stability Program – Brake Assist System) light on a car is an indicator light that is used to show the driver any issues with the Electronic Stability Program (ESP) and the Brake Assist System (BAS). The ESP is an advanced safety system, which helps the driver stay in control in emergency situations, while the BAS helps the driver come to a stop faster.

The ESP BAS light will come on if there is a problem with either system, or if the car is unable to detect the presence of either one. This can happen if the system has been deactivated or has malfunctioned, or if it has been disconnected from the car’s computer.

## What is the difference between ESP BAS and ABS light?

It is important to note that the ESP BAS light should not be confused with the ABS (Anti-lock Braking System) light. While the ABS light indicates an issue with the vehicle’s braking system, the ESP BAS light indicates a problem with the ESP and BAS systems.

If the ESP BAS light comes on, it is important to take your vehicle to a certified mechanic or automotive technician to get it checked out. An experienced technician can diagnose and repair any issues with either system, so that the vehicle will be safe for driving.

It is also important to note that the ESP BAS light should not be ignored. Ignoring the light can lead to further damage, and it can also lead to a decrease in vehicle safety. Taking the time to get the light checked out is the best way to ensure optimal safety on the road.
