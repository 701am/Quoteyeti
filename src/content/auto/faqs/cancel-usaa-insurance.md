## How To Cancel USAA Car Insurance?

To cancel [USAA coverage](<https://www.quoteyeti.com/usaa-insurance-review/>), the subscriber must go online to https://members.usaa.com/, mail a request to the address below, or call 1-800-531-8722 to speak with a representative. The cancellation can be postponed for a date of your choice or activated right away.

## Requirements To Cancel USAA Insurance

You'll need your name, address, birth date, policy information, and the day you want your coverage to expire.

  * You may cancel your membership by phone by calling 1-800-531-8722.
  * You can cancel your policy by mail if you send USAA a letter that includes your name, address, date of birth, policy details, and the day you want your insurance to run out. Sign, date, and send a letter to: USAA 9800 Fredricksburg Road / San Antonio TX 78288.

## Does USAA Refund Your Car Insurance Premium When You Cancel?

If you've paid your premium for the policy period in full, USAA will credit any unused portion. There is no cancellation fee or penalty for terminating your coverage early with USAA.

It's crucial to remember that you don't have to cancel your policy because of life circumstances. If you're relocating or purchasing a new vehicle, for example, USAA should be updated with your mailing address and vehicle information.

## Find New Car Insurance Before Canceling USAA

If you're canceling your old policy in order to switch to a new one, be sure to activate the new policy before canceling the old one. This avoids having your insurance lapse, which will eventually result in higher premiums.
