question: "How is auto insurance calculated?"
description: "The factors that determine your auto insurance premium."

publishDate: 2026-01-08
---

Auto insurance rates are calculated based on a mix of factors including:

- Your driving history
- The make and model of your vehicle
- Your ZIP code
- Your age and credit score (in most states)
- The coverage limits you choose
- Available discounts

Each carrier weights these factors differently, which is why getting multiple quotes matters.
