## Factors Influencing Acura Insurance Costs

You’re probably thinking, _Okay, but what makes the rates go up or down?_ I got you. Here’s the truth:

#### 1\. Driver Age

Age matters. A lot.

  * **17-year-old drivers**? Buckle up for a jaw-dropping **$8,017 annually**.
  * **40-year-old drivers**? You’re paying about **$2,361**.



Lesson: Time heals everything… including insurance premiums.

#### 2\. Driving Record

Insurance companies love clean records.

  * Example: If you drive an Acura ILX: 
    * **Clean record** : You pay **$2,435 annually**.
    * **Recent accident** : Say hello to **$3,575 annually**.



One fender bender can cost you **over $1,000 extra** per year. Ouch.

#### 3\. Credit Score

Insurance and credit? It doesn’t seem fair, but in most states, it’s a factor.

  * **Good credit** : ILX insurance = **$2,435**.
  * **Poor credit** : ILX insurance = **$4,182**.



That’s almost _double_. Protect your score like your life depends on it.

#### 4\. Location

City traffic. Theft rates. State laws. Where you live matters. Urban areas? You’ll pay more. Rural? Lucky you.

#### 5\. Coverage Type

  * **Full Coverage** : More protection = more $$$. For example, MDX full coverage costs **$155/month**.
  * **Liability Only** : Basic protection for **$85/month**.



Bottom line: Choose wisely.

## How to Save on Acura Insurance

Want to save money? Here’s how you win the insurance game:

  1. **Drive Clean** : No tickets, no accidents. Period.
  2. **Bundle Policies** : Combine auto and home insurance. Easy win.
  3. **Compare Quotes** : Be picky. Compare prices. They _will_ vary.
  4. **Snag Discounts** : Look for safe driver, multi-car, or low mileage discounts.
  5. **Pick Smart Models** : RDX = Lower insurance. RLX = Higher insurance. Choose wisely.



Saving on insurance isn’t magic. It’s strategy.
