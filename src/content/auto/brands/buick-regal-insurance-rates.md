## **About the Buick Regal**

Since 1973, the [Buick Regal](<https://www.buick.com/discover/legacy-vehicles/regal>) has existed and 2018 will introduce the sixth version. The medium-size sports sedan is famous for its nimble handling and performance. Production of the Buick Regal in North America ceased in 2004 and was substituted with the LaCrosse Buick. In 2011, Buick returned the Regal model. More than 40% of Regal customers come from brands other than General Motors, according to General Motors.
