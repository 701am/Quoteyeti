[Volkswagen CC](<https://www.volkswagen-newsroom.com/en/cc-3623>) insurance costs on average $220 per month — or $2,640 per year. Your exact cost will vary according to your driving record, the number of miles you travel each year, and other factors.

## **About the Volkswagen CC**

Since 2008, Volkswagen has offered the CC. The CC model will be phased out in 2017 and replaced by the Arteon model. The midsize luxury automobile is distinguished by its affordable starting price and premium interior.

## **Volkswagen CC money-saving safety features**

If your vehicle is equipped with these safety features, you may be able to save money on your Volkswagen CC insurance cost.

  * Anti-lock brakes
  * Stability control
  * Airbags
  * Seatbelt pretensioners
  * Anti theft system



## **How safe is the Volkswagen CC?**

The [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA)](<http://nhtsa.gov/>) only assessed the 2017 Volkswagen CC's rollover crash test. The federal agency assigned the rollover test a four-star safety grade out of five. The [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS)](<http://iihs.org/>) assigned a marginal grade to the small overlap front test, which is below average. All other IIHS crash testing resulted in an excellent safety rating, the best attainable.

Lane departure warning, automated post-collision braking, and blind spot monitoring are all standard on the [Volkswagen CC](<https://www.jdpower.com/cars/2013/volkswagen/cc/sedan-4d-sport>) model.
