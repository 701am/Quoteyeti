The average premium for a [Nissan Sentra](<https://www.nissanusa.com/vehicles/cars/sentra.html>) insurance costs is $220 per month, or $2,640 per year, according to the National Insurance Crime Bureau. According to your driving record, the amount of miles you travel in a year, and other factors, your real cost may be more or lower than this.

## **About the Nissan Sentra**

Nissan introduced the Sentra in 1982 and it is now in its seventh generation. The small vehicle is well-known for its great fuel economy and ample luggage space. Nissan introduced turbocharged engines to the Sentra model for the 2017 model year.

Insurance companies base the cost of insurance on a [Nissan Sentra's](<https://www.jdpower.com/cars/2021/nissan/sentra>) safety rating, theft rate, replacement cost, and general susceptibility to damage. Insurance costs vary according to your driving history and geographic area. If you're contemplating a Nissan Sentra, obtain quotes from various insurance providers to ensure you're paying the lowest rate available. Conduct research to determine whether you are eligible for insurance savings.

## **How safe is the Nissan Sentra?**

The [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2020/NISSAN/SENTRA/4%252520DR/FWD>) awarded the 2017 Nissan Sentra a 4-Star Overall Safety Rating (NHTSA). The NHTSA awarded the side impact test five stars, the best possible grade. Additionally, the government body awarded four stars to the frontal collision and rollover crash tests. The Nissan Sentra was designated a Top Safety Pick by the [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS)](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/nissan/sentra-4-door-sedan/2021>). The IIHS gave an excellent grade to all crash tests it conducted, the best attainable rating.
