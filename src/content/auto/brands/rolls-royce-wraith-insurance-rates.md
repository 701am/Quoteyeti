The Wraith is a high-performance coupe that offers a luxurious ride — and a hefty insurance premium to match. [Rolls-Royce Wraith](<https://www.rolls-roycemotorcars.com/en_US/showroom/wraith.html>) insurance cost should around $1,083 per month or $12,996 per year. This is more expensive than insuring a luxury sports vehicle such as the [Aston Martin Rapide](<https://www.astonmartin.com/en/models/rapide-amr>), which costs $7,512 per year, or the [Ferrari 488 GTB](<https://www.ferrari.com/en-EN/auto/ferrari-488-gtb>), which costs $9,396 per year.

## **How do I compare insurance for the Rolls-Royce Wraith?**

The Wraith is a high-performance luxury coupe with a high starting price. This implies you may want additional protection in the form of a luxury insurance coverage.

Factors that contribute to its high premium include the following:

  * **Body type.** The Wraith is a premium coupe with a long wheelbase and a wide body – characteristics that very certainly contribute to its high insurance costs.
  * **MSRP.** This car's base pricing is comparable to that of high-end sports vehicles, starting at $300,000. This expense increases the cost of repairing or replacing the vehicle following an accident.
  * **Fuel**. Due to the car's strong engine, which is the most powerful in the Rolls-Royce automobile series to date, it achieves low fuel economy of 12/19 mpg.
  * **Maintenance**. While maintenance at a Rolls-Royce dealership is not inexpensive, the automobile comes with a comprehensive guarantee that covers service and maintenance.
  * **Theft.** Rolls-Royce is not recognized for having a high theft rate, and the Wraith is no exception.
  * **Coverage**. Consider purchasing luxury car insurance for this vehicle, which includes agreed value coverage and greater policy limits. Additionally, uninsured/underinsured motorist, umbrella, and gap insurance can cover gaps left by potentially costly accident claims on this automobile.
  * **Insurer options**. While many regular insurers provide coverage, a luxury insurance firm may be advantageous, albeit with fewer provider options.
  * **Warranty**. Rolls-Royce provides a four-year, unlimited mileage guarantee that covers service, maintenance, repairs, and roadside assistance in the territory in which the vehicle is registered.

## **Why is insurance so high for the Rolls-Royce Wraith?**

The Rolls-Royce Wraith is more expensive to insure due to its high base price and modified frame, which requires specialized components and servicing. Additionally, because this is a high-end luxury vehicle, specialized insurers may be the best alternative.

## **How reliable is the Rolls-Royce Wraith?**

While owners and experts alike lack information regarding the Wraith's safety and durability, the car comes with a lengthy warranty and no recalls to date.

  * **Safety**. This vehicle does not have any safety ratings from [IIHS](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings>) nor [NHTSA](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/road-safety>) reputable safety groups. This might be because more people are choosing less costly automobiles for daily use.
  * **Reliability**. There is little information available regarding the Wraith's long-term durability, but [Rolls-Royce](<https://www.jdpower.com/cars/rolls-royce/luxury>) includes a four-year maintenance and service warranty to ensure the car is in good shape from the outset.
  * **Recalls**. The Wraith did not have any recalls for any of its model years, implying a high level of manufacturing quality.



## **Bottom line**

The Rolls-Royce Wraith is a big luxury coupe with a powerful engine that completes the Rolls-Royce experience. While these variables may result in increased insurance premiums, you may compare different insurers to obtain the greatest value for your money.
