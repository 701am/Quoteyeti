## **How reliable is the Audi A4?**

According to U.S. News and [J.D. Power](<https://jdpower.com>), the 2017 A4 has a 9.6/10 rating. Furthermore, the NHTSA offers it a 5/5 overall star rating, with flawless ratings in all categories except frontal crash testing, and a 4/5 inside crash test. The 2017 model was named an IIHS Top Safety Pick+, with high marks in all categories.

While many sources agree that the A4 is a dependable car, it is on par with other vehicles in its class. The 2017 A4 and A4 Allroad models got a 3/5 dependability rating from U.S. News and J.D. Power, which is regarded about average. However, your experience may differ based on the model year you buy, the trim and extras you choose, and other factors.

There have been a number of recalls on Audi A4 vehicles due to various problems.

  * **2017.** Over 30,000 Audi A4, A4 Allroad, and A5 Sportback cars were recalled because the metal trim around the door speakers became loose, exposing sharp edges. Frontal and head airbags not deploying correctly, side marker lights not lighting, and seatbelt pre-tensioner problems are among the other recalls for this model.
  * **2016**. Almost 350,000 Audi cars, including the 2016 A4, were impacted by overheating problems with the engine's electronic cooling pump, increasing the danger of a fire.
  * **2015**. A handful of 2015 Audi A4 vehicles were recalled after complaints of the electrical connection on the auxiliary heater corroding and posing a fire danger. Coolant pumps overheating and front airbags not deploying are two more recalls for this model year.
  * **2013-2014**. Many of the 2015 A4 recalls also apply to the 2013 and 2014 models. This includes overheated auxiliary heater wiring, coolant pump problems, and front airbags that do not activate correctly.



The majority of contemporary Audi A4 vehicles have relatively few common issues. The 2009 model has received the most complaints overall, but the 2011 model is regarded as the worst owing to high maintenance costs and reduced mileage. Excessive oil consumption, faulty brakes, and clutch shaking are all common problems with the 2011 model.
