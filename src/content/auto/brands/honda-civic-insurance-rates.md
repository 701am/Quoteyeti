## **Honda Civic Servicing**

The Honda Civic is a low-cost daily driver that has been built to be dependable and low-maintenance in nature. Maintaining a regular service schedule, even if it will not require as much maintenance as a sports car, is always a good idea. Maintenance that is performed on a regular basis, such as oil changes, air filter replacements, and component checks, may assist to extend the life of your vehicle and prevent it from breaking down unexpectedly. 

What you may expect in terms of maintenance is as follows:

  * Honda does not recommend a set oil replacement schedule for the Civic. Because contemporary cars are equipped with the Maintenance Minder System, which warns you when oil and other components need to be replaced, this is the case.
  * The average cost of an oil change for a Civic is $75 to $125, but your exact cost will depend on your mileage, location, and other factors.
  * For more information on the Honda Civic maintenance schedule, contact your local Honda dealer or refer to your owner's manual. 



## **Is an extended warranty for the Honda Civic worth it?**

Limited Vehicle Warranty (three years/36,000 miles) and Limited Powertrain Warranty (five years/60,000 miles) are included as standard equipment on the Honda Civic. In addition, genuine Honda accessories are covered under the warranty for three years or 36,000 miles, whichever comes first, if they are installed at the time of purchase.

Additionally, the Civic is covered by a corrosion warranty that lasts for five years and unlimited miles, as well as 24-hour roadside assistance.

Car Service Contracts (VSCs) are offered by Honda instead of extended warranties, and they cover components and powertrains while also providing extra benefits such as roadside assistance, OEM part replacement, and rental vehicle allowances.

Despite the fact that the Civic is quite trustworthy, extended service plans can provide additional peace of mind and security after the limited warranty period has expired.
