## **About the Mitsubishi Outlander**

[Mitsubishi Outlander](<https://www.mitsubishicars.com/outlander/2022>) debuted in 2001 and is now in its third generation. Between 2001 and late 2016, approximately 1.5 million units were sold globally. The small SUV is renowned for its quiet interior, premium interior, and third-row seating, which is not available on many compact SUVs. There is also a hybrid model available.

## **How safe is the Mitsubishi Outlander?**

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration ([NHTSA](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2021/MITSUBISHI/OUTLANDER%2520SPORT/SUV/FWD>)) bestowed a 4-Star Overall Safety Rating to the Mitsubishi Outlander. The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration awarded four stars out of five for the frontal collision and rollover tests. The government body granted the side crash test a five-star grade. Mitsubishi Outlander was rated a Top Safety Pick+ vehicle by the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety ([IIHS](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/mitsubishi/outlander-4-door-suv/2020>)). The Mitsubishi Outlander received excellent ratings, the best possible, in all IIHS crash testing.

The Mitsubishi Outlander's newest model is equipped with side impact beams, seat-mounted side airbags, and electronic stability control.
