## **How safe is the Nissan Titan?**

The [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS)](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/nissan/titan-crew-cab-pickup/2021>) evaluated the Nissan Titan's crashworthiness. The small overlap front crash test received a marginal rating, which is the industry standard. All other IIHS crash tests earned an [excellent rating](<https://cars.usnews.com/cars-trucks/nissan/titan/reliability>), the best attainable.

Lane departure warning, blind spot monitoring, and electronic stability control are all standard on the Nissan Titan.

Nissan introduced the Titan in 2004 and it is now in its second generation. The full-size pickup truck is renowned for its roomy seats and pleasant ride. The most recent model is offered in regular or high duty configurations.
