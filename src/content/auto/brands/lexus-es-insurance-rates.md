## **How safe is the Lexus ES?**

Lexus ES 2017 has been rated a Top Safety Pick Car by the Highway Safety Institute ([IIHS](<https://www.iihs.org/>)). In all crash testing, the IIHS issued excellent ratings, the best possible grade. The Lexus ES got a 5-star overall safety rating from the National Administration for Traffic Safety ([NHTSA](<http://nhtsa.gov>)). The premium mid-size automobile got the highest rate of five Stars for the NHTSA side crash test. For the frontal crash test and rollover crash test, the government organization has given the Lexus ES four stars.  


The newest Lexus ES model has airbags, side effect airbags and antilock brakes.  
According to NHTSA, there were 21 documented thefts of the Lexus ES in 2013.
