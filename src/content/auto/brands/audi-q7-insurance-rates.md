## **How reliable is the Audi Q7?**

According to U.S. News and J.D. Power, the Q7's dependability is slightly below average. The 2018 model has a 2.5/5 star rating, indicating that there is still space for development. Many owners appear to be pleased with their purchase, although some have expressed concern that it may not be as dependable as other vehicles in its class.

The Q7 was Audi's first SUV, and it was introduced in 2007. There are two generations: one from 2007 to 2015, and one from 2016 forward. Since its release, there have been several recalls.

  * **2017:** The 2017 Audi Q7 has been subjected to a number of vehicle recalls, including those concerning gasoline leaks, airbags that did not deploy correctly, power steering assist failures, side marker lights that did not illuminate, and front airbags that deployed with excessive force.
  * **2014-2016:** The recall also affected several 2014, 2015, and 2016 vehicles owing to gasoline leaks from the pump location. 
  * **2013:** The gasoline leak recall also affected certain 2013 vehicles. Furthermore, certain vehicles had power brake assist failures.
  * **2010-2012:** A number of models from 2010, 2011, and 2012 were recalled due to gasoline leaks that might produce fuel smells and fire concerns.



Aside from the recalls, the Audi Q7 does not have many frequent issues. Some owners of 2015 and 2017 models have reported maintenance difficulties, although there have been no severe recurrent issues that stand out. However, 2007 is regarded as the worst model year in terms of difficulties, high maintenance costs, and problems at lesser mileage.

## **Audi Q7 maintenance**

The Q7 is an excellent family vehicle that is ideal for road trips, cargo carrying, and practically any other frequent use. Its mileage isn't fantastic, so there may be better choices if you want to use it as a daily vehicle. Regardless of how you want to use your Q7, it is critical to keep up with maintenance to ensure that it runs smoothly and without problems. Here's what to expect for oil changes, mileage, and other services.

  * Audi suggests changing the oil every 10,000 miles or 12 months, whichever comes first. An oil change can cost between $100 and $200, depending on where you take your vehicle for repair, among other things.
  * According to the 2017 Audi maintenance plan, your Q7 should be taken in for minor repair at 10,000 miles and then every 20,000 miles thereafter. Standard maintenance should be performed after the first 20,000 miles and then every 20,000 miles after that.
  * The 2018 Audi Q7 gets 21 miles per gallon on average, with 19 in the city and 25 on the interstate.
  * The 2017 Audi Q7 is backed by a four-year/50,000-mile guarantee as well as four years of 24-hour roadside support.



## **Is an extended warranty worth it for the Audi Q7?**

All-new Audi cars come standard with a four-year, 50,000-mile warranty. Owners can, however, add an additional warranty called the Audi Certified Plus Limited guarantee. This extends your Q7's warranty for another two years or up to 50,000 miles after your original warranty expires. The basic warranty provides excellent coverage, but upgrading to the extended warranty can bring peace of mind and even enhance the resale value of your Q7.
