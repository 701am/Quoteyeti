## **Why is insurance so high for the Maserati Ghibli?**

Because it is a luxury car that requires original Maserati components and a specialized dealership, the Maserati Ghibli is more expensive to insure. Furthermore, specialist foreign automobiles need a more sophisticated service procedure, which adds to the already expensive repair expenses.

## **How reliable is the Maserati Ghibli?**

While the Maserati has a beautiful appearance and a romantic Italian aura, its dependability and model recall track record is less than outstanding.

  * **Safety**. The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety rates the Ghibli as "good" for crashworthiness, which assesses its ability to withstand a collision. Its crash prevention and mitigation capabilities were classified as "basic."
  * **Reliability**. There are no major complaints about the Ghibli's dependability, but it isn't a blazing diamond of perfection either. Regular maintenance, like with any premium automobile with custom engineering, is critical to ensure dependable operation.
  * **Recalls**. Maserati recalled some Ghiblis in 2018 because of two issues: an unfinished weld on the front subframe and a possibly leaking fuel delivery line in the engine compartment. Maserati provided free servicing and repair to affected vehicles because these flaws represent a significant risk to the driver.
