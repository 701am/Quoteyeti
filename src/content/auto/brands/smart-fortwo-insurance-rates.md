## **About the Smart Fortwo**

Since 1998, the [Smart Fortwo](<https://www.smartusa.com/>) has been in production and is presently in its third generation. The subcompact automobile is renowned for its superior fuel efficiency, tiny size, and mobility. By early 2015, the business had manufactured over 1.7 million Fortwo units globally.

Insurance companies base the Smart Fortwo insurance cost on safety rating, theft rate, replacement cost, and general susceptibility to damage. ## **How safe is the Smart Fortwo?**

The [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2017/SMART/FORTWO>) awarded the 2017 Smart Fortwo a 4-Star Overall Safety Rating (NHTSA). The NHTSA awarded the side crash test five stars, the best attainable grade. Additionally, the government agency gave the vehicle a four-star safety grade in the frontal collision and rollover crash tests. The [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/smart/fortwo-2-door-hatchback/2019>) evaluated just the moderate overlap front collision test and the side impact crash test, both of which got the best safety rating available.

A proximity warning system, rear parking aid, and crosswind assistance are all included on the [Smart Fortwo](<https://www.jdpower.com/cars/smart>) model.
