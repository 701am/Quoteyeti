## What factors affect the cost of Dodge Charger automobile insurance?

Your automobile insurance premiums are significantly influenced by your propensity of filing claims and the cost of fully resolving such claims. Due to the fact that vehicle performance, theft rates, safety features, and other attributes can vary substantially between brands and models, insurance companies base their premiums on the vehicle you select.

  * **Body type**. This four-door sedan is engineered for performance, but it's also equipped with safety measures, making it an excellent second car for families. This has the potential to reduce insurance costs.
  * **Type of car**. The Dodge Charger, which is classified as a muscle car, has a lot of power available. It may be more expensive to insure because to the risk of it being driven at high speeds.
  * **Theft**. In comparison to other vehicles, the Charger has one of the highest theft rates, which might result in an increase in insurance costs.
  * **Repairs**. Dodge Charger damage is quite affordable to fix, costing an average of $625 each year. While this cost may be higher on more expensive models, the low cost of repairs may result in a savings on insurance.
  * **Safety ratings**. The Dodge Charger receives outstanding safety ratings in virtually every category, which may assist to reduce the vehicle's insurance costs.
  * **Fuel type**. The Charger is gasoline-powered, which is frequently less expensive to insure than diesel or hybrid vehicles.
  * **Trim & packages**. If you opt to enhance your automobile in a way that dramatically increases its power or value, your insurance costs may increase significantly. On the other hand, features that improve safety or visibility may have the exact opposite effect.

## Dodge Charger reliability and safety ratings

US News and JD Power have rated the 2018 Dodge Charger's overall safety at 8.9 out of 10. This grade is backed up by nearly perfect IIHS ratings in virtually every category and a 5-star safety rating from the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration.

On the issue of reliability, US News and [J.D. Power](<https://jdpower.com>) award the Charger two out of five stars, which is lower than the average for comparable vehicles in its class. Prior years appear to have garnered slightly higher scores, although not significantly so.

Despite the Charger's great performance, it is presently the only four-door muscle car in production. ## Is an extended warranty for the Dodge Charger worth it?

The Charger comes standard with a three-year/36,000-mile limited warranty and a five-year/60,000-mile powertrain warranty. Corrosion protection and roadside assistance are also included in the purchase price of new automobiles for a five-year term.

Dodge does not provide an extended warranty for the Charger, but its parent company, Mopar, does. Terms of up to eight years and 125,000 miles are available, and they include parts, rental allowances, and other benefits.

These protection options may provide additional peace of mind when the first warranty period expires, as the Charger is rated below average for reliability.
