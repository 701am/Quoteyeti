## **About the Nissan Rogue**

The [Nissan Rogue](<https://www.nissanusa.com/vehicles/crossovers-suvs/rogue.html>) has been on the market since 2008 and is presently in its second generation of production. The small SUV is renowned for its high-quality interior, excellent fuel efficiency, and ample load space. ## **How safe is the Nissan Rogue?**

The 2017 Nissan Rogue got a 4-Star Overall Safety Rating from the [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2021/NISSAN/ROGUE/SUV/AWD>), which is the highest rating available (NHTSA). The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration awarded the side impact test five stars, the best possible grade. In addition, the frontal crash test and the rollover crash test received four stars from the government agency. The Nissan Rogue has been designated as a Top Safety Pick+ vehicle by the [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS)](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/nissan/rogue-4-door-suv/2021>). The IIHS gave the automobile strong scores, including the top possible grade, in all of the crash tests that were conducted on it.

According to the [National Insurance Crime Bureau](<https://www.nicb.org/news/news-releases/nicbs-hot-wheels-americas-10-most-stolen-vehicles-2018-data>), from 2009 to 2011, the Nissan Rogue was the ninth most stolen SUV in the United States, ranking ninth overall. During this time period, there were over 540 thefts that were reported.
