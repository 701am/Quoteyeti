## **Why is insurance so expensive for the Land Rover Discovery?**

Insurance costs extra for the Land Rover Discovery, as it is a premium vehicle built for off-roading, which increases the chance of accidents and necessitates more repairs. 

A luxury SUV, with a premium interior and high-quality components, is somewhat of a rare luxury.

## **About the Land Rover Discovery**

The Land Rover Discovery has been on the market since 1989, and the third-generation model is presently in production. The vehicle was first presented in the U.S. in 1994. In the midsize luxury SUV, a strong engine, roomy interior, and great off-road capability are common characteristics. It was recently revealed that roughly 1.2 million Land Rover Discovery vehicles have been sold since the automobile initially went on sale at the beginning of 2017. In 2016, sales in the U.S. totaled 50,000.
