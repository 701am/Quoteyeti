## **About the Jeep Grand Cherokee**

The Jeep Grand Cherokee has been on the market since 1993 and is now in its fourth generation. The car was intended to be a bigger replacement for the Jeep Cherokee. The Jeep Grand Cherokee is always built on a unibody chassis, unlike many other SUVs that are built on a body on frame. This midsize SUV is well-known for its excellent off-road skills, powerful engine lineup, and luxury cabin. In the United States, there were approximately 195,000 sales in 2015.

## **How safe is the Jeep Grand Cherokee?**

The newest Grand Cherokee model has been assessed for safety ratings by the [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA)](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2021/JEEP/GRAND%252520CHEROKEE/SUV/4WD>) and the [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS)](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/jeep/grand-cherokee-4-door-suv/2021>). The Grand Cherokee received a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating from the NHTSA. The NHTSA gave the automobile four out of five stars in the rollover crash test. The small overlap front collision test was judged marginal by the IIHS. The IIHS gave the best possible grade to the other crash tests.

According to the [National Insurance Crime Bureau](<https://www.nicb.org/HotWheels2019>), approximately 9,700 Jeep Cherokee/Grand Cherokees were stolen in 2013, ranking it as the eighth most stolen vehicle that year.
