The average monthly insurance premium for a [Lexus LS](<https://www.lexus.com/models/LS>) is $155 — or $1,860 per year. With a starting price of $72,520, the yearly insurance cost–to–base vehicle price ratio is a manageable 2.6 percent.

## **About the Lexus LS**

Lexus introduced the LS in 1989 and it will reach its fifth generation in 2018. Lexus' flagship model is the LS. The full-size luxury automobile is renowned for its affordable pricing in comparison to other models in its class, its smooth ride, and its excellent dependability rating. For 17 years, the Lexus LS was ranked top in the [J.D. Power](<http://jdpower.com>) & Associates' US Vehicle Dependability Survey.

  * Anti-lock brakes

## **How safe is the Lexus LS?**

As is the case with a large number of premium automobiles on the market, the 2017 Lexus LS has not been assessed for safety ratings by the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety or the [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<http://nhtsa.gov>).

The Lexus LS fifth generation has enhanced safety technologies. The new automobile has an automated steering wheel to prevent colliding with other vehicles or pedestrians, blind spot detection to identify oncoming traffic, and the world's biggest full-color head-up display to display impending pedestrians.

According to the NHTSA, the Lexus LS was stolen only three times in 2013.
