## **How Much Does Chevy Corvette Car Insurance Cost?**

The average premium for Chevrolet Corvette car insurance for the model year 2004 is $238 per month, or $2,856 per year. A year's worth of insurance costs an average of $2,856, resulting in an annual insurance cost–to–base car price ratio of 5.2 percent for the vast majority of automobiles now on the market.

It is possible that your actual cost will be more or lower than this, depending on your driving record, the number of miles you travel in a year, and a variety of other factors.

## **Chevrolet Corvette Information**

Corvettes were introduced in 1953 and are now in their seventh generation. They are sold globally. The high-end sports car is widely sought after because of its powerful engine and agile handling.

[Chevrolet Corvette](<https://www.chevrolet.com/>) was selected 2014 North American Car of the Year and is still manufactured. The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety and the [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<https://nhtsa.com>) have yet to complete their evaluations of the 2021 model year's safety ratings.

## **How is it that the Chevrolet Corvette is so outrageously costly to insure?**

Because the Chevrolet Corvette is a sports car with a powerful engine designed for high speeds, it is more expensive to insure than other automobiles. Insurance companies view this as a high-risk condition and raise premiums accordingly.

Additionally, there is no defined Corvette safety rating by which insurance prices may be calculated.
