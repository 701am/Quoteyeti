## **How safe is the Toyota Tacoma?**

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration awarded the 2017 [Toyota Tacoma](<https://www.toyota.com/tacoma/>) a 4-Star Overall Safety Rating ([NHTSA](<https://nhtsa.com>)). The NHTSA awarded the side crash test five stars, the best attainable grade. The government body also gave the frontal crash test and the rollover crash test four stars.

The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS) only evaluated two crash tests: the moderate overlap front crash test and the side-impact crash test, both of which got the highest safety ratings available.

The [Toyota](<https://www.toyota.com/>) Tacoma model is equipped with lane departure warning, braking assist, and blind-spot monitoring as standard equipment.
