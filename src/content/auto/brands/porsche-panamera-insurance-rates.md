A Porsche Panamera's average insurance cost is $105 per month, or $1,260 per year. Your exact cost may vary based on your driving record, the number of miles you travel in a year, and other variables. 

## **About the Porsche Panamera**

The [Porsche Panamera](<https://www.porsche.com/international/models/panamera/>) has been on the market since 2010, and it is now in its second generation. The super luxury vehicle is well-known for its precise handling, powerful turbocharged engine choices, and roomy seats. Porsche introduced the Panamera Hybrid in 2013, and it is still available today.

## **Porsche Panamera money-saving safety features**

  * Anti-lock brakes
  * Stability control
  * Airbags
  * Seatbelt pretensioners
  * Anti theft system



### **How safe is the Porsche Panamera?**

The [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety](<http://iihs.org>) and the [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<http://nhtsa.gov>) have not rated the 2017 Porsche Panamera for safety.

Adaptive cruise control, blind spot monitoring, and a collision mitigation system are standard on the Porsche Panamera.

According to the [National Insurance Crime Bureau](<https://www.nicb.org/>), the Porsche Panamera was the fourth most stolen sports vehicle in America from 2009 to 2012. Approximately 103 thefts occurred during this time period.
