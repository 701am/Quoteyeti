The average monthly insurance premium for a [Lexus RC](<https://www.lexus.com/models/RC>) is $220 — or $2,640 per year. Your exact cost will vary according to your driving record, the number of miles you travel each year, and other factors.

## **Why is insurance so expensive for the Lexus RC?**

The Lexus RC is more expensive to insure due to its high-end status, which necessitates premium parts and a more involved maintenance process. Additionally, some owners may prefer a higher-quality car shop, which results in higher insurance rates.

## **About the Lexus RC**

The Lexus RC debuted in 2015. The RC designation stands for Radical Coupe. The compact luxury automobile is renowned for its powerful engine options, sumptuous interior, and superior safety and reliability ratings. In 2016, approximately 11,000 sales occurred in the United States.

## **Lexus RC money-saving safety features**

If your Lexus RC is equipped with certain safety features, you may be able to save money on car insurance.

  * Anti-lock brakes
  * Stability control
  * Airbags
  * Seatbelt pretensioners
  * Anti theft system



## **How safe is the Lexus RC?**

The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety designated the 2017 Lexus RC a Top Safety Pick+ vehicle ([IIHS](<http://iihs.org>)). The IIHS gave all crash tests a good rating, the best attainable.  


The Lexus RC's most recent model is equipped with blind spot monitoring, side impact airbags, and antilock brakes.
