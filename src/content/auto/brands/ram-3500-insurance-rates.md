The average monthly premium for a Ram 3500 insurance cost is $151 — or $1,812 per year. Your exact cost will vary according to your driving record, the number of miles you travel each year, and other factors.

## **About the Ram 3500**

Since 1994, the [Ram 3500](<https://www.ramtrucks.com/ram-3500.html>) has been in production and is now in its fourth generation. The full-size pickup truck is renowned for its superior riding comfort, fuel efficiency, and towing capacity of over 31,000 pounds. The heavy duty truck is available in a range of engine configurations, body designs, and bed lengths.

## **Ram 3500 money-saving safety features**

If your Ram 3500 is equipped with these safety features, you may be able to save money on your Ram 3500 insurance cost.

  * Anti-lock brakes
  * Stability control
  * Airbags
  * Seatbelt pretensioners
  * Anti theft system



## **How safe is the Ram 3500?**

The [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2020/RAM/3500>) and the [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/class-summary/large-pickups>) have not rated the 2017 Ram 3500 for safety.

A rear view camera, blind spot monitoring, and stability control are standard on the [Ram 3500](<https://www.jdpower.com/cars/ram-truck>) model.
