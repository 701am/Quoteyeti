## **About the Lexus NX**

Lexus introduced the NX in 2015. The NX suffix denotes Nimble Crossover. The compact luxury SUV is famous for its affordability, luxurious interior, spacious seating, and comfortable ride. In 2016, the United States saw around 54,000 sales.

## **How safe is the Lexus NX?**

The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety designated the 2017 Lexus NX a Top Safety Pick+ vehicle ([IIHS](<http://iihs.org>)). The IIHS gave all crash tests a good rating, the best attainable. The Lexus NX has a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating from the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration ([NHTSA](<http://nhtsa.gov>)). The NHTSA rated the side impact test five stars, the best possible grade. Additionally, the organization awarded four stars for the frontal collision and rollover tests.

The Lexus NX's most recent model is equipped with blind spot monitoring, side impact airbags, and antilock brakes.
