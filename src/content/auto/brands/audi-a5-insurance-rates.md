## **What factors affect car insurance rates for Audi A5?**

Insurance companies determine your rates based on your likelihood of being involved in an accident, the cost of repairs, the likelihood of your car being stolen, and other factors. As a result, the car you drive has a significant impact on the cost of auto insurance. Here are a few things that influence Audi A5 insurance prices.

  * **Body type.** The Audi A5 is a two-door coupe. This will most likely cost more to insure than a sedan but less than a sports vehicle.
  * **Type of car.** The Audi A5 Coupe is classified as a premium compact vehicle. Luxury automobiles are typically more expensive to insure owing to the expense of maintenance, the higher likelihood of theft, and other factors.
  * **Theft.** Because sedans are the most commonly stolen kind of car, opting for a coupe may save you money on auto insurance.
  * **Repairs.** Audi car parts and service providers are plentiful, which may assist to reduce the cost of auto insurance. However, because it is a premium car, fixing interior or body damage may be costly.
  * **Safety ratings.** The A5 is equipped with a variety of safety measures that can assist in reducing harm to occupants and the car itself in the event of an accident. This might assist to lower the cost of car insurance.

## **How reliable is the Audi A5?**

According to [U.S. News](<https://cars.usnews.com/cars-trucks/audi/a5>) and [J.D. Power](<https://www.jdpower.com/cars/2021/audi/a5>), the 2018 Audi A5 coupe got a 4/5 star dependability rating, which is higher than the majority of vehicles in its class. The previous year's ratings were somewhat lower, but still above average in its class. Furthermore, a driver study of over 30,000 automobile owners yielded a dependability score of 94 percent.

The Audi A5 has been on the market since 2007, giving Audi plenty of time to iron out any major faults with the car. As a result, there have been several recalls over the years:

  * **2018:** A small number of 2018 vehicles were recalled because the metal trim on the front and rear doors became loose, exposing sharp edges.
  * **2017:** Some A5 Coupe and A5 Cabriolet cars were recalled owing to an overheated coolant pump that may cause a fire.
  * **2016:** A handful of A5 Coupe and Cabriolet models were recalled as a result of the coolant pump problem, as well as electrical system wiring concerns that might cause heater wires to overheat or melt.
  * **2013-2015:** The coolant pump and wiring faults identified on the 2016 A5 Coupe and Cabriolet variants also affected some 2013-2015 A5 Coupe and Cabriolet vehicles.



The 2017-2018 Audi A5 models have practically no common issues. The majority of this vehicle's owners are quite pleased with their purchase and have no serious complaints. Some owners report slight carbon buildup, which may result in lower power and fuel efficiency, although this is likely to be resolved at a normal service visit.

## **Audi A5 maintenance**

The Audi A5 is a premium sports car intended for both comfort and pleasure. It doesn't take much care, but it's vital to keep up with oil changes and manufacturer recommendations to guarantee it runs smoothly. Here's what to anticipate from an Audi A5 in terms of oil changes, mileage, and maintenance.

  * Audi suggests changing the oil every 10,000 miles or 12 months, whichever comes first. Oil changes may cost anywhere from $50 to $150, depending on where you take it for repair.
  * According to the 2017 Audi maintenance plan, your A5 should be serviced at 10,000 miles and then every 20,000 miles after that. Standard maintenance should be performed after the first 20,000 miles and then every 20,000 miles after that.
  * The 2017 Audi A5 Coupe Quattro gets 26 miles per gallon on average, with 23 in the city and 32 on the interstate.
  * The 2017 Audi A5 Cabriolet Quattro gets 24 miles per gallon on the interstate and 21 in the city.
  * Audi urges owners to take their A5 to a dealer for repair, but it will not void your warranty if you do so.
  * The 2017 Audi A5 comes with a four-year/50,000-mile warranty and four years of 24/7 roadside assistance.
