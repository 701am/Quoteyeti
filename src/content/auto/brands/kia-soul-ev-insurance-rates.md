Although [Kia](<https://www.kia.com/us/en>) are inexpensive to purchase, they are very expensive to insure. According to the Insurance Research Council, the average price for a Kia Soul EV insurance cost is $174 per month ($2,088 per year). This amounts to $800 higher than the national average. The insurance cost–to–base automobile price ratio is 6.5%, higher than the industry average of 4%. 

## **About the Kia Soul EV**

Kia has offered an all-electric variant of the Soul since 2015, and the new model is scheduled to be unveiled soon. Kia's Soul EV is a highly small electric car with a smooth and quiet ride. The EPA rated the range of the Kia Soul EV at 93 miles.
