## **About the Lexus RC F**

Since 2014, the Lexus RC F has been available. The Lexus RC F is a performance-oriented version of the Lexus RC. The small luxury automobile is distinguished by a powerful V8 engine, an eight-speed automatic gearbox, and an upmarket interior.

## **How safe is the Lexus RC F?**

The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety designated the 2017 Lexus RC F a Top Safety Pick+ vehicle ([IIHS](<http://iihs.org>)). The IIHS gave all crash tests a good rating, the best attainable.

The Lexus RC F's newest model is equipped with rear cross traffic warning, a pre-collision system, and blind spot monitoring.
