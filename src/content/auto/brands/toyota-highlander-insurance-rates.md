## **About the Toyota Highlander**

Toyota introduced the [Highlander](<https://www.toyota.com/highlander/>) in 2001 and it is now in its third generation. The midsize SUV is renowned for its fuel efficiency and ride comfort. Toyota sold the most Highlanders in a single year in 2016, with over 191,000 sold in the United States.

## **How safe is the Toyota Highlander?**

The [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2021/TOYOTA/HIGHLANDER/SUV/AWD>) awarded the 2017 Toyota Highlander a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating (NHTSA). The NHTSA awarded the side crash test five stars, the best attainable grade. Additionally, the government agency gave the vehicle a four-star safety grade in the frontal collision and rollover crash tests. Toyota Highlander was rated a Top Safety Pick+ vehicle by the [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS)](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/toyota/highlander-4-door-suv/2021>). All IIHS crash testing resulted in an excellent [safety rating](<https://www.jdpower.com/cars/expert-reviews/2021-toyota-highlander-review>), the best attainable.

Forward collision warning, lane departure assist, and driver knee airbags are standard on the Toyota Highlander.
