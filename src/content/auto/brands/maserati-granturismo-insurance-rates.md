The [GranTurismo](<https://www.maseratiusa.com/us/en/models/granturismo>) has one of the highest MSRPs of any Maserati model, at $134,775 with an estimated average monthly insurance cost of $535. Its enticing race-car engine and classic design imply an exciting ride, but protecting this automobile is an investment in and of itself.

## **How do I compare insurance for the Maserati GranTurismo?**

GranTurismo, or GT for short, is an Italian word for automobiles that travel long distances, or "Grand Tours," in luxury and style. Some things to consider while shopping for insurance are:

  * **Body type**. The GranTurismo is a large coupe that is also available as a convertible. While coupes are typically less costly to insure, this is a premium speedster, so it won't make much of a difference. Convertible insurance is typically more expensive since they are less secure.
  * **Fuel**. It's a gasoline-powered coupe with a 13-mpg city rating and a 20-mpg highway rating, which is poor in comparison to comparable coupes.
  * **Maintenance**. Maserati, like any other luxury vehicle store, suggests servicing your GranTurismo with authentic Maserati Parts at one of its shops. Because of specialization, parts and labor are likely to be more expensive.
  * **Coverage**. Consider purchasing luxury vehicle insurance for your GranTurismo, as you will most likely want higher maximums to cover the car's worth.
  * **Warranty**. Maserati's New Car Limited Warranty covers the vehicle for four years or 50,000 miles, whichever comes first. For the first few years, the Maserati warranty includes roadside assistance, so there is no need to purchase it separately via your insurance.



### **Does the Maserati GranTurismo qualify for discounts?**

The Maserati GranTurismo is quick and beautiful, but the firm maintains that one of its major objectives is safety. Some safety measures that may qualify you for an insurance discount are as follows:

  * Stability control (MSP)
  * Rear-view camera
  * ABS with electronic brakeforce distribution
  * Anti Slip regulation
  * Anti Lock brakes
  * Adaptive light control
  * Daytime running lights with LED technology
  * Front-impact airbags
  * Side-impact airbags
  * Tire pressure monitoring system
  * Pretensioners
  * Security system



## **Why is insurance so high for the Maserati GranTurismo?**

The Maserati GranTurismo is more expensive to insure since it has a quick racing car-like engine, making it a high-risk vehicle to cover. This speedster also needs more frequent maintenance checks to guarantee that its unique design remains in tune.

### **How reliable is the Maserati GranTurismo?**

Maserati creates visually appealing vehicles that sometimes fall short in terms of durability.

  * **Safety**. The crashworthiness of the GranTurismo has yet to be determined by the [IIHS](<http://iihs.org>) and the [NHTSA](<http://nhtsa.gov>). However, another Maserati model, the Ghibli, had a “good” rating, and the GT is fitted with safety equipment.
  * **Reliability**. In general, you can rely on the GT to get you from point A to point B. However, owing to its unique engineering and specialist design, it is critical to have regular maintenance inspections to guarantee everything is in working order.
  * **Recalls**. Maserati has not issued a recall for a GT since 2012. When a problem is discovered, it provides free servicing and repair to impacted cars.



## **Bottom line**

Insurance for a Maserati GranTurismo can be expensive, but it's critical to choose a coverage with maximums that cover the vehicle's purchase price. Still, take the time to shop around for auto insurance quotes to ensure you're receiving the greatest bargain.
