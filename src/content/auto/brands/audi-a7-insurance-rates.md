## **How reliable is the Audi A7?**

Audi cars are usually regarded as mediocre in terms of dependability. While many of Audi's other sedans and coupes receive favorable reviews, the A7 is a cut above. The 2018 Audi A7 receives a 4/5 star rating from U.S. News and J.D. Power, which is higher than the majority of vehicles in its class. The 2017 model is much better, receiving 4.5 out of 5 stars. Other sources and internet evaluations offer the latest versions of the A7 high dependability ratings as well.

The Audi A7 is a very new automobile, having just been on the market since 2010. As a result, the majority of difficulties are linked with early generations of this car, when Audi was still ironing out the kinks. Here are a few examples of recent recalls:

  * **2017:** A small number of 2017 Audi A7s were recalled due to problems with the passenger, side, and window airbags not deploying correctly.
  * **2016:** The 2017 airbag recall also affected certain 2016 models.
  * **2015:** Some 2015 Audi A5 vehicles were also affected by the aforementioned airbag recall, however the majority of the issues involved the head curtain airbag.
  * **2012-2013:** A handful of Audi A7 models from 2012 to 2013 were recalled owing to gasoline leaks caused by a faulty fuel injection mechanism. Some vehicles were also recalled owing to a problem with the Passenger Occupant Detection System, which might prevent the passenger-side airbags from deploying in the event of a collision.



The Audi A7 receives practically no frequent criticism. Despite the fact that it is a recent vehicle, Audi did not take long to resolve any recurrent difficulties. On the 2012 model, there have been allegations of faulty steering, early tire wear, and screeching brakes, although these concerns appear to have been rectified over time.
