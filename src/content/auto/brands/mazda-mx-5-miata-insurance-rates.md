## **About the Mazda MX-5 Miata**

The [Mazda MX-5 Miata](<https://www.mazdausa.com/vehicles/2021-mx-5-miata>), which has been on the market since 1989, was meant to be a compact roadster. The sports vehicle is noted for its nimble handling, good fuel efficiency, folding top, and good fuel economy. 

The Mazda MX-5 Miata was named the best-selling two-seat sports vehicle by the Guinness Book of World Records in 2000. By the end of 2016, more than one million models had been made.
