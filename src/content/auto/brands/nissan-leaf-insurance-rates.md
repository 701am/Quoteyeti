## **How safe is the Nissan Leaf?**

The Nissan Leaf 2017 was assessed for safety ratings by the [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS)](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/nissan/leaf-4-door-hatchback/2021>). The IIHS classified the small front-crash overlap test as bad, as low as possible. All other crash tests have achieved the [best possible rating](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2020/NISSAN/LEAF%252520PLUS/5%252520HB/FWD>) and an excellent rating.

The Nissan Leaf model contains a lane departure warning, a rear view and an upgraded airbag system.
