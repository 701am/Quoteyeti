The car you drive, on the other hand, has a considerably bigger influence since performance, safety features, theft rates, and other aspects might differ depending on the brand and model. And, because carriers factor both your risk and the cost of filing claims when determining your premiums, the vehicle you choose might have a direct impact on your rates.

Here's how to do it:

  * **Body type**. The Accord is a four-door sedan that is best suited for families or casual use. This might be one of the reasons why coverage is so inexpensive.
  * **Type of car**. The Accord is a midsize car with a starting price of little more than $20,000. It should be less expensive to insure than a premium or high-performance car.
  * **Theft**. The Honda Accord is one of the most stolen automobiles in the United States, which may raise insurance costs.
  * **Repairs**. Honda Accord parts and repair should be easily available, resulting in cheaper insurance costs.
  * **Safety ratings**. The Accord boasts excellent safety ratings and features that may result in lower vehicle insurance rates.
  * **Fuel type**. The gas version of the Honda Accord should be less expensive to insure than the hybrid version, owing to decreased repair costs.
  * **Trim & packages**. The Honda Accord offers a variety of safety and driver aid features. Including them might assist to lower insurance costs.



## **How to compare insurance for the Honda Accord**

Because the Accord is a popular vehicle with high safety ratings, getting coverage should be simple. Knowing what to look for may make insurance buying easier and, if done correctly, can save you money.

  * **Price**. Coverage varies across carriers, making pricing comparison difficult. To truly compare your options, use the same driver and vehicle information when receiving estimates.
  * **Deductible.** Higher deductibles frequently result in cheaper rates, but smaller deductibles raise the cost. Choose an amount that is appropriate for your financial condition.
  * **Minimum coverage.** Each state has its own set of coverage criteria. Before making a commitment, ensure that any possible coverage satisfies your state's minimum requirements.
  * **Additional coverage**. Personal injury protection and medical expenses coverage are just two of the numerous enhancements you may make to your policy. When obtaining estimates from carriers, make sure to include any additional coverage choices.
  * **Extras**. Your coverage may contain features like roadside assistance and accident forgiveness. If not, find out how much additions will cost and if they are worthwhile.
  * **Restrictions**. To avoid gaps in coverage or refused claims, find out whether your insurance has any limits.
  * **Manufacturer offer or loyalty discount**. Inquire with your dealership to see if you are qualified for any manufacturer incentives or loyalty discounts, as you may be able to save money on vehicle insurance.
  * **Warranty**. To prevent paying for overlapping coverage, find out what is covered by your manufacturer's or extended warranty.

## **Honda Accord maintenance**

Because the Accord is built to deliver a comfortable and easy driving experience, it requires little maintenance to stay on the road. Routine service appointments to replace the oil and examine components, like with most cars, may help extend its life and ensure it drives smoothly.

  * Honda does not propose an oil change regimen for the Accord. In fact, all modern vehicles come included with the Maintenance Minder system, which alerts you when oil and other parts need to be updated.
  * An oil change for the Accord should cost between $75 and $125, depending on where you get it serviced. As a general rule, dealerships are more expensive than independent repair businesses.
  * To learn more about the Honda Accord maintenance schedule, contact your Honda dealer or reference your owner's handbook.



## **Is an extended warranty worth it for the Honda Accord?**

All new Honda cars have a 3-year/36,000-mile Limited Vehicle Warranty as well as a 5-year/60,000-mile Powertrain Limited Warranty. Genuine Honda accessories are also protected for three years or 36,000 miles if fitted at the time of purchase. The Accord also has a 5-year/Unlimited-mileage corrosion guarantee and 24-hour emergency roadside assistance.

Honda also provides a number of Vehicle Service Contracts, which are effectively extended warranties. They cover components, powertrains, and a variety of perks like rental car reimbursement, 24-hour concierge emergency services, and so on. While the Accord is usually regarded as a dependable vehicle, these service plans can safeguard your vehicle after the original warranty has expired.
