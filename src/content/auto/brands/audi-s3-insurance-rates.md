## **How to compare insurance for the Audi S3**

The S3 is a high-performance sedan built for commuting, errands, and general daily driving. Its sumptuous design includes a multitude of safety and driver assistance systems as standard, so you should have no problem obtaining coverage. However, looking for vehicle insurance coverage, like shopping for other forms of insurance, can be a difficult experience. 

Knowing exactly what you're searching for will help you compare your alternatives for the best [Audi S3](<https://www.audiusa.com/us/web/en/models/a3/s3/2022/overview.html>) auto insurance. The most effective method is to build a sample policy with all of the choices you want and then use that template to receive quotations from several providers. Here's what you should know.

  * **Price.** Price is an essential factor to consider, but it is not necessarily the most accurate. Even though policies appear to be comparable, coverage might differ between providers. The easiest method to compare rates is to acquire numerous quotes using your sample policy. This approach will allow you to compare comparable coverage with precision.
  * **Deductible.** The size of your deductible might have a direct impact on the cost of your insurance. A greater deductible may be more expensive when filing a claim, but it will most likely result in cheaper rates. A lower deductible, on the other hand, may cost less out of pocket after an incident, but it will lead to higher monthly rates.
  * **Minimum coverage.** The majority of states have mandated minimum coverage standards. Before acquiring insurance, be certain that your state's minimums are satisfied.
  * **Additional coverage.** If you need extra coverage, like personal injury protection or medical expenses, make sure to include it in your sample policy to receive a more accurate quotation.
  * **Extras.** Some insurance companies provide policy add-ons such as accident forgiveness or roadside assistance. If they aren't included for free, think about whether it's worth it to add them to your policy.
  * **Restrictions.** Some carriers may have coverage limits that may limit your coverage while driving or while not driving. Find out whether any of these apply to your insurance to avoid refused claims or coverage gaps.
  * **Manufacturer offer or loyalty discounts.** Check with your dealer to see if you are qualified for any special discounts or loyalty programs via insurance provider relationships.
  * **Warranty.** To prevent paying for overlapping coverage, find out what is covered by your manufacturer's or extended warranty.

## **How reliable is the Audi S3?**

Unfortunately, there isn't much information available concerning the S3's dependability. However, this car is built on Audi's entry-level sedan, the A3, about which there is a lot of information. The 2018 A3 earned a 2.5/5 star dependability rating from U.S. News and [J.D. Power](<https://jdpower.com>), which is somewhat lower than the average for comparable cars in its class. Owners on Audi enthusiast sites are pleased with their automobiles, although many complain about excessive oil use and small coolant leaks.

The 2017 S3 has only one major recall for airbags not deploying correctly in the case of a low-speed accident. However, the A3, on which the S3 is based, has a plethora of them.

  * **2018:** A limited number of A3 cars were recalled due to a problem in which the engine control unit incorrectly adjusted the air/fuel ratio at startup, potentially causing the engine to stall.
  * **2017:** The 2017 S3 airbag issue and the 2018 engine control unit problem also affected certain 2017 A3 vehicles.
  * **2016:** A handful of 2016 Audi A3 vehicles were recalled owing to faulty exterior lighting and a fuel system problem that might cause leaks and raise the danger of a fire.
  * **2015:** Fuel system and engine control unit problems plagued some 2015 versions as well.



Despite the fact that the Audi A3 is a recent model, owners have reported a number of problems. Rust on roof rails and door trims, electric window difficulties, failure of mass airflow sensors, loss of power, and other concerns are common. Repair prices may vary based on the severity of the problem, mileage at the time the problem is discovered, and other variables.

## **Audi S3 maintenance**

Audi automobiles are known for their comfort and convenience, and the S3 is no exception. While it has a strong engine and sport tune as standard, this car was designed to be driven regularly without requiring much maintenance. Keeping your automobile up to date on oil changes and other service appointments, on the other hand, may boost mileage and guarantee that it lasts as long as feasible.

Here's what to expect in terms of oil changes, mileage, and service appointments, among other things.

  * Audi suggests changing the oil every 10,000 miles or 12 months, whichever comes first. However, if you drive your car regularly, you may want to have it replaced more frequently.
  * Oil changes should cost between $75 and $150, depending on how long it has been since your previous oil change and where you choose to take your car for repair.
  * According to the 2017 Audi maintenance plan, your S3 should be serviced at 10,000 miles and then every 20,000 miles after that. Standard maintenance should be performed after the first 20,000 miles and then every 20,000 miles after that.
  * The 2018 Audi S3 gets 25 miles per gallon on average, with 22 in the city and 28 on the interstate.
  * The 2017 Audi S3 is covered by a four-year/50,000-mile guarantee as well as four years of 24-hour roadside support.
