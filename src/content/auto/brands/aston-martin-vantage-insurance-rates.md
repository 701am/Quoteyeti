Want to know Aston Martin Vantage Insurance Cost? To ensure the Aston Martin Vantage, you'll almost certainly need to seek specialized premium car insurance. Additionally, you'll want increased coverage due to the unique parts and maintenance requirements.

## **How do I compare Vantage insurance quotes?**

This vehicle is designed for both performance and elegance. When shopping for insurance, take into account the vehicle's body, engine, and warranty.

  * **Body type.** The Vantage is a luxury sports vehicle, which means its insurance costs will be higher.
  * **Fuel.** As you might imagine, this automobile gets poor gas mileage - approximately 14 to 16 miles per gallon combined city/highway driving. This is because the manufacturer prioritizes performance over economy.
  * **Maintenance.** European sports vehicles have a reputation for being extremely expensive to maintain. Basic maintenance and repairs are more expensive due to the specialized parts.
  * **Theft rates.** Despite its higher price tag, the Vantage is seldom stolen.
  * **Coverage.** This is an expensive car, from the sticker price through the expense of upkeep and repairs. Consider purchasing comprehensive coverage in addition to uninsured/underinsured motorist, loan/lease gap, and high-limit collision.
  * **Insurer options.** While some large insurers, such as [Geico](<https://www.geico.com/>), may decline to cover this premium sports automobile, many others would.
  * **Warranty.** The Vantage is covered by a three-year, unlimited mileage warranty.



### **Why is insurance so expensive for the Aston Martin Vantage?**

The [Aston Martin Vantage](<https://www.astonmartin.com/en-us/models/new-vantage>) is more expensive to insure because it requires specialist parts, which makes repairs more expensive. Additionally, a more powerful engine results in higher rates for this British-built vehicle.

### **Does the Aston Martin Vantage qualify for discounts?**

The Vantage may be able to help you save money on your insurance premiums through safety discounts:

  * Anti-theft system
  * Airbags
  * Anti-lock brakes
  * Driving stability control



### **Why is insurance so high for the Vantage?**

While the Vantage provides a high-quality ride, it also comes with high insurance costs. Its rate is greater than the national norm for a variety of reasons:

  * **High ticket price.** This entry-level [Aston Martin](<https://www.astonmartin.com/en-us/>) retains its six-figure price tag. Your insurance company will be required to cover the full cost of repair or replacement, which will result in an increase in your rate.
  * **Higher coverage.** Numerous luxury automobile owners choose for additional coverage to safeguard their significant investment..
  * **Powerful engine.** The Vantage is equipped with more than 500 horsepower to satiate your need for speed. The strong engine, on the other hand, implies you're more inclined to drive it at faster speeds on the road.
  * **Specialized repairs.** This European car may need parts imported overseas, making repairs a more costly endeavor.
  * **Smaller body size.** In comparison to family mid size sedans, sports vehicles have smaller body shapes, which also contribute to their allure as a fast ride.



## **How reliable is the Aston Martin Vantage?**

The Vantage looks to be a dependable vehicle with minimal recalls. However, it has not been subjected to rigorous safety testing by reputable institutions. This information may have an effect on your insurance premiums.

  * **Safety.** Neither the [NHTSA](<https://www.carindigo.com/aston-martin/vantage/safety>) nor the [IIHS](<https://www.carindigo.com/aston-martin/vantage/safety>) have assigned this car a safety rating.
  * **Reliability.** Both the V8 and V12 engines provide sufficient dependability for daily drivers as well as weekend drivers who conduct routine maintenance.
  * **Recalls.** This vehicle has been subject to a few recalls. In one V8 Vantage recall, passengers were unable to unlock the doors from within the vehicle after it had been locked from the outside.



Additionally, the V12 has been the subject of many recalls, including one for the door-locking issue. Additionally, it has experienced difficulties with the throttle pedal arm snapping. This issue may impair the driver's ability to manage the engine's speed.

## **Bottom line**

The Aston Martin Vantage is equipped with a number of safety features that may qualify you for an insurance discount. Additionally, insurance companies may appreciate the car's low theft rate. Nonetheless, ensuring a high-end European sports vehicle will always have drawbacks.
