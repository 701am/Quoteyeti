## **How do I compare insurance for the Venue?**

There are several elements that influence your insurance premiums, including vehicle-specific characteristics. Among them are the following:

  * **Body type.** The [Hyundai Venue](<https://www.hyundaiusa.com/us/en/vehicles/venue>) is a subcompact crossover SUV that is less expensive to insure than sedans but more expensive than pickups.
  * **Fuel.** With an estimated combined fuel economy of 33 mpg, you'll be able to save money on gas when compared to comparable SUVs.
  * **Theft rates.** According to NHTSA figures from November 2019, Hyundai SUVs have a low theft rate, which helps to keep insurance costs low.
  * **Maintenance.** Average yearly maintenance expenses in the Venue model of cousin, Santa Fe, have been around $515 as of November 2019, based on the RepairPal website. Small maintenance expenditures may assist maintain low rates of insurance.
  * **Coverage.** Consider maintaining a new location with both a collision and extensive coverage.
  * **Warranty.** The Venue includes a 5-year, 60,000-mile guarantee or 10-year, 100,000-mile guarantee from Hyundai. Both guarantees include roadside assistance, which enables you to avoid the insurance coverage.
  * **Manufacturer offer.** At the time of this writing, no official offers were made for the 2020 Venue, although Hyundai generally provides offers for their latest models including 0 percent of APR funding.
