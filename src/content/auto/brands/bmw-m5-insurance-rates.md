## **What factors affect car insurance for a BMW M5?**

A variety of factors may impact the costs of car insurance, among others:

  * **Vehicle age.** The newer your M5, the more costly it is to insure, generally speaking. But be aware that discounts from new safety measures might reduce part of this price.
  * **Engine type.** Four, six and eight-cylinders with choices for diesel engines and integrated electrical engines are offered in the BMW 5 range. The more power your motor has, the more probable it is that your rates will be higher.
  * **Types of tires.** While the normal 20" pneumatic tire on its M5 is recommended by BMW, it can elect to use 19-pneumatic tires, thereby enabling a larger premium for suspension damage when driving over uneven road conditions.
  * **Add-ons.** BMW provides a range of bundled improvements, including improved trimmings, upholstery, luxury sitting and dynamic packages, for the M5. While these changes increase the comfort in the inside of your M5, they add value to your car and might increase the cost of your insurance.

## **Reliability and safety ratings for the BMW M5**

In general, the BMW M5 is a trustworthy car you can trust in many various circumstances of weather and road. Its strong engine and smart technological [security features](<https://iihs.org>) enable you and your passengers to be secure and comfortable.

That said, online reviews of this make and model indicate that if you’re going to run into mechanical problems with your M5, it will likely be an issue with the throttle actuator, the oil pump, or an electronics failure. In 2019, BMW recalled the aforementioned vehicles for defective wire harnesses for 2019-2020 M5.

While M5s are running well many times, a mechanical remedy is probably costly, since BMW components are not cheap. Many owners of the M5 offer to buy the extended warranty on this car, which you can thank in the long run.
