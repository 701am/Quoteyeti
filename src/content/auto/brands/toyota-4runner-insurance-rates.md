A Toyota 4Runner's average annual insurance cost is $2,724 or $227 per month. Your exact cost will vary according to your driving record, the number of miles you travel each year, and other factors.

## **Factors that can affect your auto insurance rate**

  * Toyota 4Runner’s model year
  * Vehicle’s trim
  * Amount of coverage you need
  * Estimated miles your drive in a year
  * Safety and anti-theft features
  * Driver’s credit score and location
  * Driver’s age, marital status and gender
  * Driver’s driving history in the past five years
  * Whether the driver rents, leases or owns the vehicle

## **How safe is the Toyota 4Runner?**

[Toyota](<https://www.toyota.com/>) introduced the 4Runner in 1984 and it is now in its sixth generation. The midsize SUV is renowned for its superior off-road capability and spacious load area. In 2016, approximately 111,000 sales occurred in the United States. The Toyota 4Runner is the third-longest-lasting car in the United States, according to a 2016 research by iSeeCars.com.

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration awarded the 2017 Toyota 4Runner a 4-Star Overall Safety Rating ([NHTSA](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2017/TOYOTA/4RUNNER/SUV/AWD>)). The NHTSA awarded the side crash test five stars, the best attainable grade. Additionally, the government agency gave the vehicle a four-star safety rating in the frontal crash test and a three-star rating in the rollover crash test. The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety ([IIHS](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/toyota/4runner-4-door-suv/2021>)) assigned a marginal grade to the small overlap front test, which is below average. All other IIHS crash testing resulted in an excellent safety rating, the best attainable.

Downhill assists control, brake assist, and smart stop technology are all standards on the Toyota 4Runner.
