## **How safe is the Toyota Avalon?**

The [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2019/TOYOTA/AVALON/>) awarded the 2017 Toyota Avalon a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating (NHTSA). The NHTSA awarded the side crash test five stars, the best attainable grade. Additionally, the government agency gave the vehicle a four-star safety grade in the frontal collision and rollover crash tests. Toyota Avalon was designated a Top Safety Pick by the [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS)](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/toyota/avalon-4-door-sedan/2021>). All IIHS crash testing resulted in an excellent [safety rating](<https://www.jdpower.com/cars/2020/toyota/avalon/limited>), the best attainable.

Toyota's Avalon model is equipped with a collision warning system, blind spot monitoring, and lane keeping assist.
