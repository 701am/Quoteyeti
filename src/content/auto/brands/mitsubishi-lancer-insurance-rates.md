## **About the Mitsubishi Lancer**

[Mitsubishi](<https://www.mitsubishicars.com/#filter-modal>) introduced the Lancer in 1973. Between 1973 and 2008, approximately six million models were sold globally. The tiny automobile is renowned for its precise handling, all-wheel-drive capabilities, and affordable starting price. Mitsubishi will discontinue the manufacture of the Lancer in August 2017 in order to focus on crossover and SUV vehicles.

## **How safe is the Mitsubishi Lancer?**

The [Mitsubishi Lancer](<https://www.mitsubishicars.com/lancer-history>) has a 4-Star Overall Safety Rating from the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration ([NHTSA](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2017/MITSUBISHI/LANCER/4%252520DR/FWD>)). The NHTSA awarded the Mitsubishi Lancer four out of five ratings in all crash testing. The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety ([IIHS](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/mitsubishi/lancer-4-door-sedan/2017>)) assigned a satisfactory rating to the small overlap front test, the second-highest possible. All other IIHS crash testing resulted in an excellent rating, the best attainable.

The Mitsubishi Lancer's newest generation has anti-lock brakes, seat-mounted side airbags, and electronic stability control.
