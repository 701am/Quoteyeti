## **How safe is the Toyota Sienna?**

The Toyota Sienna 2017 was granted a 5-star overall safety rating by the [National Highway Safety Authority (NHTSA)](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2020/TOYOTA/SIENNA/VAN/FWD>). The NHTSA lateral crash test earned the highest grade, five stars. In the Frontal Crash Test and Crash Test the government agency also gave the four-star safety assessment. The [Highway Insurance Institute (IIHS)](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/toyota/sienna-minivan/2021>) ranked the small front overlap test as satisfactory and average. All other IIHS crash tests have gotten the highest possible [safety assessment](<https://www.jdpower.com/cars/2021/toyota/sienna>).

The model Toyota Sienna incorporates warning of the lane exit, braking assistance and monitoring of the blind spot.
