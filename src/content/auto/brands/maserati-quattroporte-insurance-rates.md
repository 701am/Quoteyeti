The Italian-made [Quattroporte](<https://www.maseratiusa.com/us/en/models/quattroporte>), which debuted in 2005, is almost a Maserati classic. It now costs around $107,400, with a monthly insurance premium of $433 on average. It's more expensive to insure than a standard Mercedes-Benz, but less expensive than a Ferrari.

## **How do I compare insurance for the Maserati Quattroporte?**

The word "quattroporte" means "four doors" in Italian. According to Maserati, this sports vehicle blends "race-bred performance with unmatched elegance." When purchasing insurance, keep the following considerations in mind:

  * **Body type**. It's a big sedan that's less expensive to insure than a coupe. However, because this is a premium vehicle inspired by racecars, that is unlikely to aid with insurance savings.
  * **Fuel**. The Quattroporte gets 16 city MPG and 23 highway MPG, for a total 17 to 18 MPG, which is about average for sedans.
  * **Maintenance**. Maserati suggests maintaining your Quattroporte with authentic Maserati Parts and visiting one of its shops for assistance. Parts and labor may be more expensive as a result, especially if it is a foreign vehicle.
  * **Coverage**. Because the Quattroporte is more expensive than the ordinary automobile, look into luxury car insurance, which will have greater maximums and provide more sufficient protection.
  * **Warranty**. Maserati's New Car Limited Warranty covers the vehicle for four years or 50,000 miles, whichever comes first. It also covers roadside assistance, which includes moving your vehicle to a dealership 24 hours a day, seven days a week.
  * **Transmission**. The Quattroporte features an automatic transmission, which can be more expensive to insure than a manual due to the higher cost of the gearbox.



### **Does the Maserati Quattroporte qualify for discounts?**

Despite being praised mostly for its speed and superb aesthetics, the Quattroporte has certain characteristics that may qualify you for discounts, including:

  * Optional emergency braking
  * Optional adaptive cruise control
  * Anti Lock brakes
  * Stability control
  * Front-impact airbags
  * Side-impact airbags
  * Overhead airbags
  * Knee airbags
  * Pretensioners
  * Anti Whiplash
  * Security system



## **Why is insurance so high for the Maserati Quattroporte?**

The Maserati Quattroporte is more expensive to insure since it is a high-priced luxury vehicle with a racing car spin; a fast sedan might indicate higher-than-average risk for insurers. Furthermore, this foreign vehicle necessitates costly components and labor.

## **How reliable is the Maserati Quattroporte?**

The Maserati Quattroporte has a sporty ride and Italian styling. When insuring yours, keep the following factors in mind:

  * **Safety**. The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety has not evaluated this vehicle for safety, most likely due to its high price.
  * **Reliability**. Overall, consumers appear to be pleased with the performance of the Quattroporte. However, bear in mind that its unique engineering may be time-consuming, so frequent maintenance checks are essential.
  * **Recalls**. Quattroportes has been the subject of 24 recalls since 2004. Maserati pledges to inform owners and fix or repair the faults at no cost, but recalls are severe and can endanger owners if left unattended.



## **Bottom line**

The Maserati Quattroporte's sophisticated design and strong engine make it an enticing sports vehicle, but it may be costly to insure. To get the greatest bargain on insurance, compare rates.
