The average monthly premium for a 2021 [Chevrolet Trax](<https://www.chevrolet.com/suvs/trax>) insurance cost is $146.97, or $1,763.64 per year. Your real expenses, however, are determined by your driving history, car model, location, and other considerations.

## **About the Chevrolet Trax**

The Chevrolet Trax has been on the market since 2013, although it was just recently launched in the United States. The subcompact SUV is well-known for its outstanding safety ratings and comfortable ride. The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration granted the most recent model a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating.

## **Chevrolet Trax money-saving safety features**

If your Chevrolet Trax has these safety features, you may be able to save money on car insurance.

  * Anti-lock brakes
  * Stability control
  * Airbags
  * Seatbelt pretensioners
  * Anti theft system
