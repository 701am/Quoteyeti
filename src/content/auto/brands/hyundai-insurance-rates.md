## **Hyundai roadside assistance**

Hyundai new vehicles come with five years of complimentary roadside assistance. Hyundai's roadside assistance program covers the following:

  * Towing to a Hyundai dealership or approved repair facility
  * Battery jump start
  * Flat tire change
  * Gas delivery if you run out of fuel
  * Lockout if you lost your key
  * Trip interruption for food, hotel and alternate transportation for overnight trips over 150 miles away from home



To access Hyundai roadside help, dial 1-800-243-7766, which is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week. When you contact, you must supply the brand, model, VIN, and mileage of your car.

New Hyundai drivers do not need to add this coverage to their vehicle insurance policy. Drivers with older Hyundais who no longer have roadside warranty coverage can add roadside assistance to their vehicle insurance policy to receive roadside assistance when needed.
