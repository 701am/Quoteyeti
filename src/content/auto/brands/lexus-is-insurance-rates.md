## **How safe is the Lexus IS?**

The Lexus IS 2017 was ranked 5-star overall by the National Road Safety Authority (NHTSA). Five stars were rated, the highest rating was on the side crash test and the [NHTSA ](<http://nhtsa.gov>)rollover test. The Lexus IS also scored the frontal crash test with four stars. The Highway Insurance Institute (IIHS) rated only moderate front overlap, side impact and roof strength tests. The [IIHS](<http://iihs.org>) granted the top rating for all Lexus IS testing.

Lexus IS' newest model features airbags, side impact airbags and antilock brakes.  
The Lexus IS is presently in its third generation since 1998. The compact luxury automobile is famous for its smooth ride, powerful motor options and a broad range of standard driver support systems. More than 37,000 sales were made in the United States in 2016. In 2011, Lexus IS ranked top in the [J.D. Power](<http://jdpower.com>) Car Satisfaction Survey.
