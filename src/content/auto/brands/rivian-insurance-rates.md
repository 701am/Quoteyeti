## **About the Rivian Brand**

Rivian, a Michigan-based business, has revealed a portfolio of all-electric vehicles that will hit the market shortly. The R1T is the world's first electric pickup truck, and the R1T and R1S electric SUVs can go up to 440 miles on a single charge. The premium adventure cars are built to be suitable for both offroading and road excursions.

After a $500 million investment from [Ford Motors](<https://www.ford.com/>) and a $700 million investment from Amazon, manufacturing of the first Rivian vehicles is expected to commence in 2020, with a 2021 release date.
