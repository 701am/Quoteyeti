## **How can I compare insurance for this vehicle?**

Keep these considerations in mind when comparing suppliers.

  * **Price.** Above all, vehicle insurance should be reasonably priced without sacrificing coverage. Determine how much coverage you want, and then use that sample to obtain estimates from different providers.
  * **Coverage types.** Because the G90 is a high-end, high-performance vehicle, you may wish to safeguard it by purchasing more than the bare minimum of liability insurance. Examine your options for uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage, medical costs coverage, and personal injury protection (PIP).
  * **Extras.** If you want to take advantage of optional features such as vanishing deductibles and accident forgiveness, contact your insurer for a list of those that are available.
  * **Restrictions.** You should have no trouble insuring your G90 because of its outstanding safety ratings. However, because it is a premium vehicle, many insurers will not cover minors, rideshare drivers, or policyholders traveling abroad. Read up on these limitations to avoid denied claims and coverage gaps.
  * **Warranty.** Any new G90 comes with a 5-year, 60,000-mile basic warranty or a 10-year, 100,000-mile powertrain warranty. This is longer than the majority of its competitors. The Genesis warranty package offers free scheduled maintenance for three years or 36,000 miles, whichever comes first.

## **What factors affect car insurance for the Genesis G90?**

Insurers calculate their rates using sophisticated algorithms. The premium you are offered is determined by your driving history, location, mileage, and the vehicle you drive.  
These factors will influence your rate on a Genesis G90.

  * **Body type.** The vehicle is a full-size luxury automobile. It's fashionable, sporty, and powerful, yet its lengthy guarantee and built-in safety features help to balance the expense of insurance.
  * **Safety features.** The G90 is equipped with more standard safety measures than other vehicles in its class. Blind-spot monitoring, automated emergency braking, and lane-departure alerts are among its outstanding features. These decrease the likelihood of damage in an accident and frequently lower insurance premiums as well.
  * **Repairs.** The G90, as a high-performance sedan, is expensive to fix or replace, which may result in a premium increase.
  * **Fuel option.** The 3.3L and 5L versions both feature turbocharged engines and operate on premium gas, which can cause rates to rise.



### **Driver factors that affect insurance rates**

If we were to define auto insurance prices in one word, it would be varied. In addition to your age, gender, location, and the vehicle you drive, insurers look at your driving history, as well as any infractions, accidents, and claims. If you have a spotless driving record, you will almost certainly get a lower premium.

Insurers will also look at your designated drivers. Is your car being driven by teenagers? Are drivers who have received speeding fines and traffic infractions on your list? If this is the case, you will almost certainly be charged a higher premium.

Finally, with the exception of Hawaii, Massachusetts, and California, most carriers consider your credit. The higher your credit, the lower your premium; nevertheless, there are insurers who specialize in insuring drivers with bad credit.

## **Reliability and safety ratings for the Genesis G90**

The G90 has excellent safety and dependability ratings. The G90 was designated a 2018 Top Safety Pick by the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS), the agency's top accolade. The vehicle was praised for its accident avoidance technology, robustness, and headlights. The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) has not yet tested it.

The G90 was placed second in the premium big vehicle category by US News, trailing only the Tesla Model S. It gave the vehicle a 9 out of 10 overall ratings and a flawless score for its safety features, claiming that the G90 boasts more standard safety features than any other car in its class.

The automobile also received a Women's Choice Award for safety and dependability, as well as the AutoPacific President's Award, with the best satisfaction rating in the award's history. The G90 comes standard with a comprehensive set of active and passive safety systems, including automated emergency braking with pedestrian recognition, adaptive headlights, adaptive cruise control, blind-spot monitoring, lane-keep assist, and rear cross-traffic warnings. According to US News, several of these amenities are optional in rival vehicles such as the Audi A7. The model also includes two complete sets of LATCH child-seat connections and child safety locks. You may be eligible for further reductions if you include extra features such as anti-theft devices.

Hyundai recalled 37 G80 and G90 vehicles in May 2018. According to the manufacturer, the windshields and rear windows may have been fitted with the wrong primer, increasing the danger of such pieces falling off while driving. Look up recall number 18V305000 to learn more.

The G90's fuel efficiency is rated as poor by reviewers. According to Fuel Economy, the average yearly fuel expenditure for the 3.3L model is $2,550. This number is based on current gasoline costs and a 45 percent highway/55 percent city driving mix over 15,000 miles. According to Gasoline Economy, you'll pay $5,000 more in fuel expenditures over five years than the average new car.

### **Why does the Genesis G90 look so familiar?**

If the G90 seems familiar, your eyes aren't playing tricks on you. Genesis updated and redesigned its Equus sedan for the 2017 model year, reintroducing it as the G90.  
The Equus was so luxurious and roomy in South Korea, where it was manufactured, that it was frequently employed as an executive limousine chauffeuring CEOs and VIPs. The G90 continues the trend, with a 37.8-inch-long backseat, as well as entertainment and temperature controls.
