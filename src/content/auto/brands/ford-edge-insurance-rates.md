## **How safe is the Ford Edge?**

A 5 Star overall safety rating for the 2017 Ford Edge model has been awarded by the [National Autobahn Authority (NHTSA)](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2019/FORD/EDGE/SUV/AWD>). The Ford Edge was given the NHTSA rollover crash test with four stars out of five. The IIHS assessed the Ford Edge as satisfactory in its small general front test, whereas the other collision tests were judged to be as good, with the highest possible grade.

Ford Edge was the second most stolen SUV from 2010-2013 by the [National Crime Insurance Bureau](<https://www.nicb.org/news/reports-statistics>). During that era there were about 1,140 Ford Edge thieves.
