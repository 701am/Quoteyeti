## **How safe is the Nissan Maxima?**

The government entity gained a 5-star rating, the best possible grade for all crash testing. The [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS)](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/nissan/maxima-4-door-sedan/2021>) has also assessed Nissan Maxima's crash resistance. The vehicle was designated by the IIHS as a Top Safety Pick+. All crash tests have been graded well, the best rating available.

The Nissan Maxima model features warning of the lane departure, blind spot surveillance and electronic stability control.
