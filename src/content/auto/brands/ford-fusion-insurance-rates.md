## **About the Ford Fusion**

The Ford Fusion has been in production since 1976 and is now in its seventh iteration. Ford launched the Fusion model in North America in 2010. The subcompact automobile is well-known for its low price, roomy seating, and good fuel economy. The Ford Fusion sold over 16 million units between 1976 and 2011.

## **How safe is the Ford Fusion?**

The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS) recognized the 2017 Ford Fusion a Top Safety Pick+, and the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) awarded it a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating. The [NHTSA](<https://nhtsa.com>) gave the Ford Fusion four out of five ratings in the rollover and frontal collision tests.

According to the IIHS, all crash testing were rated excellent, the best attainable grade. To protect passengers in the event of a collision, the Ford Fusion includes side airbags and a driver knee airbag. The most recent model also has anti-lock brakes and a rollover sensor to increase passenger safety.

According to the National Insurance Crime Bureau, the Ford Fusion was the second most stolen vehicle in 2013. That year, around 790 Ford Fusion automobiles were stolen.
