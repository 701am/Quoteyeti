## **About the GMC brand**

GMC is a subsidiary of General Motors, most famous for its large vehicles including pickup trucks and SUVs. The Rapid Motor Vehicle Company (GMC) can trace its roots back to the General Motors Corporation (GM) in 1909 when it was purchased by the latter. This merger provided the basis for the birth of General Motors Truck Company. It is called GMC nowadays.

### **What’s the GM Military Discount?**

General Motors provides discounts for women and men in the U.S. Military when they purchase a GMC car. This special deal is also available with our current promotional offerings, for even more savings.
