## **About the Subaru Impreza**

[Subaru's Impreza](<https://www.subaru.com/vehicles/impreza/index.html>) has been on the market since 1992 and is now in its fifth iteration. The small automobile is distinguished by its basic all-wheel-drive system and roomy interior. The Subaru Impreza is offered in two body styles: sedan and hatchback.

## **How safe is the Subaru Impreza?**

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration awarded the 2017 Subaru Impreza a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating ([NHTSA](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2020/SUBARU/IMPREZA/4%252520DR/AWD>)). All of the government organization's crash testing earned the highest possible safety rating. Subaru Impreza was rated a [Top Safety Pick+ vehicle](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/top-safety-picks>) by the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety ([IIHS](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/subaru/impreza-4-door-sedan/2021>)). All IIHS crash tests earned an excellent rating, the best attainable.

Anti-lock brakes, blind-spot monitoring, and stability control are standard on the Subaru Impreza.
