## **About the Mazda brand**

Toyo Cork Kogyo began their business way back in 1920, long before “Zoom-Zoom” was created. A decade after it became a cork business, the company's name was changed to Mazda. To test the market, the newly formed car firm put out an autorickshaw, which they called the Mazda-Go, in 1931.

Mazda launched passenger vehicles and sports cars throughout the rest of the 20th century, including the [R360 Coupe](<https://www2.mazda.com/en/stories/craftmanship/greatcar/p2.html>) and [RX-7](<https://www.carsguide.com.au/mazda/rx-7/price>). The word “Zoom-Zoom” has served as the company's guiding mantra for the last decade.
