Predicted reliability was rated 4 out of 5 stars by JD Power for the 2016 ILX. For the 2015 model year, the dependability of features and accessories gets a 5/5 rating, while the powertrain receives a 3/5 rating, the body and interior receive a 2/5 rating, and the overall rating receives a 3/5 score.

A recall for overheating headlights, defective door latches, and faulty airbag inflators was issued by Acura in February 2016 for the 2013-2016 Acura ILX. It is not yet known whether any recalls would be issued for the 2017 and 2018 models.

Most criticism is directed against the Acura ILX, which debuted in 2014. Customers have complained about concerns such as excessively noisy suspension, Freon odor, and defrost that isn't working. 2014, on the other hand, is not the only model year to experience problems. 

Customer complaints include engine oil leaks from normal operation and transmission sliding, which are particularly common in models from the years 2015, 2016, and 2017.
