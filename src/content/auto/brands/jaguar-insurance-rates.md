## **What causes Jaguar automobile insurance to be so expensive?**

Because insuring a Jaguar places you in the category of premium vehicle insurance, purchasing coverage might quickly become prohibitively expensive. When insurance companies determine your premium, they consider the following factors:

  * Driving record
  * Location
  * Annual mileage
  * Age
  * Credit score (in some states)
  * Vehicle



Take a look at the final one. The brand and model of your car have a significant impact on the cost of your coverage. While insurance companies consider various criteria differently, if you drive a premium vehicle such as a Jaguar, you may expect to pay extra for car insurance.

## **Additional automobile insurance coverage for Jaguars**

You've undoubtedly noticed that we divided our breakdown of vehicle insurance costs for Jaguar into two categories: minimal coverage and full coverage. The term "[minimum coverage](<https://www.bankrate.com/insurance/car/jaguar-insurance/>)" refers to the state-mandated bodily injury and personal property liability insurance. If you want to get the cheapest Jaguar insurance possible, carrying the bare minimal coverage and looking around for the lowest deals is your best choice.

However, if you drive a Jaguar, you're likely to want some [security](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2018/JAGUAR/F-TYPE/2%252520DR/FWD>) for your car. Complete coverage rates include the following:

  * **Collision coverage**. If you cause damage to your own vehicle, this coverage kicks in. Therefore, if you rear-end another vehicle or reverse into a mailbox, collision coverage may pay the cost, minus your deductible and up to the limits of your policy.
  * **Comprehensive coverage**. If your automobile suffers damage that is not caused by driving — for example, a tree limb falls on it or a vandal graffiti it – complete coverage comes to the rescue. Additionally, it covers [vehicle theft](<https://www.iihs.org/media/88d35af1-7e72-47bc-b4dc-382fa30d5f72/L5pY2g/HLDI%20Research/Insurance%20reports/hldi_theft_WT-18.pdf>).
  * **Uninsured motorist coverage**. If another motorist causes an accident but was not insured, you can utilize this coverage to cover the ensuing costs.



Clearly, there is a great deal to consider when it comes to Jaguar auto insurance. However, with this information in hand, you can position yourself to obtain the most comprehensive, inexpensive auto insurance coverage for your Jaguar.
