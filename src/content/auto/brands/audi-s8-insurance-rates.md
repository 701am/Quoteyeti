## **How to compare insurance for the Audi S8**

If you live in a state other than New Hampshire or Virginia, you must have vehicle insurance before you may lawfully drive on public highways. Coverage is a required purchase that virtually all drivers require but no one wants to use. That being said, having the proper coverage may give you peace of mind as well as save you a lot of money in the event of an accident. If you don't know what you're looking for, shopping for coverage may be difficult, which is why generating a sample policy can help you evaluate your alternatives more precisely. Here are a few things to think about when receiving quotes:

  * **Price**. When coverage and features differ, it is impossible to evaluate insurance just on price. Creating a sample policy can assist you in obtaining accurate quotations from different providers, which you can then compare in terms of pricing.
  * **Deductible**. When obtaining estimates, keep in mind that your deductible might impact the cost of your monthly rates. A higher deductible, on the other hand, will cost you less out of pocket after an accident, but will boost your monthly rates.
  * **Minimum coverage**. Aside from New Hampshire and Virginia, each state has its own minimum coverage standards. Before acquiring an insurance, ensure that your state's minimums are satisfied..
  * **Additional coverage**. Personal injury protection and medical expenses coverage are common extras offered by most carriers. When shopping around, make sure to include these in your quotations.
  * **Extras**. The characteristics of your policy will differ based on the provider and plan you select. If you want extras like accident forgiveness and roadside help, make sure to include them in your estimates if they aren't already included.
  * **Restrictions**. Some insurance companies may impose coverage limits that limit what you may do with your car. Do your homework to avoid refused claims or coverage gaps.
  * **Manufacturer offer or loyalty discount**. Find out whether Audi has any affiliations with insurance companies. If this is the case, you may be eligible for manufacturer promotions or loyalty discounts.
  * **Warranty**. To prevent paying for overlapping coverage, find out what is covered by your manufacturer's or extended warranty.

## **How reliable is the Audi S8?**

The S8 does not have any official dependability ratings, while the A8 does. The 2018 A8 has a 3.5/5 star dependability rating, which is higher than most vehicles in its class, according to [U.S. News](<https://cars.usnews.com/cars-trucks/audi/a8>) and [J.D. Power](<https://www.jdpower.com/cars/2021/audi/s8>). Given that the S8 is a step up in terms of price and appearance, it may also be more dependable.

Despite the fact that the S8 has been in production for several decades, there have been very few recalls on this vehicle. Audi recalled a limited number of sunroof-equipped S8s from 2007 to 2009 and 2013 to 2014 owing to a problem that might cause the sunroof glass to shatter or detach while driving. However, the A8 has been subject to a few recalls:

  * **2013** : Almost 10,000 2013 Audi A8 Quattro vehicles were recalled due to coolant leaks into the engine control module, causing a stall or loss of power.
  * **2010-2012** : The coolant leak issue also impacted some Audi A8 vehicles from 2010, 2011, and 2012.



Despite the fact that the S8 has been in production since 1996 and has undergone several overhauls, there are just a few common issues that affect all model years. Having said that, some owners have reported difficulties such as the memory seat adjustment switch not working, early suspension wear, and fluid leaks from the gearbox output shaft seal. The S8, like many other Audi vehicles, may experience carbon buildup, which may result in decreased power and fuel economy.

## **Audi S8 maintenance**

The S8 is Audi's most luxury sedan, featuring a plethora of high-tech safety and driver aid technologies to provide a comfortable and hassle-free driving experience. It was created to be safe and dependable, allowing owners to spend more time on the road and less time in the shop. However, just like any other vehicle, periodic service appointments may guarantee that the automobile runs at peak performance for as long as feasible. Here's what to expect in terms of oil changes, mileage, and other maintenance-related information.

  * Audi suggests changing the oil every 10,000 miles or 12 months, whichever comes first. However, if you drive your car regularly, you may want to have it replaced more frequently.
  * An oil change for a S8 should cost between $125 and $250, depending on how long it has been since the previous oil change and where you bring it for servicing.
  * According to the 2017 Audi maintenance plan, your S8 should be serviced at 10,000 miles and then every 20,000 miles after that. Standard maintenance should be performed after the first 20,000 miles and then every 20,000 miles after that.
  * The 2018 Audi S8 gets 18 miles per gallon on average, with 14 in the city and 24 on the interstate.
  * The 2017 Audi S8 is covered by a four-year/50,000-mile warranty as well as four years of 24-hour roadside support.
