Owning an XJ may result in increased insurance premiums. The premium vehicle has a high sticker price, a high theft rate, and bad dependability. The XJ, on the other hand, might qualify for discounts because of its numerous safety measures.

The average insurance cost for this vehicle is $158 per month or $1,896 per year. This rate is $470 more than the national average of $1,426 per year.

## **How do I compare insurance for the XJ?**

The [Jaguar XJ](<https://www.jaguarusa.com/all-models/xj/index.html>) is a larger luxury car with a high starting price, which may result in higher insurance premiums. Other points of comparison include:

  * **Body type.** The XJ is a premium sedan series that comes in a range of trim levels. The body has a higher vehicle weight for a sedan, which may increase your premium. Heavier cars are thought to be more likely to inflict greater damage in the event of an accident.
  * **Price tag.** The price of this sedan is high for a sedan, but modest for a premium performance automobile. The starting price for a ticket is about $76,000 MSRP.
  * **Theft.** XJs have a high theft rate, with dozens of theft complaints across all trim levels. To offset its increased theft rates, consider purchasing comprehensive coverage.
  * **Fuel.** This car has a modest fuel economy of 18 mpg in the city and 27 mpg on the highway.
  * **Maintenance.** All new Jaguar XJ vehicles come with a free maintenance package.
  * **Coverage.** Consider greater maximum limits and supplementary coverage to protect yourself from having to pay for repairs out of pocket after an accident. Loan/lease gap coverage, accident, and uninsured/underinsured motorists are among them.
  * **Warranty.** This vehicle comes with a five-year or 60,000-mile limited warranty that includes periodic maintenance and roadside assistance.



## **Does the Jaguar XJ qualify for discounts?**

This vehicle has several safety features that may qualify you for an insurance discount:

  * Anti-theft system
  * Airbags
  * Anti-lock brakes
  * Child safety locks
  * Electronic stability control
  * Driveline traction control
  * InControl Protect Emergency SOS
  * Parking sensors
  * Rear collision warning
  * Low tire pressure warning
  * Blind spot sensor
  * Back-up camera



## **How reliable is the Jaguar XJ?**

The XJ has a low dependability rating due to a lack of crash testing, low-reliability ratings, and several recalls.

  * **Safety.** The [NHTSA](<http://nhtsa.com>) has not given the XJ a crash test rating. This is due to the fact that the safety group has not yet officially rated this automobile, as well as many other Jaguar models.
  * **Reliability.**[J.D. Power](<http://jdpower.com>) gives this vehicle a projected dependability rating of 2 out of 5 stars.
  * **Recalls.** Many recalls have been issued for the XJ. Some have included faulty software that impacts airbag calibration, improperly shaped front wiper arms, wrong software calibration for vehicle restraints, rear-wheel alignment difficulties, leaking brake fluid, and problems with the drive belt, power steering, and battery positive cable.



## **Bottom line**

The expensive Jaguar XJ isn't the cheapest luxury car to insure, but it does come with a number of safety measures that help to balance high insurance costs and low dependability.
