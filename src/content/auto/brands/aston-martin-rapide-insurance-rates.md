## **How do I compare insurance for the Aston Martin Rapide?**

The Rapide is a high-priced car designed for performance, which means it will be costly to insure. When evaluating the cost of coverage, providers evaluate many critical factors:

  * **Body type.** Although the Rapide is a sedan, it is classified as a high-performance car, which results in a higher insurance premium.
  * **Fuel.** The Rapide is powered by a gas engine, which is generally less expensive to insure than diesel or hybrid engines.
  * **Theft rates.** The Aston Martin Rapide boasts a low theft rate and excellent anti-theft technology, which may help you save money on insurance.
  * **Maintenance.** Because maintenance and repairs on this car are likely to be pricey, insurance companies will boost premiums proportionately.
  * **Coverage.** For a car this costly, you'll almost certainly want collision and comprehensive coverage, which will boost your premiums.
  * **Insurer options.** While you may be able to obtain coverage from big carriers, you may be able to obtain lower rates from an insurance company that specializes in luxury and performance vehicles.

  * **Safety.** Although this car is not yet certified by the IIHS or NHTSA, its various safety features may assist lower insurance costs.
