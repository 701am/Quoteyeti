## **How reliable is the Audi A6?**

The 2018 Audi A6 receives a poor 2.5/5 star rating for dependability from U.S. News and J.D. Power. Previous models, including the 2017 A6, fared better than average, with a rating of 3.5 stars. According to various sources, the 2017 and 2018 Audi A6 has above-average dependability when compared to comparable cars in its class.

The original Audi A6 was introduced in 1994, and it has gone through several redesigns to get at the current variants seen today. Several recalls have occurred during the decades it has been in production; here are some of the most recent:

  * **2017:** A small number of cars were recalled due to frontal and head airbags that did not deploy correctly.
  * **2012-2015:** A handful of A6 models from 2012 to 2015 were plagued by coolant pump problems that may lead to an engine fire. These vehicles are also subject to recalls owing to gasoline leaks and passenger airbags that may not activate correctly.



There are very few frequent issues with contemporary Audi A6 models. While the 2000 and 2004 versions have an occasional engine and transmission problems, the most current models appear to be fairly dependable.
