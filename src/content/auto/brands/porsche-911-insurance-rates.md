## **About the Porsche 911**

The 911 debuted in 1963, and has been a staple of the auto industry ever since. It is a well-known fact that premium sports cars are noted for their superb handling, many engine choices, and refined interiors. According to the U.S. News & World Report, the 2017 [Porsche](<https://www.porsche.com/usa/>) 911 is the top luxury sports vehicle in the United States.

Porsche 911 is widely recognized for its world-class competitive racing skills. Porsche 911 RSR earned first and fifth position in the GTLM class at the 2019 IMSA WeatherTech SportsCar Championship at Long Beach.

## **How reliable is the Porsche 911?**

Neither the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety nor the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration have rated the 2017 Porsche 911 for its safety.

Adaptive cruise control, blind spot monitoring, and a collision mitigation system are all included in the Porsche 911 model.

According to the [National Insurance Crime Bureau](<https://www.nicb.org/>), the Porsche 911 was the tenth most stolen sports vehicle in the U.S. from 2009 to 2012. Between this time until the end of the time period, there were around 24 thefts.
