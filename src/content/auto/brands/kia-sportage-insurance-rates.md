## **How safe is the Kia Sportage?**

The [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS)](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/kia/sportage-4-door-suv/2021>) designated the Kia Sportage as a Top Safety Pick vehicle. All of the crash tests were given a high grade by the IIHS, which gave the maximum possible score. Kia received a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating from the [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2021/KIA/SPORTAGE/SUV/AWD>) for the Kia Sportage. In frontal and rollover crash tests, the 2017 Kia Sportage got four stars out of five from the NHTSA. Also, the organization gave the side crash test the top rating of five stars.

With these advanced features, the new Kia Sportage model offers forward collision warning, lane departure warning, and blind spot monitoring.
