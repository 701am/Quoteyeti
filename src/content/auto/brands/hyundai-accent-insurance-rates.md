## **About the Hyundai Accent**

The Hyundai Accent has been in production since 1994 and is now in its fourth generation. The vehicle has been the longest-running compact automobile sold in North America since 2002. The subcompact automobile is well-known for its warranty advantages, smooth ride, affordable price, and ample load space. There were approximately 73,000 sales in the United States in 2016.

## **How safe is the Hyundai Accent?**

The Hyundai Accent 2017 has received safety ratings from the [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS)](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings>) and the [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/road-safety>) (NHTSA). The NHTSA granted the newest model a 4-Star Overall Safety Rating. The NHTSA gave the Hyundai Accent four stars out of five for the rollover crash test, frontal crash test, and side impact test. The Hyundai Accent performed poorly in the IIHS's small overlap front test. The IIHS assessed the side impact test as satisfactory, while the other crash tests were rated as excellent, the best grade attainable. To protect passengers in the event of a collision, the Hyundai Accent includes side airbags and a driver knee airbag.

The most recent model also has anti-lock brakes and a rollover sensor to increase passenger safety. Hyundai Accents were among the top ten most stolen new cars in 2012, with around 80 taken.

The number of stolen Hyundai Accent automobiles increased to over 175 in 2013.
