The average [Lexus](<https://www.lexus.com/>) insurance rates is $176 per month, or $2,114 per year. This is less expensive than Lexus' luxury-segment competitors [BMW](<https://www.bmw.com/en/index.html>) ($2,234 average yearly insurance cost) and [Mercedes-Benz](<https://www.mercedes-benz.com/en/>) ($2,499 average yearly insurance cost). Lexus vehicles usually perform well in crash testing and are stolen seldom, which contributes to cheaper insurance prices.

## **What Else Factors Into Lexus Insurance Costs?**

The cost of Lexus insurance varies according to the driver's age. Insurance for younger drivers is more expensive since they lack driving expertise and are more likely to cause an accident. By the time they reach the age of 25, that chance has significantly decreased, as have the premiums. [Insurance premiums](<https://www.bbb.org/article/tips/14093-bbb-tip-auto-insurance>) continue to drop with age until the age of 60.

## **How Can You Lower Your Lexus Insurance Cost?**

Simply because you drive a Lexus does not mean you do not value affordable auto insurance. Reduce your insurance expenses by following these steps:

  * Limit your annual mileage.
  * Park overnight in a secure location.
  * Limit your claims filed.



Additionally to these procedures, if your Lexus is equipped with specific features, you may qualify for additional discounts:

  * Passive restraint systems, including motorized seatbelts and airbags.
  * Anti-lock brakes.
  * Anti-theft devices.



Simply because you possess a premium car does not mean that insuring it has to be prohibitively expensive. Conduct some [research](<https://www.jdpower.com/Cars/Ratings/Lexus/2021>) to determine the most affordable insurance rates, and concentrate on increasing your credit score. These techniques might assist you in obtaining some of the lowest Lexus auto insurance rates available.
