## **About the Rolls-Royce brand**

At the turn of the twentieth century, Henry Royce and Charles Rolls teamed together to market automobiles under the Rolls-Royce brand. They'd already won their first prize, The Best Car in the World, in 1907. Rolls Royce maintained its success over the next several decades, and by the 1960s, the brand had attracted the attention of stars and celebrities, solidifying the Rolls Royce's reputation as a symbol of success.

BMW purchased the rights to make Rolls-Royce automobiles in the 1990s, and they continue to carry on the legacy of creating one of the most famous cars on the road today.
