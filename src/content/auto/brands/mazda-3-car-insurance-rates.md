## **Mazda 3 car insurance rates by Model year**

We obtained [Mazda 3 ](<https://www.mazdausa.com/vehicles/2021-mazda3-sedan>)car insurance quotes from major insurance providers and discovered that the average monthly cost to insure a 2021 Mazda 3 is $159.11. However, depending on the safety features of your car and the insurance you pick, some older models may cost more to insure than others.

### **How we got these rates**

Our prices are based on a single 30-year-old motorist with a clean driving record who lives in California. California is one of seven states that does not base auto insurance rates on gender. We picked comprehensive coverage vehicle insurance with liability limits that surpass the California state minimum requirements, which include:

  * Bodily injury liability per person: $50,000
  * Bodily injury liability per accident: $100,000
  * Property damage liability: $50,000
  * Comprehensive deductible: $500
  * Collision deductible: $500
  * Medical payments: $5,000
  * Uninsured motorist bodily injury: $50,000/$100,000

## **How safe is the Mazda 3?**

The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety designated the 2017 Mazda 3 a Top Safety Pick+ vehicle (IIHS). All crash tests received excellent ratings, the best possible rating, from the IIHS. The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) awarded the Mazda 3 with a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating. The NHTSA rated the frontal crash test and the side impact test five stars, the highest grade. The rollover test received a four-star grade from the government body.

The most recent Mazda 3 model has blind-spot monitoring with rear cross-traffic alert, an automated emergency braking system, and anti-lock brakes.
