## **About the Volkswagen Jetta**

The [Volkswagen Jetta](<https://www.vw.com/en/models/jetta.html>) has been in production since 1979 and is now in its seventh iteration. The midsize automobile is well-known for its numerous engine options, spacious load capacity, and great fuel efficiency. From 1979 through 2014, approximately 14 million Jetta vehicles were sold.

## **How safe is the Volkswagen Jetta?**

The 2017 [Volkswagen Jetta](<https://www.vw.com/en/models/jetta.html>) got a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating from the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration ([NHTSA](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/>)). The NHTSA gave the side impact test five stars, the best attainable grade. The government body also gave the frontal crash test and the rollover crash test four stars for safety. The Volkswagen Jetta was designated a Top Safety Pick+ vehicle by the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS). All crash testing conducted by the IIHS got an excellent safety rating, the best attainable grade.

Lane departure warning, automated post-collision braking, and blind spot monitoring are standard features on the Volkswagen Jetta.
