With a base price of $43,100, the average cost for an [Infiniti QX60](<https://www.infinitiusa.com/vehicles/crossovers/qx60.html>) insurance cost is $220 per month or $2,640 per year. With an annual insurance cost of $2,640, the yearly insurance cost–to–base automobile price ratio is 6.1 percent. This is more than the national average of 4% and nearly double the average rate of $1,300.

## **Why is Infiniti QX60 insurance cost so expensive?**

The Infiniti QX60 insurance cost is more expensive since it is a midsize SUV; a larger vehicle necessitates more maintenance and damage on the road. Furthermore, because this car is a luxury brand, it will have a higher insurance cost than a normal vehicle.

## **About the Infiniti QX60**

The Infiniti QX60 has been on the market since 2013. Infiniti added a hybrid model to their range in 2014. The midsize SUV is well-known for its three rows of roomy seating as well as its exceptional fuel economy.  
Infiniti offers a variety of financing options.

  * Anti-lock brakes
  * Stability control

  * Seatbelt pretensioners

## **How safe is the Infiniti QX60?**

The [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS)](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/infiniti/qx60-4-door-suv/2020>) and the [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA)](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2020/INFINITI/QX60/SUV/FWD>) have assessed the Infiniti QX60 for safety ratings. The IIHS dubbed the midsize SUV a Top Safety Pick+, and it received the top grade in all crash testing. The Infiniti QX60 received a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating from the NHTSA. The organization gave the frontal crash test and the rollover crash test the highest possible rating of excellent.

Lane departure prevention, blind spot intervention, and backup collision intervention are all standard features on the [Infiniti QX60](<https://www.infinitiusa.com/vehicles/crossovers/qx60.html>).
