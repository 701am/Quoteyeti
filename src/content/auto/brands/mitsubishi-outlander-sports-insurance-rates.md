## **How safe is the Mitsubishi Outlander Sport?**

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration ([NHTSA](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2021/MITSUBISHI/OUTLANDER%2520SPORT/SUV/FWD>)) bestowed a 4-Star Overall Safety Rating to the Mitsubishi Outlander Sport. The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration awarded four stars out of five for the frontal collision and rollover tests. The government body granted the side crash test a five-star grade.

The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety ([IIHS](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/mitsubishi/outlander-sport-4-door-suv/2021>)) assigned a satisfactory rating to the small overlap front test, the second-highest possible. All other IIHS crash testing resulted in an excellent rating, the best attainable.

The Mitsubishi Outlander Sport's most recent model has anti-lock brakes, seat-mounted side airbags, and electronic stability control.
