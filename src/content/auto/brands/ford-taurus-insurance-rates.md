## **How safe is the Ford Taurus?**

The 2017 Ford Taurus model was assessed for safety ratings by the Highway Safety Institute (IIHS) and the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA). The newest model has the NHTSA 5-Star Safety Overall Rating. For the rollover crash test, the Ford Taurus was given four stars out of five. According to IIHS, the Ford Taurus was not rated as a small overlap front test and the other accident tests were considered to be good, as high as possible. Ford Taurus comes with side airbags and a driver knee airbag for the protection of passengers. The newest model also incorporates anti-locking and rolling sensors to give passengers more safety.

According to the NHTSA in 2013, 159 models of Ford Taurus were stolen.
