## **About the Infiniti QX50**

The Infiniti QX50, the successor of the Infiniti EX, has been on the market since 2013. The Infiniti QX50 debuted in the United States in 2014. The tiny luxury SUV is notable for its powerful V6 engine, cheap starting price, and nimble handling. For the third year in a row, GQ magazine named the Infiniti QX50 one of the best ten luxury SUVs on the market.

## **How safe is the Infiniti QX50?**

The [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS)](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/infiniti/qx50-4-door-suv/2021>) and the [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2021/INFINITI/QX50/SUV/AWD>) (NHTSA) have assessed the 2017 Infiniti QX50 for safety ratings. The IIHS only assessed the moderate overlap front crash test, side impact test, and rear crash protection test, all of which received the maximum possible rating of excellent. According to the IIHS, there is also advanced frontal collision prevention, which is below average. The rollover crash test earned four out of five stars from the NHTSA.

Lane departure prevention, blind spot intervention, and backup collision intervention are all standard features on the Infiniti QX50.
