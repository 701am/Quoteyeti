## **How reliable is the Acura RLX?**

While Acura is well-known for its dependability, the RLX appears to fall short. According to [U.S. News](<https://cars.usnews.com/cars-trucks/acura/rlx>) and [J.D. Power](<https://www.jdpower.com/cars/2020/acura/rlx>), it has a below-average rating of 2.5/5 stars and is ranked #9 out of 9 in luxury big vehicles. Previous model year ratings are slightly higher, but with an average of 3/5 stars between 2015 and 2017, there isn't much of an improvement.

According to J.D. Power, which keeps a database of recalls up to date with industry updates, about 20,000 Acura RLX vehicles have been recalled:

  * **2015:** Approximately 10,000 cars were affected by a problem in which the Collision Mitigation Braking System (CMBS) deployed the brakes improperly and abruptly in certain driving circumstances.
  * **2014:** Around 10,000 cars were recalled because the driver and front passenger seat belts would not stretch or retract correctly in cold weather.



There aren't many concerns or complaints regarding the RLX, but of all model years, 2014 appears to have had the most. Suspension problems, a faulty infotainment system, and low mpg are common complaints.

## **How safe is the Acura RLX?**

Despite the RLX's poor dependability ratings, it has excellent safety ratings, as do the majority of Acura vehicles. In terms of safety, the 2018 Acura RLX receives a perfect 10 from J.D. Power and U.S. News. Frontal, side, and rollover crash tests all yielded flawless ratings, naming the Acura RLX the IIHS's top safety selection for 2018.
