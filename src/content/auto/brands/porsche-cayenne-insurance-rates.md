## **About the Porsche Cayenne**

Porsche introduced the Cayenne in 2003 and it is now in its second generation. The midsize luxury SUV is renowned for its quick acceleration, superior handling, and luxurious cabin. In 2017, the Porsche Cayenne set a Guinness World Record for pulling the heaviest airplane by a production vehicle. The automobile hauled a 285-ton airplane 42 meters.

Insurance companies calculate the cost of insurance for a Porsche Cayenne based on the vehicle's safety rating, theft rate, replacement cost, and general susceptibility to damage.
