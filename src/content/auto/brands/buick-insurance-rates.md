## How Much Does It Cost To Insure A Buick?

When it comes to car ownership, one of the most important factors to consider is the cost of insurance. For those interested in owning a Buick, it's essential to understand the average insurance cost for these vehicles. As a reputable American car manufacturer, Buick models are known for their safety features and overall reliability, which can help ensure your insurance premiums remain reasonable.

Average Buick car insurance costs typically vary based on factors such as the specific model, your location, and your driving record. For example, the Buick Encore, a popular model, has an average insurance cost of [$125 per month](<https://www.thezebra.com/auto-insurance/vehicles/buick/encore/>), while the annual insurance cost for this model is about [$1,414 per year](<https://www.withclutch.com/insurance-cost/buick-encore>) for standard coverage. By examining these factors and comparing rates from various insurers, you can make an informed decision on which Buick model best suits your needs and budget.

## Factors Affecting Buick Insurance Cost

There are several factors that can influence the cost of insurance for Buick vehicles. Some of them are:

  1. Make and Model: Different Buick models have different insurance rates, depending on their safety ratings, repair costs, and other factors. For example, the average insurance cost for a Buick Encore is $1,433 a year, while the average cost for a Buick Envision is slightly higher, according to [Finder](<https://www.finder.com/car-insurance/buick-car-insurance-rates>).
  2. Location: Your geographic location plays a significant role in determining the cost of insurance. States with higher accident rates, theft rates, or natural disaster risks often have higher insurance costs. For example, as per [caredge.com](<http://caredge.com/buick/insurance>), the average annual insurance cost for a Buick in Alabama is $1,778, while the same vehicle in Alaska costs $1,471 to insure.
  3. Credit History: Drivers with poor credit histories tend to pay more for auto insurance. A study by [The Zebra](<https://www.thezebra.com/auto-insurance/vehicles/buick/>) found that people with low credit scores have higher premiums.
