## **What factors affect car insurance rates for Audi Q5?**

Your rates are calculated by providers depending on a variety of criteria related to your risk of being involved in an accident, making a claim, and the expense of paying out claims. This is why your location, driving history, and a variety of other factors all have a direct impact on the premium you'll pay for vehicle insurance. 

While these personal characteristics all have an influence, your car itself has a significant impact on the cost of auto insurance. Here's how the [Audi Q5](<https://www.audiusa.com/us/web/en/models/q5/q5/2021/overview.html>) compares to other vehicles and how its features may impact your insurance rates.

  * **Body type.** The Q5 is a crossover SUV, a body style that performs well in an accident. When compared to sedans or sports vehicles, this may result in reduced insurance costs.
  * **Type of car.** Audi is known for producing premium automobiles, and the Q5 is no different. Its status as a premium car may result in higher rates.
  * **Theft.** SUVs and crossovers are stolen considerably less frequently than sedans, which may explain why rates are typically cheaper.
  * **Repairs.** Despite the fact that Audi cars are manufactured elsewhere, fixing the Q5 should not be significantly more expensive than repairing other SUVs in its class. Parts and repair businesses are easily accessible, which may contribute to lower vehicle insurance rates.
  * **Safety ratings.** The Q5, like many other Audi vehicles, has excellent safety ratings, which could help you save money on insurance.



## **How do trim packages and extra build options affect insurance cost?**

As you are aware, the car you drive has a significant impact on the cost of your auto insurance. Your rates are calculated by providers depending on a variety of vehicle-specific criteria such as power, planned usage, safety features, and more. 

However, because most new cars are built to meet the demands of the driver, some choices may affect the vehicle's performance. Depending on the trims and options on your Audi Q5, your provider may need to alter your prices to reflect its features correctly. Here's how different trims, packages, and extras may influence your insurance prices based on the standard "Komfort" package.

### **Trim**

  * **Progressiv.** The Progressiv package includes an entry-level sensor system, cruise control, front and rear parking sensors, and other features. Increased visibility may help to prevent accidents and, as a result, lower your insurance premiums.
  * **Technik.** This package includes all of the features included in the Progressiv package, as well as improved lighting, Audi Connect, and a variety of additional amenities. Premiums might be decreased if visibility and security increase.



### **Packages and options**

  * **Driver Assistance Package.** The inclusion of adaptive cruise control, traffic sign recognition, and other driver aid systems may result in lower vehicle insurance rates.
  * **Upgraded suspension.** Choosing one of the suspension kits may enhance handling and, as a result, help you save money on vehicle insurance.
  * **Trailer hitch.** Towing objects may place additional pressure on your car or increase the likelihood of damage, which may result in higher insurance costs.
  * **Front and rear side airbags.** Including this safety element may assist to prevent harm and damage in the case of a collision, which may also help to lower your insurance premiums.
  * **Wheel locks.** Wheel locks significantly minimize the likelihood of your wheels being stolen. Providers are aware of this and may offer you a discount if you have them installed.

## **Audi Q5 maintenance**

The Q5, like many other Audi models, was intended for comfort and ease. Because of its dependability and low-maintenance construction, the 2018 model was named the best luxury compact SUV for families. Having said that, every vehicle requires periodic maintenance to guarantee its efficiency and dependability. Here's what to anticipate from the Audi Q5 in terms of oil changes, mileage, and needed maintenance.

  * Audi suggests changing the oil every 10,000 miles or 12 months, whichever comes first. An oil change may range from $100 to $200, depending on how long it's been since your oil and filter were changed and where you take it for repair.
  * According to the 2017 Audi maintenance plan, your Q5 should be serviced at 10,000 miles and then every 20,000 miles after that. Standard maintenance should be performed after the first 20,000 miles and then every 20,000 miles after that.
  * The 2018 Audi Q5 2.0 gets 25 miles per gallon on average, with 23 in the city and 27 on the interstate.
  * Audi urges owners to take their Q5 to a dealer for repair, but it will not void your warranty if you do so. If your car is subject to a recall, you should take it to an authorized Audi dealer for repairs.
  * The 2017 Audi Q5 is backed by a four-year/50,000-mile guarantee as well as four years of 24-hour roadside support.
