The typical cost of [Lotus](<https://www.lotuscars.com/en-GB/>) insurance cost varies based on the model, your region, your driving history, and a variety of other criteria. However, this ultra-high-end sports vehicle comes with a hefty monthly charge.

Additional factors affecting the cost of insurance include the vehicle's [safety rating](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/>), its probability of being stolen, and the cost of repair or replacement.

## **Is Lotus insurance expensive?**

The Lotus [Evora](<https://www.jdpower.com/cars/lotus>) model is more expensive to insure since its 400 horsepower engine is both powerful and dangerous to insurance providers. Also, if you have a manual, you may be increasing the danger factor with greater peak speeds and a high cost of repair if you are in an accident.

## **Should You Buy A Lotus?**

As with its high price tag, this high-performance sports vehicle will have high insurance premiums, so do your research to get the most affordable auto insurance for you.
