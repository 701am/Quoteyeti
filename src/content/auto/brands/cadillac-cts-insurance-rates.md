The average cost of Cadillac's CTS insurance is 173 dollars a month, or 2076 dollars a year. Depending on your driving record, how many miles you travel in a year, and other factors, your real costs might be much or less.

## **About the Cadillac CTS**

Since 2003 the [Cadillac CTS](<https://media.cadillac.com/media/us/en/cadillac/vehicles/cts_sport_sedan/2017.tab1.html>) has been a premium medium-size vehicle. Around 16,000 versions were sold in the United States in 2016. 

The Cadillac CTS is recognized for its strong motor, spacious cabin, and good fuel savings. The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration granted the 2017 model a 5-star overall rating.
