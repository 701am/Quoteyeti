Insurance for a 2020 Chevrolet Sonic costs on average $161.01 per month — or $1,932.12 per year. However, your real prices will vary depending on your driving history, car model, and region.

## **About the Chevy Sonic**

Chevrolet debuted the [Chevrolet Sonic](<https://www.edmunds.com/chevrolet/sonic/>) in 2011 as the second version of the Chevrolet Aveo, which has been on the market since 2002. The subcompact automobile is characterized by a spacious cabin and cargo area, as well as a comfortable ride and a turbocharged engine. The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration rated the 2017 model a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating.

Insurance companies base the cost of insurance on the Chevrolet Sonic's safety rating, theft rate, replacement cost, and general susceptibility to damage. Insurance costs vary according to a driver's driving history and area. Before making a purchase, Chevrolet customers should obtain quotes from various insurance providers to ensure they are paying the lowest feasible cost. Chevrolet Sonic purchasers should also conduct research to determine whether they qualify for insurance savings with certain insurance companies.

In 2012, the Aveo was rebranded and sold as the Chevrolet Sonic in the majority of the world.
