The average monthly premium for a Nissan Armada insurance cost is $220, or $2,640 per year. Your exact cost will vary according to your driving record, the number of miles you travel each year, and other factors.

## **Why is Nissan Armada insurance cost so expensive?**

The [Nissan Armada](<https://www.nissanusa.com/vehicles/crossovers-suvs/armada.html>) is more expensive to insure since it is a high-priced luxury SUV that requires extensive repair in the event of an accident. Along with being a premium car, it has a high theft rate, which insurance must account for.

## **About the Nissan Armada**

Nissan's Armada has been on the market since 2004 and is now in its second generation. The big SUV is well-known for its powerful engine, luxurious cabin, and ample seating. The Armada is Nissan's biggest SUV.

## **How safe is the Nissan Armada?**

The [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety](<https://www.iihs.org/news/detail/nissan-titan-slips-in-crash-test-gets-high-marks-for-crash-prevention>) and the [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2019/NISSAN/ARMADA/SUV/AWD>) have not assessed the 2017 Nissan Armada for safety ratings.

Lane departure warning, blind spot monitoring, and electronic stability control are all standard on the Nissan Armada.

According to the [Highway Loss Data Institute](<https://www.jdpower.com/cars/expert-reviews/2021-nissan-armada-review>), the Nissan Armada was the sixth most stolen vehicle in America in 2010.
