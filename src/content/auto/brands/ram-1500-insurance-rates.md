## **How safe is the Ram 1500?**

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration awarded the 2017 Ram 1500 a 4-Star Overall Safety Rating ([NHTSA](<http://nhtsa.gov>)). Both the frontal collision and rollover crash tests earned four out of five ratings. Five stars were awarded for the side crash test. The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety ([IIHS](<http://iihs.org>)) also evaluated the Ram 1500's crash worthiness.

The small overlap and roof strength crash tests both earned a mediocre safety rating. The moderate overlap front crash test, side impact crash test, and rear crash protection test all got the maximum possible safety grade of "good."

Anti-lock brakes, blind spot monitoring, and stability control are standard on the Ram 1500 model.
