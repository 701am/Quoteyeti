The Kia Sorento has been on the market since 2002, and this is the third version of the vehicle. When it comes to midsize SUVs, it's well known that the vehicle has a powerful V6 engine and confidence-inspiring handling.

## **How safe is the Kia Sorento?**

The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety ([IIHS)](<http://iihs.org>) has awarded the Kia Sorento its Top Safety Pick designation. The IIHS found that all of the crash tests received a Good rating, which is the best attainable grade. The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration ([NHTSA](<http://nhtsa.gov>)) has given the Kia Sorento a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating. In all crash tests save for the rollover crash test, the NHTSA rated the Kia Sorento five stars.

Adaptive lighting, automated emergency braking, and blind-spot monitoring are among the latest features included in the Kia Sorento.
