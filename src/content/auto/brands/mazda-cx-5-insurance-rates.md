## **How safe is the Mazda CX-5?**

The [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<https://nhtsa.gov>) gave the 2017 Mazda CX-5 a 4-Star Overall Safety Rating. For the frontal and side crash tests, the government body gave the Mazda CX-5 five stars, the maximum grade. The rollover crash test received four stars. The [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety](<https://iihs.org>) only assessed the moderate overlap front test and the side impact test, both of which earned the highest rating.

The Mazda CX-5's most recent model has blind-spot monitoring with rear cross-traffic alert, an automated emergency braking system, and anti-lock brakes.

## **How does the Mazda CX-5 compare to other car models?**

The Mazda CX-5 has been on the market since 2013 and is now in its second generation. The small SUV is well-known for its outstanding performance, smooth ride, premium interior, and roomy seating.
