This rate is significantly more than that of comparable luxury sedans, such as the [Maserati Quattroporte](<https://www.maseratiusa.com/us/en>), which costs $5,196 per year, and even higher than that of sports and supercars, such as the [Ferrari GTC4 Lusso](<https://www.ferrari.com/en-EN/auto/gtc4lusso>), which costs $11,436 per year. Consider purchasing insurance that includes coverage for any high-value damage to your customized vehicle.

## **How do I compare insurance for the Phantom?**

The [Phantom](<https://www.rolls-roycemotorcars.com/en_GB/showroom/phantom.html>) is at the pinnacle of its luxury class, with a price tag to match – which means your insurance company will want more money to cover you in the event of an accident. Expect Rolls-Royce Phantom insurance cost to be far more than usual, especially for a premium vehicle.

These reasons contribute to the Phantom's exorbitant premium:

  * **MSRP**. Due to the Phantom's half-million-dollar price tag, insurance companies will want additional funds to repair your automobile following a collision.
  * **Coverage**. This luxurious ride may need luxury car insurance, which provides agreed-upon value coverage and greater liability limits for your vehicle. Additionally, if not included in a specialized policy, consider uninsured/underinsured motorist, umbrella, and gap insurance to cover all damage suffered in an accident.
  * **Body type**. Although the Phantom is classified as a sedan or coupe, its full-sized body and premium class place it at the top of the insurance scale.
  * **Powerful engine**. While the Phantom is not a sports vehicle by definition, it does feature a strong 563-horsepower engine, making it a contender for high-speed danger.
  * **Insurer options**. Choosing the appropriate luxury vehicle insurance for your requirements restricts your alternatives, but the trade-off in terms of service and coverage may be worth it.
  * **Maintenance**. This automobile may require special attention from a Rolls-Royce expert with access to European-made parts, making it more expensive to maintain than comparable vehicles. However, the company's new automobiles come with a four-year guarantee that includes maintenance and service.
  * **Warranty**. Each new Rolls-Royce vehicle comes with a four-year, unlimited mileage guarantee that includes maintenance, service, and roadside support.
  * **Fuel.** Due to the Phantom's size, its combined fuel efficiency rating is only 14 mpg.
  * **Theft**. To the benefit of both car owners and insurance, the Phantom boasts a zero-theft history.

## **Why is insurance so expensive for the Rolls-Royce Phantom?**

The Rolls-Royce Phantom insurance cost has a higher price of premium since it is a highly customized luxury automobile at the top of its class that requires expensive European-imported components. Additionally, it features a powerful engine, posing a high-speed danger to insurers.

## **How reliable is the Rolls-Royce Phantom?**

While the Phantom has not been evaluated by prominent safety groups, it is deemed safe and dependable for owners throughout its existence. While there have been a few recalls in the past, this automobile's long manufacturing history demonstrates its popularity and dependability among luxury car owners:

  * **Safety**. The [IIHS](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings>) and [NHTSA](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2020/ROLLS-ROYCE>) have not yet assessed this premium vehicle, probably because people choose less costly vehicles for daily usage.
  * **Reliability**. In addition to the Phantom's four-year maintenance and repair warranty, automobile owners and professionals alike see the vehicle as dependable. Bear in mind, however, that [Rolls-Royce](<https://www.jdpower.com/cars/2021/rolls-royce/phantom>) components are costly when the automobile needs maintenance.
  * **Recalls**. There are a few recalls on older vehicles, including airbags, brakes, and a safety mechanism that stops the car from being misfuelled – all of which raise safety issues. However, these issues appear to have been fixed in subsequent versions.



## **Bottom line**

Due to its top-of-the-line features and lengthy manufacturing history, the Rolls-Royce Phantom maintains an exclusive standing among luxury automobiles. However, anticipate the most expensive auto insurance premiums for luxury vehicles to reflect the vehicle's ticket price and coverage requirements.
