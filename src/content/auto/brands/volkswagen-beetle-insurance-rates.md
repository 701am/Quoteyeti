## **About the Volkswagen Beetle**

The [Volkswagen Beetle](<https://www.volkswagen.com.my/volkswagen-service/older-models/beetle>) has been in production since 1938, making it one of the longest-running models and most-produced cars on a single chassis. By 2003, Volkswagen had manufactured over 21 million Beetle vehicles. The subcompact car is distinguished by its strong engine, premium interior, quick driving, and vintage-style appearance.

## **How safe is the Volkswagen Beetle?**

The [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2019/VOLKSWAGEN/BEETLE/2%252520DR/FWD>) bestowed a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating for the 2017 Volkswagen Beetle (NHTSA). The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration rated the side impact test five stars, the highest possible rating. Additionally, the government agency awarded four stars to the frontal collision and rollover crash tests. The [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS)](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/volkswagen/beetle-2-door-hatchback/2019>) assigned a marginal rating to the small overlap front test, which is below average. All other IIHS crash tests received the highest possible [safety rating](<https://www.jdpower.com/cars/2019/volkswagen/beetle/s-auto>) of good.  
  
The [Volkswagen Beetle](<https://cars.usnews.com/cars-trucks/volkswagen/beetle>) comes standard with lane departure warning, automatic post-collision braking, and blind spot monitoring.
