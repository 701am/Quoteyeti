Since 2011, the Chevrolet Bolt has existed. The hybrid plug-in automobile is well-known for its excellent safety and strong acceleration. Until the 2015 Nissan Leaf was surpassed, the Chevrolet Bolt was ranking among the best selling plug-in electric vehicles.
