## **How Much Is Jeep Gladiator Insurance?**

[Jeep Gladiator](<https://www.jeep.com/gladiator.html>) insurance premium costs are expected to be more than the Wrangler's price of $254 per month, or $3,048 per year. With a starting price of $33,545, the Gladiator's annual insurance cost-to-base ratio is around 9%, which is much higher than the national average of 4%..

## **How do I compare insurance for the Gladiator?**

Insurance for the Jeep Gladiator is more than usual for a compact vehicle.

  * **Body type**. Trucks are more expensive to insure, despite the fact that they are just a Wrangler with a bed.
  * **Fuel.** With a combined fuel economy of approximately 19 mpg, the Gladiator's fuel expenditures will be greater than a car with a higher mileage rating.

  * **Warranty**. Jeep offers a five-year, 60,000-mile powertrain warranty through Fiat's Mopar service. In most cases, the warranty covers roadside assistance, allowing you to avoid such coverage on your insurance until the warranty expires.

## **How reliable is the Jeep Gladiator?**

This new [Gladiator](<https://www.jeep.com.ph/en/2021/gladiator.html>) has yet to earn official reliability and safety ratings, but the similar Wrangler has gotten mediocre scores in both areas.

  * **Safety.** The Jeep Gladiator receives no certified safety ratings, whilst the similar Wrangler receives just average ratings.
  * **Reliability.** Although dependability ratings have not yet been released, [J.D. Power](<https://jdpower.com>) forecasts a 2.5 out of 5 rating, which is the same as the Wrangler.
  * **Recalls.** Jeep has been subjected to a slew of recalls. Jeep issued over 1.5 million recalls across various models in 2013, and the 2019 Wrangler has already received two recalls for the steering wheel and front track bar.
