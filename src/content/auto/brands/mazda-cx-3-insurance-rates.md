## **How safe is the Mazda CX-3?**

The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety designated the 2017 Mazda CX-3 a [Top Safety Pick+ vehicle (IIHS)](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/mazda/cx-3-4-door-suv/2021>). All crash tests received excellent ratings, the best possible rating, from the IIHS. The Mazda CX-3 has a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating from the [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA). The NHTSA](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2021/MAZDA/CX-3/SUV/AWD>) rated the frontal crash test and the side impact test five stars, the highest grade. The rollover test received a four-star grade from the government body.

The Mazda CX-3's most recent model has blind spot monitoring with rear cross traffic alert, an automated emergency braking system, and antilock brakes.
