Car insurance for a 2021 Subaru Legacy costs on average $162.42 per month — or $1,949.04 per year. However, your real prices will vary depending on your driving history, car model, and region.

## **About the Subaru Legacy**

[Subaru](<https://www.subaru.com/index.html>) introduced the Legacy in 1989 and it is now in its sixth generation. The midsize car is famous for its roomy interior, great fuel economy, and superior safety ratings. Between the automobile's introduction and 2008, over 3.6 million Legacy versions were produced worldwide.

## **How safe is the Subaru Legacy?**

The [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2021/SUBARU/LEGACY/4%252520DR/AWD>) awarded the 2017 Subaru Legacy a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating. All of the government organization's crash testing earned the highest possible safety rating. Subaru Legacy was rated a [Top Safety Pick+ vehicle](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/top-safety-picks>) by the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety ([IIHS](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/subaru/legacy-4-door-sedan/2021>)). All IIHS crash tests earned an excellent rating, the best attainable.

Anti-lock brakes, blind-spot monitoring, and stability control are standard on the Subaru Legacy model.
