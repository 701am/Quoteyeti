## **About the Mazda 6**

The Mazda 6 has been in production since 2002 and is now in its third generation. The midsize automobile is well-known for its precise handling, smooth ride, and outstanding fuel efficiency. Car and Driver rated the Mazda 6 one of the top ten selections of 2015. In 2016, there were over 45,000 sales in the United States.

## **How safe is the Mazda 6?**

The [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/mazda/6-4-door-sedan/2021>) designated the 2017 Mazda 6 a Top Safety Pick+ vehicle (IIHS). All crash tests received excellent ratings, the best possible rating, from the IIHS. The Mazda 6 received a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating from the [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA)](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2021/MAZDA/MAZDA6/4%252520DR/FWD>). The NHTSA rated the frontal crash test and the side impact test five stars, the highest grade. The rollover test received a four-star grade from the government body.

The most recent [Mazda 6](<https://www.mazdausa.com/vehicles/mazda6>) model has blind spot monitoring with rear cross traffic alert, an automated emergency braking system, and anti-lock brakes.
