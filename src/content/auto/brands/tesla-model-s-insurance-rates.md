Tesla Model S insurance costs on average $306.22 per month — or $3,674.64 per year — for a 2021 model. However, your real prices will vary depending on your driving history, car model, and region.

## **Why is insurance for the Tesla Model S so expensive?**

The [Tesla Model S](<https://www.tesla.com/models>) insurance cost is more expensive, simply because it is classified as a premium car with a higher than average manufacturer's suggested retail price. Expensive automobiles imply high repair costs in the event of an accident.

## **How dependable is Tesla's Model S?**

[Tesla's Model S](<https://www.tesla.com/models>) has been on the market since 2012 and is an all-electric vehicle. The big luxury automobile is renowned for having the greatest range of any electric vehicle, a spacious luggage area, and world-class performance. Tesla Model S was named World Green Car of the Year in 2013.

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration ([NHTSA](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2016/TESLA/MODEL%252520S%25252090/5%252520HB/RWD>)) assessed just the rollover crash test, which earned the highest safety grade available. The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety ([IIHS](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/tesla/model-s-4-door-hatchback/2020>)) assigned an acceptable rating to the small overlap front test, an average rating. All other IIHS crash testing resulted in an excellent safety rating, the best attainable.
