## **About the Nissan brand**

Nissan, headquartered in Yokohama, Japan's second-largest city, produces automobiles ranging from low-cost family sedans to high-performance supercars. The firm, which was founded in 1933, distributes vehicles with the Nissan, [Infiniti](<https://www.infinitiusa.com/>), and [Datsun](<https://www.datsun.com/>) trademarks.

Nissan is the sixth-largest automaker in terms of output, after [Ford](<https://www.ford.com/>) and [GM](<https://www.gm.com/>) but ahead of [Honda](<https://www.honda.com/>). It also sells the [Leaf](<https://www.nissanusa.com/vehicles/electric-cars/leaf/features/range-charging-battery.html>), the world's second-best-selling electric vehicle.

## **Nissan car facts**

  * Nissan sells automobiles at a variety of pricing ranges, from entry-level to luxury. You'll discover affordable versions like the Versa ($11,990 MSRP), as well as more expensive models like the GT-R ($109,990 MSRP). There are even hybrid variants available.
  * The [Nissan Altima](<https://www.nissanusa.com/>) is the sixth-most-stolen vehicle in the United States, according to the National Insurance Crime Bureau. The [Maxima](<https://www.nissanusa.com/vehicles/cars/maxima.html>) is also frequently targeted by car thieves.
  * Insurance Institute for Highway Safety rating is generally good for Nissans (IIHS). Insurance expenses are lowered. There are certain vehicle models that get a “Fair” or worse grade in driver-side collision protection. The Frontier received a “Marginal” grade, while the Leaf received a “Poor” grade.
  * Nissan Motor Acceptance Corporation (NMAC) offers financing and leasing options with turnaround times that are typically faster than auto loans from a bank. But you may not get the best financing rates on a Nissan through NMAC.
