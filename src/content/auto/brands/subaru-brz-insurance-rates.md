Compare your alternatives to see which business is ideal for commuting in your [Subaru BRZ](<https://www.subaru.com/vehicles/brz/index.html>). Knowing what to search for might help you save money and obtain coverage that is customized to your needs.

## **What factors affect car insurance rates for Subaru BRZ?**

While the BRZ was designed as a sports car, it boasts excellent safety features and crash test scores, which means it should be covered by any major insurance. Having said that, you may wind up paying a greater premium as a result of its design and low dependability.

How the Subaru BRZ's features may impact insurance costs:

  * **Body type**. The BRZ is a two-door coupe with a performance-oriented design. This may result in increased rates.
  * **Type of car**. The Subaru BRZ is a high-performance car capable of reaching high speeds, which may result in increased insurance costs.
  * **Theft**. Sports cars like the Subaru BRZ are stolen at a lower rate than other vehicles, which should result in lower insurance costs.
  * **Repairs**. While the majority of BRZs will require little maintenance, repairs can be costly. This might result in an increase in the cost of vehicle insurance.
  * **Safety ratings**. The 2018 BRZ receives excellent crash test scores in nearly every area, which may result in lower auto insurance rates.
  * **Fuel type**. The BRZ is a gas-powered car, which makes it less expensive to insure than diesel, hybrid, or electric automobiles.
  * **Trim & packages**. Selecting models or trims that enhance power or total value may increase the BRZ's insurance cost. Adding features that increase visibility or safety, on the other hand, may result in lower insurance costs.

## **Subaru BRZ maintenance**

The BRZ is a two-door coupe with a performance-oriented design and the ability to reach faster speeds than an ordinary car. Due to its sport tuning and performance-oriented architecture, it may require more regular maintenance than the economy or luxury cars, especially if it is driven often. Having said that, even basic maintenance may help extend the life of the vehicle and keep it running properly. What to anticipate:

  * Subaru recommended that you schedule service every six months or 6,000 miles, whichever comes first. The initial appointment will involve the replacement of the engine oil and filter, an inspection of different components, and battery service.
  * Oil changes should cost between $75 and $150, however the actual cost may vary depending on the service facility.
  * The BRZ's average yearly repair cost is more than $650, which is more than the average for comparable vehicles.
  * Subaru recommends servicing the BRZ every 6,000 miles or six months, whichever comes first. Consult your dealer or your owner's handbook to determine the scope of each service.
