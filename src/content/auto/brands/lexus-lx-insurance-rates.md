The average monthly insurance premium for a [Lexus LX](<https://www.lexus.com/models/LX>) is $161 — or $1,932 per year. With a starting price of $89,880, the yearly insurance cost–to–base automobile price ratio is a respectable 2.1 percent.

## **About the Lexus LX**

Lexus introduced the LX in 1995 and it is now in its third iteration. The Lexus LX is the brand's largest and most costly SUV. The big luxury SUV is renowned for its off-road prowess and opulent cabin. In 2016, approximately 5,000 sales occurred in the United States.

## **Lexus LX money-saving safety features**

If your Lexus LX is equipped with certain safety features, you may be able to save money on car insurance.

  * Anti-lock brakes
  * Stability control
  * Airbags
  * Seatbelt pretensioners
  * Anti theft system



## **How safe is the Lexus LX?**

As is the case with a large number of premium automobiles, the 2017 Lexus LX has not been assessed for safety ratings by the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety or the [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<http://nhtsa.gov>).

The Lexus LX's most recent model is equipped with side impact airbags and antilock brakes.
