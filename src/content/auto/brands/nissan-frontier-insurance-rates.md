## **About the Nissan Frontier**

[Nissan](<https://www.nissanusa.com/vehicles/future-concept/2022-frontier.html>) introduced the Frontier in 1997 and it is now in its third generation. The small pickup truck is well-known for its affordable starting price, superior off-road capability, and towing capacity of over 6,000 pounds. In Asian and European regions, the vehicle is marketed as the Nissan Navara.

## **How safe is the Nissan Frontier?**

The [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration ](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2019/NISSAN/FRONTIER/PU%25252FCC/RWD>)awarded the 2017 Nissan Frontier a 4-Star Overall Safety Rating (NHTSA). The NHTSA awarded the frontal crash test and the side impact test five stars, the highest possible grade. Additionally, the government body awarded four stars to the rollover crash test.

The [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS)](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/nissan/frontier-crew-cab-pickup/2021>) also assessed the Nissan Frontier's crashworthiness. The moderate overlap front crash test and the side impact test both received an excellent grade from the IIHS, the highest attainable. The IIHS gave the rear crash protection test a satisfactory grade, the second highest attainable.

Lane departure warning, blind spot monitoring, and electronic stability control are all standard on the Nissan Frontier.
