## **How safe is the Ford Explorer?**

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration gave the 2017 Ford Explorer a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating (NHTSA). The NHTSA gave the Ford Explorer four out of five ratings in the rollover crash test. According to the IIHS, the Ford Explorer's small overlap front test was scored as acceptable, while the other crash tests were rated as excellent, the best grade available.

To protect passengers in the event of a collision, the Ford Explorer features side airbags and a driver knee airbag. To increase passenger safety, the newest model has antilock brakes and a rollover sensor.
