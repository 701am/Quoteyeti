The sporty Veloster is an expensive two-door sedan to insure. The average insurance cost for a Hyundai Veloster is $220 per month, or $2,640 per year, which is over double the national average. With a base price of $18,100, the yearly insurance cost–to–base automobile price ratio is 14.6 percent, which is more than triple the national average of 4 percent.

## **About the Hyundai Veloster**

The [Hyundai Veloster](<https://www.hyundaiusa.com/us/en/vehicles/veloster>) has been on the market since 2011. The asymmetric door design distinguishes the automobile from its competitors; one huge door on the driver's side and smaller doors on the passenger's side. 

The most recent edition of the small Veloster is noted for its cheap starting price and a wide range of standard amenities. The Veloster race vehicle was used at the Summer X Games in 2011. The Veloster racing vehicle was released in 2013.

## **How safe is the Hyundai Veloster?**

The Hyundai Veloster 2017 has received safety ratings from the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS) and the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA). The NHTSA granted the most recent model a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating. The NHTSA gave the Hyundai Veloster four stars out of five for the rollover and frontal collision tests. The IIHS evaluated the 2017 model's small overlap front test as minor and its side impact test as acceptable. The IIHS's other crash tests were rated as excellent, with the highest possible score.

The NHTSA recorded 25 stolen models in the United States in 2013. Based on the number of models manufactured that year, that equates to a 0.48 percent theft rate.
