## **How reliable is the Chevrolet Trailblazer?**

Overall, the Trailblazer might enhance its dependability. This car has standard or mediocre crash test scores, average long-term reliability, and several recalls on earlier versions. These safety features might result in a higher auto insurance rate.

  * **Safety.** The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety gave older vehicles low to acceptable ratings for front and side collisions (IIHS). Although there are no crash test ratings for the 2021 model yet, keep in mind that newer automobiles have additional safety features.
  * **Reliability.** Older versions, such as the 2009 Trailblazer, have a dependability rating of 3 out of 5 stars from [J.D. Power](<https://jdpower.com>). Except for gearbox difficulties in 2005 models, the majority of automobile complaints revolve around minor issues like faulty fuel gauges.
  * **Recalls.** As of May 1, 2020, there have been no recalls on the most recent models. However, the earlier Trailblazers have had multiple power window recalls. Fluid flowing into power window wiring might cause a short circuit or possibly a fire. Other recalls included overheated headlights, incorrect windshield mounting, and leather seats that might interfere with airbag sensors.
