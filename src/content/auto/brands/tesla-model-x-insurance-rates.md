Car insurance costs on average $317.07 per month — or $3,804.84 per year — for a 2021 Tesla Model X. However, your real prices will vary depending on your driving history, car model, and region.

## **Why is insurance for the Tesla Model X so expensive?**

The [Tesla Model X](<https://www.tesla.com/modelx>) is more expensive to insure than the typical vehicle, owing to its status as a luxury vehicle that may require high-end parts for repair. 

Being classified as a luxury vehicle increases the cost of repair and, consequently, the cost of insurance.

## **Is the Tesla Model X a safe vehicle?**

The [Tesla Model X](<https://www.tesla.com/modelx>) is an all-electric crossover SUV that has been available since 2016. The premium midsize SUV is renowned for its extended driving range, superior acceleration, and advanced driving technologies. By the end of 2016, over 18,000 vehicles had been sold in the United States, making it the third best-selling electric vehicle on the market. Worldwide, approximately 25,000 units were sold.

The 2017 Tesla Model X is the world's safest SUV. The Tesla Model X is the world's first SUV to get five stars in every area and subcategory. The [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2018/TESLA/MODEL%252520X%25252075D/SUV/AWD>) bestowed a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating on the Tesla Model X. The SUV got the best possible safety rating in all crash testing.

Lane departure warning, improved autopilot, and the automated emergency braking are all included on the Tesla Model X.
