The 2018 Audi RS3 has made its debut in the United States, becoming the first model to do so in several decades. As one of the most expensive sedans in the world, it is expensive to insure, as is customary for Audis. The average cost of Audi insurance is about $200 per month or $2,400 per year. Your rate is determined by your insurance provider, driving history, amount of coverage, and state. Car insurance is frequently less costly in states such as Maine and notoriously expensive in states such as New Jersey, Minnesota, and Georgia.

## **How to compare insurance for the Audi RS3**

Car insurance may be tailored to your specific needs. Change providers or adjust your policy, level of coverage, and extras to fit your needs, resulting in a competitive and flexible market.

Consider the following while comparing coverage for your [Audi RS3](<https://www.audiusa.com/us/web/en/models/a3/rs3-sedan/2022/overview.html>):

  * **Price.** Determine how much coverage you require and then use that sample to compare providers.
  * **Coverage types.** Because the Audi RS3 is a high-priced vehicle, you should consider purchasing more than your state's minimum liability coverage. Many policyholders supplement their coverage with comprehensive coverage, uninsured motorist coverage, collision coverage, and personal injury protection.
  * **Extras.** Depending on your insurer, you may supplement your insurance with extras such as accident forgiveness and vanishing deductibles.
  * **Restrictions.** Audis are generally inexpensive to insure because of their great demand in the United States. However, many insurance companies will not cover teenagers who drive high-end vehicles such as the RS3. Some carriers may have limitations on ridesharing and driving abroad. Learn about restrictions now to prevent refused claims and coverage gaps later.
  * **Warranty.** Any new Audi RS3 comes with a four-year, 50,000-mile basic warranty. It comes with four years of 24-hour roadside support, a 12-year corrosion-perforation guarantee, and the first regular maintenance service at 10,000 miles or 12 months, whichever comes first.

## **What factors affect car insurance for the Audi RS3?**

The cost you are offered is determined by your provider, as well as your driving history, where you reside, and the vehicle you drive. Insurers employ their own algorithms that take into account mileage, body style, and engine power in different ways.

Your insurance premium for an Audi RS3 is impacted by

  * **Body type.** The RS3 is a four-door luxury car. While it is little, it is strong and costly, therefore it is more expensive to insure.
  * **Fuel option.** This vehicle operates on premium gas and has a turbocharged engine, therefore premiums may be higher.
  * **Theft.** The RS3 is not a car that is commonly stolen. In general, Audis have low theft rates, which is excellent news for insurance prices.
  * **Repairs.** The RS3 is a sedan, but it's a high-performance car with sports amenities like a boost gauge and G-meter. The expense of replacing the interior or repairing body damage can be expensive, which boosts the cost of insurance. Audi, on the other hand, provides a comprehensive warranty and maintenance package.
  * **Safety features.** The RS3 is equipped with top-notch safety features, like airbags, electronic stability control and an anti theft alarm system. Insurance is a game of risk, and because these features lower the risk of damage in an accident, they often lead to lower rates.



### **Driver factors and insurance costs**

When crunching the statistics, insurers consider your age, gender, and marital status when calculating your rate. They will also take into account your driving history, as well as any infractions, accidents, or claims. If you haven't had any serious traffic infractions in the last three to five years, you'll most likely get a lower rate.

In addition to your driving record, insurers consider your credit history. In general, the higher your credit score, the lower your premium – however, some companies specialize in insuring drivers with bad credit.

Finally, they evaluate any additional drivers you intend to be in the car with you. Expect a higher premium if teens or anybody with a quick temper will be driving your automobile.

## **Reliability and safety ratings for the Audi RS3**

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) and the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety have not conducted crash tests on the 2018 Audi RS3 (IIHS). It hasn't yet been rated by U.S. News or JD Power because it's only recently arrived in US dealerships.

While it hasn't been formally tested, the rest of the new Audi lineup boasts excellent safety ratings. Almost all of the 2018 Audi models that the NHTSA evaluated received a 5-star safety certification.

The RS3 has great safety features such as power central locking, tire pressure monitoring, and an antilock braking system (ABS) with brake assist and computerized brake pressure distribution. There are also several fronts and side airbags, electronic stability control, and an electromechanical parking brake. Lower anchors and tethers for children (LATCH) in the rear seats will be appreciated by families with little children.

The NHTSA receives two complaints about the vehicle, one of which is regarding the difficulty in maneuvering a booster seat in the rear. Other reviews claim it's a tight fit, particularly for people in the back seats and drivers who have to slide their seats forward to accommodate them.

Meanwhile, Audi recalled certain 2018 RS3 vehicles in 2018 due to poor rear-seat frame welding, and again in 2019 due to missing latch pins for the center rear-seat head restraint and faulty passenger airbag sensors.

The sedan's gasoline tank holds 14.5 gallons. According to Fuel Economy, the average yearly fuel cost for driving the RS3 is $2,350.

This figure incorporates current gasoline costs of $50 per gallon and a combination of 45 percent highway and 55 percent city driving over 15,000 miles. Fuel Economy predicts that you will pay $4,000 more in fuel expenditures over five years than the average new car.
