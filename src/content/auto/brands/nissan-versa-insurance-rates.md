The Nissan Versa insurance cost is $2,292 per year, according to the National Insurance Crime Bureau. This high premium is far more than the typical $1,300 premium that the majority of drivers pay. Theft records, safety ratings, and safety features can all have an impact on the cost of auto insurance for the Versa.

## **About the Nissan Versa**

Nissan introduced the [Versa](<https://www.nissanusa.com/vehicles/cars/versa-sedan.html>) in 2002 and it is now in its second generation. The subcompact automobile is characterized by a cheap starting price, exceptional fuel efficiency, and a roomy cabin. The Nissan Versa was the cheapest vehicle in the United States in 2013.

Insurance companies base the cost of insurance on a Nissan Versa's safety rating, theft rate, replacement cost, and general susceptibility to damage. Insurance costs vary according to your driving history and geographic area. If you're contemplating a Nissan Versa, obtain quotes from various insurance providers to ensure you're paying the lowest rate available. Conduct research to determine whether you are eligible for insurance savings.

## **How safe is the Nissan Versa?**

The [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA)](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2020/NISSAN/VERSA>) tested the 2017 Nissan Versa for safety ratings. Both the frontal collision and rollover crash tests earned four out of five ratings.

Lane departure warning, blind spot monitoring, and electronic stability control are all standard on the Nissan Versa.
