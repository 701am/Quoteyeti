## **How reliable is the Audi RS7?**

While there isn't much information on the RS7's dependability, it is essentially a performance version of Audi's A7, which has a plethora of ratings. The 2018 RS7 has a 4/5 star dependability rating, which is higher than most vehicles in its class, according to U.S. News and J.D. Power. The 2017 model gets a higher rating of 4.5/5 stars.

Because the RS7 has just been on the market since 2013, there haven't been many recalls. However, the A7, on which the RS7 is based, has been the subject of a few recalls. While the A7 recalls may not be immediately relevant to you, it never hurts to check to see if your car was impacted.

  * **2014-2018:** A total of 3,500 Audi RS7s from 2014 to 2018 were recalled due to inaccurate tire information. The label indicated a greater maximum weight than the vehicle can bear, which might raise the danger of an accident if the vehicle is overloaded.
  * **2017:** A small number of 2017 Audi A7s were recalled owing to issues with the passenger, side, and window airbags not properly deploying.
  * **2016:** The 2017 airbag recall also affected certain 2016 models.
  * 2015: Some 2015 Audi A5 vehicles were also affected by the aforementioned airbag recall, however the majority of the issues involved the head curtain airbag.
  * **2012-2013:** A handful of Audi A7 models from 2012 to 2013 were recalled owing to gasoline leaks caused by a faulty fuel injection mechanism. Some vehicles were also recalled owing to a problem with the Passenger Occupant Detection System, which might prevent the passenger-side airbags from deploying in the event of a collision.



The Audi RS7 has practically no common problems. Some RS7 owners have claimed situational troubles on Audi enthusiast sites, but there have been no instances of recurrent issues. Having said that, a small number of Audi A7 owners have reported problems with steering, screeching brakes, and early tire wear, although these concerns may not apply to the RS7.
