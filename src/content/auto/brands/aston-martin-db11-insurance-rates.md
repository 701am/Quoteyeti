## **How do I compare insurance for Aston Martin DB11?**

The [Aston Martin DB11](<https://www.astonmartin.com/en/models/db11>) is a premium sports car with top-of-the-line equipment and styling, which makes insurance extremely costly. While most major insurers cover Aston Martins, you may get more specialized coverage via a high-performance vehicle insurance company.

Whichever choice you pick, each provider takes specific variables into account when determining your coverage costs.

  * **Body type.** Insurance for two-door coupes such as the DB11 is more expensive than for four-door sedans.
  * **Fuel.** The DB11 is powered by a gas engine, which is often less expensive to insure than diesel or hybrid engines.
  * **Theft rates.** The Aston Martin DB11 is equipped with robust anti-theft measures and is not a popular target for theft, which may assist decrease insurance costs.
  * **Maintenance.** Due to the high cost of maintenance and repairs on this car, anticipate paying a higher premium.
  * **Coverage.** With a vehicle this uncommon and costly, you're likely to want collision and comprehensive coverage, which can significantly boost your insurance costs.
  * **Insurer options.** While you may obtain coverage from major carriers, you can save money by working with a company that specializes in luxury vehicle insurance.
  * **Warranty.** Although the DB11 comes with a three-year or unlimited mileage guarantee, you may wish to explore extra coverage, such as an extended warranty or mechanical breakdown coverage.
  * **Safety.** Because this vehicle is manufactured in tiny quantities, it is unlikely to undergo crash testing, which may add to the high cost of insurance.



## **Why is insurance so expensive for the Aston Martin DB11?**

The Aston Martin DB11 is more expensive to insure because insurance companies see two-doors as sports vehicles that are more likely to be involved in an accident. If your automobile is involved in an accident, better-quality parts and a more involved repair process contribute to the higher rates for this UK-built car.

## **How reliable is the Aston Martin DB11?**

Although Aston Martins are designed with safety in mind, they have not been crash-tested because of their limited availability.

  * **Safety.** Due to the limited production of about 1,000 Aston Martin DB11s, the vehicle has not been subjected to [NHTSA or IIHS](<https://www.carindigo.com/aston-martin/db11/safety>) crash testing. It does, however, come standard with several airbags and driver aid systems.
  * **Reliability.** Although the DB11 lacks official dependability ratings, other from minor paint defects and a few unusual clutch difficulties, there do not appear to be many frequent problems with this car..
  * **Recalls.** Certain 2017–2018 DB11 Coupe vehicles were recalled owing to an unexpected deployment of the front airbags caused by a defective steering column. Additionally, a recall was issued for some 2017 vehicles due to an improperly calibrated tire pressure monitoring system.
