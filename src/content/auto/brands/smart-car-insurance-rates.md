## **How a Smart Car affects the cost of insurance**

The high [safety ratings](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/>) and inexpensive price of a Smart car assist to keep insurance rates down. It is a low-cost automobile to fix since components are inexpensive and there isn't much to repair, therefore insurance premiums will be below.

Other factors that influence insurance costs include the model, mileage, and trim package. Because convertibles are a greater risk body style, Smart Cars' Cabrio convertible versions will be more costly. Ensuring the 2-door Coupe vehicles will be somewhat less expensive.

Your charges will also be affected by the trim level. Smart Cars are available in three trim levels: Pure, Passion, and Prime. A higher MSRP will result in higher insurance rates, however, there are additional safety features available for the Passion and 

Prime versions that will result in safety savings. In addition, if you qualify for a hybrid or electric car discount, the electric [Smart Car](<https://www.smartusa.com/>) will generally be less expensive to insure.
