## **About the Ram 2500**

Since 1994, the [Ram 2500](<https://www.ramtrucks.com/ram-2500.html>) has been in production and is now in its fourth generation. The pickup truck is renowned for its luxurious cabin, powerful turbo diesel engine, and towing capacity of more than 17,000 pounds. The heavy duty pickup truck is available in two-wheel drive or four-wheel drive configurations and is available in a range of designs and trim levels.

## **How safe is the Ram 2500?**

The [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2018/RAM/2500/PU%25252FCC/4WD>) awarded the 2017 Ram 2500 a 4-Star Safety Rating (NHTSA). The NHTSA awarded the side crash test five stars, the best attainable grade. The government agency awarded four stars for the frontal barrier crash test and the [rollover crash test](<https://cars.usnews.com/cars-trucks/ram/1500>).

A rear camera, blind spot monitoring, and stability control are standard on the Ram 2500 model.
