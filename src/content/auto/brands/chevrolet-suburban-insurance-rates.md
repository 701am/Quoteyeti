Car insurance costs on average $164.86 per month — or $1,978.32 per year — for a 2021 Chevrolet Suburban. However, your real prices will vary depending on your driving history, car model, and region.

## **About the Chevrolet Suburban**

Chevrolet introduced the Suburban in 1935. The big SUV is renowned for its lavish interior space and ample luggage space. In 2016, approximately 60,000 units were sold in the United States. 

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration granted the newest model a 4-Star Overall Safety Rating.

  * Anti-lock brakes
  * Stability control
  * Airbags
  * Seatbelt pretensioners
  * Anti theft system
