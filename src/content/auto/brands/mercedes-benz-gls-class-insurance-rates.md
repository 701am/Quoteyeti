## **Why is Mercedes-Benz GLS-Class insurance cost so expensive?**

The Mercedes-Benz GLS-Class is more expensive to insure since it is a high-priced vehicle that costs more to repair than the ordinary automobile. Furthermore, this vehicle has not been assessed by the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety, resulting in increased insurance prices.

## **About the Mercedes-Benz GLS-Class**

The Mercedes-Benz GLS-Class has been on the market since 2017, replacing the company's GL-Class. The big luxury SUV is well-known for its powerful engine options, smooth ride, and ample seating. The majority of the vehicles are built in Alabama.
