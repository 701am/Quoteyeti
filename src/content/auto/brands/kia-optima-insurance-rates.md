## **How safe is the Kia Optima?**

The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety ([IIHS](<http://iihs.org>)) selected the Kia Optima as a Top Safety Pick vehicle. The IIHS evaluated all the crash tests and gave them all a good grade, which is the best attainable. [NHTSA](<http://nhtsa.gov>) gave the Kia Optima five stars in overall vehicle safety and scored the highest in all crash testing.

The 2012 list of the 20 most stolen new cars by [Forbes Magazine](<https://www.forbes.com/?sh=320b33d92254>) ranks the Kia Optima at number 18. The number of thefts on the Kia Optima is about equal to 2.39 per 1,000 units.
