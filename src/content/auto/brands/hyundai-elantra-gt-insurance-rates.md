## **How safe is the Hyundai Elantra GT?**

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) has tested the 2017 Hyundai Elantra GT for safety ratings. The NHTSA granted the newest model a 4-Star Overall Safety Rating. The NHTSA gave the Hyundai Elantra GT four out of five ratings in the rollover and frontal crash tests.

The most recent model has an optional rearview camera and Blue Link, Hyundai's emergency system that provides help and theft recovery.
