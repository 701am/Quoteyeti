## **About the Mitsubishi Mirage**

Since 1978, the [Mitsubishi Mirage](<https://www.mitsubishicars.com/mirage/2021>) has been produced in six generations. The subcompact automobile is well-known for its lengthy warranty and outstanding fuel efficiency. 

The United States saw more than 22,000 sales in 2016. Mitsubishi Mirage hatchbacks and sedans are both available.

## **How safe is the Mitsubishi Mirage?**

The Mitsubishi Mirage has a 4-Star Overall Safety Rating from the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration ([NHTSA](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2020/MITSUBISHI/MIRAGE/5%252520HB/FWD>)). The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration awarded four stars out of five for all crash testing conducted. The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety ([IIHS](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/mitsubishi/mirage-4-door-hatchback/2021>)) gave the small overlap front test a mediocre rating. All other IIHS crash testing resulted in an excellent rating, the best attainable.

The Mitsubishi Mirage's newest model is equipped with anti-lock brakes, seat-mounted side airbags, and electronic stability control.
