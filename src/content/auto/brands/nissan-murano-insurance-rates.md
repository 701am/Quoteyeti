## **About the Nissan Murano**

The [Nissan Murano](<https://www.nissanusa.com/vehicles/crossovers-suvs/murano.html>) has been on the market since 2003 and is presently in its third generation of production. The midsize SUV is renowned for its spacious interior and outstanding fuel economy, especially for a V6 engine of its size. When Ward's 10 Best Interiors held their annual contest in 2015, the Nissan Murano was voted the winner.

## **How safe is the Nissan Murano?**

The [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2021/NISSAN/MURANO>) awarded the 2017 Nissan Murano a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating. This is the highest rating available (NHTSA). The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration awarded the side impact test five stars, the best possible grade. In addition, the rollover crash test and the frontal crash test received four stars from the government agency. The Nissan Murano was also given a crashworthiness rating by the [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS)](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/nissan/murano-4-door-suv/2021>) as well. The IIHS declared the vehicle a Top Safety Pick model, and it got the best possible grade in all [crash tests](<https://www.jdpower.com/cars/2020/nissan/murano/awd-sl>) conducted on it.

Among the features of the Nissan Murano are lane departure warning, blind spot monitoring, and electronic stability control (ESC).
