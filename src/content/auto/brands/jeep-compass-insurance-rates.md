## **About the Jeep Compass**

The [Jeep Compass](<https://www.jeep.com/compass.html>) has been on the market since 2007 and is now in its second generation. The Jeep Compass was one of the earliest crossover SUVs produced by the firm. 

The small SUV is recognized for its strong off-road four-wheel-drive system, premium interior, and spacious cabin. There were approximately 94,000 sales in the United States in 2016. The Jeep Compass had its best year in terms of sales since its debut.

## **How safe is the Jeep Compass?**

The newest Jeep Compass model has been tested for safety ratings by the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration ([NHTSA](<http://nhtsa.gov>)) and the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS). The Jeep Compass received a 4-Star Overall Safety Rating from the NHTSA. The frontal crash test received three out of five stars for safety, while the rollover crash test received four stars. Only the rear crash protection was assessed by the [IIHS](<http://iihs.org>), and it received the maximum possible score.

Only 15 Jeep Compass vehicles were stolen in 2013. Based on the number of automobiles produced that year, that equates to a 0.71 percent theft rate.
