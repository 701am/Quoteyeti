## **What factors influence the cost of Subaru WRX auto insurance?**

Insurance companies examine several factors while determining your insurance quote. While driver and location-specific factors may influence your chances of being involved in an accident and the cost of repairs, the automobile you pick has a considerably greater impact. The insurance rates for the WRX are competitive with those of comparable vehicles.

  * **Body type**. The Subaru WRX is a sedan designed for commuting and other low-risk driving, which should help keep insurance rates down.
  * **Type of car**. While the WRX is a sedan, it has a powerful engine and a sports tune that may allow for more daring driving. This might lead to an increase in the cost of coverage.
  * **Theft**. The WRX is a sedan, which is the most often stolen kind of vehicle. Insurance rates may rise as a result of this.
  * **Repairs**. The WRX has rather high repair costs, which may quickly add up if you misuse your vehicle. With this in mind, insurance premiums may be higher than usual.
  * **Safety ratings**. The Subaru WRX has near-perfect crashworthiness ratings, which could lead to lower total insurance costs.
  * **Fuel type**. In comparison to diesel and hybrid vehicles, gasoline-powered vehicles are less expensive to insure. Rates may be cheaper because the WRX is a gas-powered vehicle.
  * **Trim & packages**. If you choose trims or options that increase performance or overall value, your premiums may be hiked. Nonetheless, improvements to visibility and safety may qualify for vehicle insurance reductions.
