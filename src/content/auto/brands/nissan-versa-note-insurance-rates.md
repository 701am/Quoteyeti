## **How safe is the Nissan Versa Note?**

The [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA)](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2019/NISSAN/VERSA%252520NOTE/5%252520HB/FWD>) has tested the 2017 Nissan Versa Note for safety ratings. The government agency awarded the rollover crash test four out of five stars. Three stars were awarded for the frontal crash test's safety. The [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/nissan/versa-note-4-door-hatchback/2019>) bestowed the highest possible safety rating on the moderate overlap front crash test.

Lane departure warning, blind spot monitoring, and electronic stability control are all standard on the Nissan Versa Note.
