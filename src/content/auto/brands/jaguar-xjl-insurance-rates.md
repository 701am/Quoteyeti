The Jaguar XJL is a spacious sedan with reasonable insurance rates for a premium vehicle. The XJ series, on the other hand, isn't recognized for its dependability. The average insurance premium for this vehicle is $110 per month or $1,320 per year. This cost is roughly $100 less than the national average of $1,426 per year.

## **How do I compare insurance for the XJL?**

The[ XJL](<https://www.jaguarusa.com/all-models/xj/index.html>) is a continuation of Jaguar's XJ series, with a bit of extra space in the rear. Its larger size and the higher price tag may boost your premium. To cover repair costs, the XJL comes with a comprehensive warranty, maintenance plan, and roadside support.

  * **Body type.** This premium sedan has a larger wheelbase and a more spacious backseat than the regular XJ. The larger size may result in a higher premium.
  * **Price tag.** This vehicle has a high sticker price of about $76,000 MSRP. However, for a premium performance car, this is still reasonable.
  * **Theft.** This vehicle has a low number of theft complaints, especially when compared to the regular XJ.
  * **Fuel.** This car has a mediocre fuel economy. It gets 21 mpg on the interstate and in the city.
  * **Maintenance.** On all new XJ models, Jaguar offers a comprehensive maintenance plan as part of the warranty.
  * **Coverage.** To save money on your premium, you might pick basic coverage. Consider purchasing additional coverage to protect yourself in the event of an accident. Comprehensive, loan/lease gap, uninsured/underinsured motorist, and collision coverage with a high limit may be desirable.
  * **Warranty.** This vehicle is covered by a 5-year, 60,000-mile warranty, which includes periodic maintenance and roadside assistance.

## **How reliable is the Jaguar XJL?**

The XJ series does not have good dependability ratings and no crash testing safety statistics. The series has also had several recalls, implying that the maker hasn't yet mastered production.

  * **Safety.** The XJL lacks crash testing data from reputable safety groups. This might be because the automobile sells at a lesser pace than comparable lower-priced family sedans.
  * **Reliability.**[J.D. Power](<http://jdpower.com>) gives the XJL series a projected dependability rating of 2 out of 5 in general.
  * **Recalls.** Many recalls have been issued for the XJL series, covering issues with software, airbags, windshield wipers, rear-wheel alignment, and other components.



## **Bottom line**

The [Jaguar XJL](<https://www.jaguarusa.com/all-models/xj/index.html>) comes with a long warranty and few theft complaints. Despite its position as a premium vehicle, the automobile generally has cheap insurance rates. However, XJs are not well-known for their dependability.
