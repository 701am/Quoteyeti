## **How safe is the Lexus CT?**

The Lexus CT 2017 was designated the Top Safety Pick Car by the [Highway Safety Institute (IIHS)](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/lexus/ct-200h-4-door-hatchback/2017>). The IIHS gave all crash tests excellent grades, the best possible rating.

The Lexus CT's newest model features overhead airbags, lateral airbags and antilock brakes.

In 2013, according to the [NHTSA](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2015/LEXUS/CT200H>), 4 Lexus CT thieves were documented.
