## **Jeep vehicle's reliability**

The Renegade is a newcomer to the team. Because the Renegade model was launched in 2015, there is less historical data available for this car. The Renegade was also a blank canvas for Jeep to begin repairing its image for poor reliability, but Jeep did not fully utilize it. The Renegade's dependability rating is just somewhat greater than that of comparable vehicles with a longer track record.

It's an average automobile, with a predicted reliability rating of 2.5/5 from [J.D. Power](<https://www.jdpower.com/cars/jeep>) for the current model. The 2018 Renegade has received the highest dependability complaints. This is quite strange, considering the 2017 Jeep Renegade was widely regarded as the most dependable model. The up and down trend of the Renegade's dependability indicates that future versions may suffer from a slew of reliability concerns.
