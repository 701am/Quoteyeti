A Chrysler Pacifica's average insurance cost is $132 per month or $1,584 per year. Your exact cost may vary based on your driving record, the number of miles you travel in a year, and other variables.

### **About the Chrysler Pacifica**

The [Chrysler Pacifica](<https://www.chrysler.com/pacifica/hybrid.html>) has been on the market since 2004. The minivan is well-known for its quick acceleration, nimble handling, and high fuel efficiency. The 2017 model comes with a plug-in hybrid option.

The popular Chrysler Town & Country vehicle was retired in 2016 and was replaced in Chrysler's range by the Pacifica. Car insurance is somewhat more expensive for the Pacifica, with typical monthly premiums about $132 compared to $111 for a 2016 Town & Country.

  * Anti-lock brakes
  * Stability control
  * Airbags
  * Seatbelt pretensioners
  * Anti theft system



## **How safe is the Chrysler Pacifica?**

The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS) recognized the 2017 Chrysler Pacifica as a Top Safety Pick, and the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) awarded it a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating. The NHTSA gave the Chrysler Pacifica four out of five ratings in the rollover crash test. The 2017 model features excellent frontal collision prevention, which is the highest grade, according to the IIHS, with optional equipment.

To protect passengers in the event of a collision, the Chrysler Pacifica includes side airbags and a driver knee airbag. The most recent model also has anti-lock brakes and a rollover sensor to increase passenger safety.
