## **About the Jeep Patriot**

The Jeep Patriot has been on the market since 2007, and the model will be phased out in 2017 when the inventory is depleted. The Jeep Patriot will be replaced by the Jeep Compass of the second generation. The small SUV is recognized for its low pricing, outstanding off-road capabilities, four-wheel drive, and robust interior. From its introduction until April 2017, the Jeep Patriot sold over 876,000 units in the North American market.

## **How safe is the Jeep Patriot?**

The newest Jeep Patriot model has been tested for safety ratings by the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) and the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS). The NHTSA awarded the Jeep Patriot a 3-Star Overall Safety Rating. The frontal crash test and the rollover crash test both received three stars out of five. The side crash test received the maximum possible rating of five stars. The IIHS gave the small overlap front crash test a bad grade and the other crash tests a good rating, the best attainable.

In 2013, 43 Jeep Patriot vehicles were stolen. Based on the number of Jeep Patriot models produced that year, the theft rate is 1.21 percent.
