### **About the Fiat 500**

Since 1957 the [Fiat 500](<https://www.fiatusa.com/500.html>) has been around and the model was first introduced in the US in 2010. The European Car of the year in 2008 was selected as the Fiat 500. The automobile is noted for its tiny size and powerful engine, suited for city situations.

## **How safe is the Fiat 500?**

The 2017 Fiat 500 model has been given a National Highway Traffic Safety Authorities (NHTSA) 4-star Overall Safety Rating for safety ratings. For the NHTSA rollover crash test and front crash test, Dodge Journey has rated four stars out of five. The Dodge Journey earned a low grade for a small, overlapping front collision test according to the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS).

More than 98 Fiat 500 vehicles, according to the NHTSA, were stolen from 2012 to 2013 in the United States.
