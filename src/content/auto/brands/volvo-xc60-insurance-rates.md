The Volvo XC60, which is now in its second generation, has been around since 2008. The small luxury SUV is famous for its fuel economy, its powerful engines and its greatest luggage area. More than 20,000 sales were made in the USA in 2016.

## **How safe is the Volvo XC60?**

The[ National Road Traffic Safety Administration](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2021/VOLVO/XC60%252520T6/SUV/AWD>) gave the 2017 Volvo XC60 a 5-Star overall security rating (NHTSA). A four-star safety grade of five has been given for the roll-over crash test. All other NHTSA crash tests were evaluated at the maximum possible five-star safety rating. The Volvo XC60 has also been designated the Top Safety Pick+ vehicle by the [Highway Insurance Institute (IIHS)](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/top-safety-picks/2021/all/volvo>). The highest [safety rating](<https://www.jdpower.com/cars/2021/volvo/xc60/t6-awd-momentum>) possible has been granted to all crash testing undertaken by the IIHS.

The Volvo XC60 has blind spot surveillance, adaptive control and braking support.
