## **About the Lexus RX**

Lexus introduced the RX in 1998 and it is now in its fourth iteration. The midsize luxury SUV is recognized for its spacious cabin, competitive starting price, and extensive list of standard amenities. Lexus's best-selling hybrid vehicle is the RX. From 2005, when Lexus began offering the RX hybrid, through March 2016, the hybrid model sold over 335,000 times globally.

## **How safe is the Lexus RX?**

The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety designated the 2017 Lexus RX a Top Safety Pick+ vehicle ([IIHS](<http://iihs.org>)). The IIHS gave all crash tests a good rating, the best attainable. Additionally, the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration granted the Lexus RX a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating (NHTSA). The [NHTSA](<http://nhtsa.gov>) awarded the side impact test five stars, the best possible grade. Additionally, the government agency granted the vehicle a four-star grade in the frontal collision and rollover tests.

The Lexus RX's most recent model is equipped with blind spot monitoring, side impact airbags, and antilock brakes.

According to the [NHTSA](<http://nhtsa.gov>), the Lexus RX was stolen 56 times in 2013.
