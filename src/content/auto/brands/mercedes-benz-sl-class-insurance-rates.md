The average monthly rates for a 2020 [Mercedes-Benz SL-Class](<https://www.mbusa.com/en/vehicles/class/sl/roadster>) insurance cost is $356.99, or $4,283.99 per year. Your real expenses, however, are determined by your driving history, car model, location, and other considerations.

## **Why is Mercedes-Benz SL-Class insurance cost so expensive?**

The Mercedes-Benz SL-Class is more expensive to insure since it is a sports automobile recognized for its sporty handling, which indicates a high risk to insurance companies. Furthermore, the [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2006/MERCEDES-BENZ/SL-CLASS>) has not formally evaluated this car for safety, adding to the risk.

## **About the Mercedes-Benz SL-Class**

The Mercedes-Benz SL-Class has been in production since 1954, although until 1994 it was known as the SL Roadster. The premium sports vehicle is well-known for its agile driving, roomy cabin, and powerful engine options.
