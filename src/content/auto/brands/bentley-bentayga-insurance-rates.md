According to our study, the average monthly cost for the [Bentley Bentayga](<https://www.bentleymotors.com/en/models/bentayga/bentayga-hybrid.html>) insurance cost is about $301 — or $3,612 a year. However, not every insurance provider is willing to take on such a high-priced vehicle, so your exact prices and selections may be limited.

## **How to compare Bentley Bentayga insurance rates?**

Consider the following criteria when comparing insurance prices for the Bentley Bentayga:

  * **Price**. Bentaygas aren't inexpensive to buy, and they're much more expensive to fix. If you need to submit a claim in the future, skimping on insurance coverage may not be in your best interests.
  * **Deductible**. Most luxury automobile owners like to avoid having to pay a large deductible. Choose a moderate or low deductible unless you know you'll be able to pay for repairs before insurance kicks in.
  * **Coverage**. You'll almost certainly want coverage that goes above and beyond the state's minimum insurance requirements. Because repairs might be expensive, consider adding comprehensive and collision coverage to your insurance. These coverage choices may be necessary if you are leasing or financing your Bentley.
  * **Restrictions**. Because not every insurance provider will cover your Bentayga, your alternatives may be restricted.
  * **Manufacturer offers**. Bentley provides a three-year, unlimited-mile roadside assistance package on all new vehicles, so you don't need to worry about adding this option to your policy if you're still insured by Bentley.



## **What factors affect car insurance for the Bentley Bentayga?**

Bentley claims that the Bentayga is the fastest SUV ever built, with peak speeds of 187 mph. Expect insurance prices to rise at this rate. Insuring a Bentayga, on the other hand, is likely to be less expensive than insuring a luxury sports vehicle like the Continental, Mulsanne, or Flying Spur, because these cars can drive quicker and are generally deemed high risk by insurance providers.

Your Bentayga may be easier to insure than, say, a Mulsanne, but while SUVs are deemed less risky than sports cars, you'll still need to shop around for the most accurate prices. Some insurance providers, such as Geico, Progressive, and Travelers, will not insure a Bentley, SUV or not. Others, such as Allstate, may cover your Bentley, but you won't be able to receive an online quotation. However, Erie and Farmers should be able to provide you with a ballpark number online.

## **Driver factors and insurance costs**

Aside from the type and model of your car, driver variables such as your age, gender, marital status, location, and credit score all have an impact on your insurance costs. If you reside in a city with high crime rates, such as Los Angeles, you may pay a higher premium than a similar driver who lives in a smaller rural town with low theft rates.

## **Reliability and safety ratings for the Bentley Bentayga**

Because there aren't very many Bentaygas on the road, official safety and dependability ratings aren't available. Furthermore, there aren't any comparable super-fast SUVs to compare it to just yet – though [Rolls-Royce](<https://www.rolls-roycemotorcars.com/>) and [Lamborghini](<https://www.lamborghini.com/>) are expected to release similar vehicles shortly.  
[Bentleys](<https://www.bentleymotors.com/en.html>) do have a reputation for being unreliable, especially if you buy a secondhand vehicle rather than a new one. However, most luxury vehicle owners do not use their vehicles on a daily basis, which may save you money on repairs and basic maintenance.

### **The Bentley Bentayga series maintenance**

The Bentayga, like other Bentleys, may be expensive to maintain and repair. The typical repair cost for this car is $880, plus $500 for oil changes and an additional $1,400 to $2,500 each year for other normal maintenance. Because you'll almost certainly need to send your Bentayga to a Bentley dealership or specialized mechanic for repairs and maintenance, you may have to wait a while for your car to be returned, and you'll almost certainly spend more than you would at a regular repair shop.

### **When did the Bentayga debut?**

The Bentley Bentayga debuted in 2016 as the brand's first SUV. This vehicle, which weighs more than a large pickup truck, is also the world's fastest SUV to date, and there's nothing else like it on the road right now. The [Mulliner](<https://www.bentleymotors.com/en/models/continental/continental-gt-mulliner.html>), an opulent Bentayga variant launched in 2017, may cost even more to insure and repair. Except for an extra aesthetic package with black wheels and additional chrome, and another package with a third row of seats, the 2018 models haven't altered much.
