## **How safe is the Mazda 3?**

The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety designated the 2017 Mazda 3 a Top Safety Pick+ vehicle ([IIHS](<http://iihs.org>)). All crash tests received excellent ratings, the best possible rating, from the IIHS. The Mazda 3 received a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating from the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration ([NHTSA](<http://nhtsa.gov>)). The NHTSA rated the frontal crash test and the side impact test five stars, the highest grade. The rollover test received a four-star grade from the government body.

The most recent Mazda 3 model has blind spot monitoring with rear cross traffic alert, an automated emergency braking system, and anti-lock brakes.
