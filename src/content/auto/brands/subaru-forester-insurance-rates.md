[Subaru](<https://www.subaru.com/index.html>) introduced the Forester in 1997 and it is now in its fourth generation. The small SUV is renowned for its low starting price, ample luggage space, and roomy seating. Motor Trend awarded the Subaru Forester the SUV of the Year in 2014.

## **How safe is the Subaru Forester?**

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration awarded the 2017 Subaru Forester a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating ([NHTSA](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2021/SUBARU/FORESTER/SUV/AWD>)). The NHTSA awarded the side crash test five stars, the best attainable grade. Additionally, the government agency gave the vehicle a four-star safety grade in the frontal collision and rollover crash tests. Subaru Forester was rated a Top Safety Pick+ vehicle by the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety ([IIHS](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/subaru/forester-4-door-suv/2021>)). All IIHS crash tests earned an excellent rating, the best attainable.

Anti-lock brakes, blind-spot monitoring, and stability control are standard on the Subaru Forester model.
