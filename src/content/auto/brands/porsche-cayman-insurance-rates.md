Insuring a [Porsche Cayman](<https://www.porsche.com/international/models/718/718-models/718-cayman/>) costs an average of $108 per month — or $1,296 per year — according to the [National Insurance Crime Bureau](<https://www.nicb.org/>). Using a base price of $53,900 as a starting point, the yearly insurance cost–to–base automobile price ratio is a manageable 2.4 percent.

## **About the Porsche Cayman**

The Porsche Cayman is a sports car that has been on the market since 2006. Known for its superb handling, powerful engine options, and luxurious interior, the premium sports vehicle is a popular choice among celebrities. The Porsche Cayman has been renamed the Porsche 718 Cayman for the 2017 model year, according to the manufacturer.

## **Porsche Cayman money-saving safety features**

Having these safety features on your Porsche Cayman might result in a reduction in the amount you pay for automobile liability insurance each year.

  * Anti-lock brakes
  * Stability control
  * Airbags
  * Seatbelt pretensioners
  * Anti theft system



## **How safe is the Porsche Cayman?**

The [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety](<http://iihs.org>) and the [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<http://nhtsa.gov>) have not rated the 2017 Porsche Cayman for safety.

Adaptive cruise control, blind spot monitoring, and a collision mitigation system are standard on the Porsche Cayman.
