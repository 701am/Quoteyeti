## **How reliable is the Audi A3?**

According to J.D. Power, the 2017 Audi A3 earned an average dependability rating of 3/5 stars. Various customer evaluations on other websites, however, score it 4.5/5 stars or above, with one source giving it a 95.5 percent dependability rating based on an extensive driver poll.

Since the A3's redesign in 2015, there have been a number of recalls. Here are a few recent examples:

  * **2017-2018.** A total of 8,400 2017-2018 Audi A3 cabriolets and sedans were recalled owing to an engine stalling issue. According to Audi, the engine control unit software may misunderstand the flywheel spin at engine starts as a "knocking" condition, and the modified air/fuel mixture may result in a stall, increasing accident risk.
  * **2017.** A small number of 2017 vehicles were recalled owing to an issue in which the passenger frontal airbag may deploy incorrectly, possibly injuring the person when the airbag deployed with greater force than planned.
  * **2016.** A tiny proportion of 2016 Audi A3s were recalled due to problems with the engine cooling system, fuel systems, and side marker lights.
  * **2015.** Fewer than 1,000 Audi A3s from 2015 were recalled due to poorly sewn front seats, which might prevent side airbags from deploying in the case of an accident.



According to numerous internet sites and forums, the 2015 Audi A3 has the greatest difficulties and is regarded as the worst model year due to high repair prices and frequency of troubles. However, this might be due to the fact that 2015 was the first model year following a substantial redesign. Excessive oil usage, transmission alerts, and unexpected power outages are all common concerns with the 2015 A3. Owners of 2016 vehicles have reported jerking and hesitancy during acceleration, as well as water seeping into the car.
