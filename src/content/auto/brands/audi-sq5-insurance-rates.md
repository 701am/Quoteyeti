## **How reliable is the Audi SQ5?**

Because the SQ5 has only been in production for a few years, no official dependability ratings are available at the time of writing. However, according to [U.S. News](<https://cars.usnews.com/cars-trucks/audi/q5/2021#:~:text=The%20National%20Highway%20Traffic%20Safety,stars%20in%20the%20rollover%20test.>) and [JD Power](<https://www.jdpower.com/automotive-news/2021-audi-q5-q5-sportback-earn-top-safety-ratings-from-iihs?make=Audi&model=Q5>), the Q5 receives a 3/5 star rating, which is about average for vehicles in its class.

The SQ5 has only been in production since 2013, and there have been two recalls as of August 2021. Audi recalled a limited number of 2014-2015 SQ5 cars built between May 22, 2013, and April 14, 2015, due to a power steering system issue that might cause the vehicle to shut down in cold conditions. In addition, certain 2018-2019 SQ5 vehicles were recalled in 2019 due to body, seat, and rear axle assembly problems.

The Audi SQ5 has practically no frequent issues, however this might be owing to its short manufacturing duration. Having said that, many Audi vehicles suffer from carbon buildup over time, which can result in lower power or mileage. This issue, however, is typically avoidable with periodic service visits.

The SQ5 is designed to tackle a wide range of terrain. This vehicle is meant to be comfortable and reliable, whether traveling on the highway or traversing gravel roads up to the cottage. Here's a rundown of what to expect in terms of oil changes, maintenance schedules, mileage, and other details.

  * Audi suggests changing the oil every 10,000 miles or 12 months, whichever comes first. If you drive a lot, you may want to get it replaced more regularly.
  * An oil change for a SQ5 may cost between $100 and $250. The exact cost is determined by where you get your vehicle serviced and how long it has been since the previous oil change.
  * According to the 2017 Audi maintenance plan, your SQ5 should be taken in for minor repair at 10,000 miles and then every 20,000 miles thereafter. Standard maintenance should be performed after the first 20,000 miles and then every 20,000 miles after that.
  * The 2018 Audi SQ5 gets 21 miles per gallon on average, with 19 in the city and 24 on the interstate.
  * The 2017 Audi SQ5 is covered by a four-year/50,000-mile warranty as well as four years of 24-hour roadside support.
