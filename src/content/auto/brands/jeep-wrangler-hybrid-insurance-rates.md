## **How do I compare insurance for the Wrangler Hybrid?**

Insurance providers look at several different aspects when calculating your insurance premiums, including vehicle-specific characteristics. Here are some of these:

  * **Body type**. The Wrangler Hybrid's tough, off-road body style is maintained. It's possible that you'll have to pay more for insurance, since you engage in riskier activities, even if you don't drive your vehicle on the road.
  * **Fuel**. A plug-in hybrid that is intended to run on all electric power for around 20 kilometers is currently under development. The total fuel economy should be just over 22 mpg thanks to the better mileage when driving on petrol. Because of the cost of insuring hybrids, they are slightly more expensive.
  * **Theft rates**. Many stolen Jeeps might make insurance on the Wrangler Hybrid more expensive. The Hybrid is a spinoff model of the Wrangler, however it will certainly be negatively affected by the higher insurance theft rates associated with the Wrangler.
  * **Maintenance**. The average annual maintenance cost of a Jeep Wrangler is about $694, as reported by Repair Pal's website. A percentage of your cost will depend on how much off-roading you undertake.
  * **Coverage**. Collision and comprehensive coverage should definitely be included on the new Wrangler. If you'll be frequenting rugged terrain, you should ensure your car is protected by both collision and comprehensive coverage.
  * **Warranty**. Both the three-year, 36,000-mile warranty and the five-year, 60,000-mile warranty come with a five-year, 60,000-mile powertrain warranty. Even though the extended warranty includes limitless roadside help, you should choose to use your insurance's roadside assistance if you don't have the extended warranty.
  * **Manufacturer offer**. When it comes to older models, discounts, such as an advertised 3.75% APR for 60 months, or cash back incentives, are often available.



### **Why is Jeep Wrangler Hybrid insurance cost so expensive?**

Because of a combination of reasons, insurance for the regular and hybrid Wranglers is more than average: frequent and expensive repairs, increased theft risk, and greater off-road driving potential. Hybrids are more expensive to insure, which is why you should expect insurance premiums for the hybrid version of this popular SUV to be a bit more.
