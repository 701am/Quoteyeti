## **How reliable is the Audi Q3?**

According to U.S. News and J.D. Power, the 2018 Audi Q3 has a dependability rating of 3/5 stars, which is about average for vehicles in its class. Customer evaluations of this car appear to be unanimous, with many owners stating they are pleased with their purchase.

Despite the fact that the Audi Q3 has only been on the market since 2015, there have been a number of recalls affecting various model years.

  * **2016-2017:** Almost 25,000 2016 and 2017 Audi Q3 cars were recalled due to a problem that prevented the brake lights from functioning during emergency braking if the parking brake was engaged. Some vehicles also experienced difficulties where the side marker lights did not activate when the parking lights or headlights were turned on.
  * **2015:** The recall for side marker lights also affected certain 2015 cars. Furthermore, Audi recalled a handful of 2015 Q3s because the sunroof continued to close after the car was turned off.



The Audi Q3 has few typical problems. However, it has just been in production since 2015, so common faults may arise with greater miles. Having said that, the most prevalent complaint is the windshield shattering on the 2016 model, which may cost around $1,350 to fix.

## **Audi Q3 maintenance**

The Q3 is Audi's entry-level crossover SUV, but don't let its low price fool you. The Q3 is loyal to the Audi name, with a beautiful appearance and a slew of amenities that make driving a pleasure. However, just like any other vehicle, it is critical to follow a regular service plan in order to keep the Q3 running at peak performance. Here's what you can anticipate from an Audi Q3 in terms of oil changes, maintenance, and mileage.

  * Audi suggests changing the oil every 10,000 miles or 12 months, whichever comes first. Oil changes may range from $50 to $150, depending on how long it has been since your previous oil change and where you take it for repair.
  * According to the 2017 Audi maintenance plan, your Q3 should be taken in for minor repair at 10,000 miles and then every 20,000 miles thereafter. Standard maintenance should be performed after the first 20,000 miles and then every 20,000 miles after that.
  * The 2017 Audi Q3 2.0 gets 23 miles per gallon on average, with 20 in the city and 28 on the interstate.
  * The 2017 Audi Q3 2.0 Quattro also achieves 23 miles per gallon on the interstate and 20 in the city.
  * Audi recommends owners to take their Q3 to a dealer for repair, but it will not void your warranty if you do so. If your car is subject to a recall, you should take it to an authorized Audi dealer for repairs.
  * The 2017 Audi Q3 comes with a four-year/50,000-mile warranty and four years of 24/7 roadside assistance.
