## **About the Nissan Altima**

The Nissan Altima has been on the market since 1992 and is now in its fifth generation. The midsize automobile is recognized for its outstanding fuel efficiency and roomy seats. The Altima is one of Nissan's best-selling vehicles. In 2016, there were approximately 307,000 sales in the United States.

## **How safe is the Nissan Altima?**

The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety named the 2017 Nissan Altima a Top Safety Pick+ vehicle (IIHS). The IIHS received a good grade, the best attainable, for all crash tests undertaken. The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration also awarded the Nissan Altima a 5-Star Overall Safety Rating ([NHTSA](<https://nhtsa.com>)). The NHTSA awarded the automobile five stars, the highest grade, for both the frontal and side impact tests. The rollover crash test was rated four out of five stars.

The Nissan Altima was the seventh most stolen car in 2014, according to the National Insurance Crime Bureau (NCIB). That year, over 9,000 models were reported stolen.
