## **How do I compare insurance for the Defender?**

Because of its off-road capabilities, the cost of insurance for the Defender will be slightly higher. But it will be cheaper to own as an SUV, even though SUVs are typically more expensive than equivalent pickups. 

In addition to these criteria, other elements influence the Defender's charges.

  * **Body type.** A tiny or medium SUV developed for off-roading, the Defender. Sedans and pickup trucks are cheaper to insure, while SUVs usually enjoy lower insurance rates than sedans or pickup trucks. Off-road vehicles, however, are more expensive even if you don't venture off-road.
  * **Fuel.** At the time of writing, no official mpg figures have been revealed, although it appears that the Defender may get up to 28 mpg. Competitor off-road vehicles would be much more expensive. The sole downside is that electric motors and battery parts necessitate greater repair costs, resulting in higher rates.
  * **Theft rates.** About 6% of the cars that are stolen in the US are Land Rovers. Because no official rates on this Defender model are available for a few years, the rate decrease will be very severe.
  * **Maintenance.** Due to the regular maintenance and pricey parts, Land Rovers are one of the most expensive cars to maintain. Although it's not very likely to compensate for the increased insurance costs, the Defender's off-road capability should enhance its reliability.
  * **Coverage.** The standard model starts at $49,900, but the customization options let you select coverage that will cost over $100,000, making this the most-covered vehicle of its kind. In addition to collision and comprehensive coverage, it offers limitations for excessive liability. Additionally, ensure that your uninsured motorist property damage policy limits are appropriate.
  * **Warranty.** Land Rover has made a baseline four-year, 50,000-mile warranty standard on all of its new cars, and gives customers the option to extend it for an additional five years. With this warranty, you can delay purchasing a supplemental vehicle insurance policy until your warranty expires.
  * **Manufacturer offer.** Several special deals on the Defender, such as low APRs and cashback offers, are expected to be offered. You may discover the most favorable price by contacting your local dealership.
