The average insurance premium for a Hyundai Ioniq is $227 per month, or $2,724 per year, which is roughly $1,000 more than the national average. The yearly insurance cost–to–base car price ratio is 5.8 percent with a base price of $22,200, making it an affordable mid-range sedan to insure.

### **About the Hyundai Ioniq**

The [Hyundai Ioniq](<https://www.hyundaiusa.com/us/en/ioniq-family>) debuted in 2016 and is available in hybrid, plug-in hybrid, and all-electric variants. In 2017, the Ioniq was released in the United States. 

The small automobile is well-known for its low starting price, outstanding performance, excellent fuel efficiency, and nimble handling.

## **How safe is the Hyundai Ioniq?**

The Hyundai Ioniq 2017 has not been tested for safety ratings by the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety or the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration. The vehicle has an automated emergency braking system that may apply full force brakes to avoid an accident. Blind-spot recognition and lane change assist are two more important safety features.
