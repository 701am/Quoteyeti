## **About the Chevrolet Spark**

[Chevrolet Spark](<https://www.chevrolet.com/cars/spark>) has been on the market since 1998 when it was launched in South Korea. The subcompact vehicle made its US debut in 2009. The Chevrolet Spark is renowned for its superb handling and quickness. 

The vehicle is ideally suited for urban environments and is available exclusively in electric form.
