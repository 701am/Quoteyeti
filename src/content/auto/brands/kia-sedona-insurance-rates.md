According to CarInsurance.com, the cost of insurance for a [Kia](<https://www.kia.com/us/en>) Sedona is $190 a month, or $2,280 annually. The real cost might be more or lower based on many criteria, including your driving record, the number of miles you travel in a year, and various other considerations.

## **Other factors that can affect your auto insurance rate**

  * The Kia Sedona’s model year
  * The vehicle’s trim
  * The amount of coverage you need
  * The estimated miles your drive in a year
  * The vehicle’s safety and anti-theft features
  * The driver’s credit score
  * The driver’s location
  * The driver’s age
  * The driver’s driving history in the past five years
  * The driver’s marital status
  * Your (or the driver’s) gender
  * Whether the driver rents, leases or owns the vehicle



## **About the Kia Sedona**

Kia has been making the Kia Sedona since 1998 and it is in its third iteration at this time. Minivans are well-known for their high-quality interiors and safety rankings. The Kia Sedona is one of the best 12 family vehicles available today, according to Kelly Blue Book.

  * Anti theft system



## **How safe is the Kia Sedona?**

The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety ([IIHS](<http://iihs.org>)) has given the Kia Sedona a Top Safety Pick+ rating. The IIHS tested all the crash tests and graded them as "good." The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration ([NHTSA](<http://nhtsa.gov>)) rated the Kia Sedona as having a perfect overall safety rating, with five stars being the highest rating given. In the crash tests conducted by the NHTSA, the Kia Sedona got five stars for every test except for the rollover test, which was awarded four stars.

Automatic emergency braking, frontal collision warning, lane departure warning, and blind-spot monitoring are all new features of the new Sedona model.
