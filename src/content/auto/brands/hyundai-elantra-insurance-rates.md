## **How safe is the Hyundai Elantra?**

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) and the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS) have examined the newest model of the Hyundai Elantra for safety ratings. The NHTSA awarded the 2017 model a 4-Star Overall Safety Rating. The NHTSA gave the automobile four out of five stars in the frontal crash test and one out of five stars in the rollover crash test. The Hyundai Elantra was also designated an IIHS Top Safety Pick+, with the highest rating in all crash testing. The frontal collision prevention was likewise rated superior by the IIHS, earning it the best possible grade.

According to the NHTSA, approximately 450 Hyundai Elantra cars were stolen in 2013. Based on the number of models manufactured that year, this equates to a 1.14 percent theft rate.

The Hyundai Elantra has been in production since 1990 and is now in its sixth iteration. Since its inception, the vehicle has been manufactured in South Korea. The small vehicle is well-known for its outstanding fuel efficiency as well as it's high safety and dependability ratings. The firm introduced a sports variant to the North American market in 2017. There were around 208,000 sales in 2016.
