## **How safe is the Volkswagen GTI?**

The [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](<http://nhtsa.gov/>) awarded the 2017 Volkswagen GTI a five-star overall [safety rating](<https://www.jdpower.com/cars/2021/volkswagen/golf-gti>) (NHTSA). The NHTSA awarded the side crash test a five-star grade, the maximum attainable. Additionally, the government agency gave the vehicle a four-star safety rating in frontal and rollover crash tests. The Volkswagen GTI was designated a Top Safety Pick by the [Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS)](<https://www.iihs.org/ratings/vehicle/volkswagen/gti-4-door-hatchback/2021>). The IIHS awarded an excellent safety rating, the best attainable, to every vehicle subjected to crash tests.
