The [E-Class Mercedes-Benz](<https://www.mbusa.com/en/vehicles/class/e-class/sedan>) costs extra since it's a luxury car with a costly MSRP. This automobile also reaches the top 10 for the most stolen luxury car that adds to the insurers' risk.

## **About the Mercedes-Benz E-Class**

The Mercedes-Benz E-Class is now in its sixth iteration since 1993. The premium medium-sized automobile is famous for its great performance, smooth ride, and high-quality interior. More than 300,000 models and 55,000 sales were created in 2016 in the United States. The 2017 Mercedes-Benz E-Class was named among the top premium mid-size cars by the [U.S. News](<http://usnews.com>) & World Report.

## **How safe is the Mercedes-Benz E-Class?**

The E-Class Mercedes-Benz has been tested for safety ratings by the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration ([NHTSA](<https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle/2021/MERCEDES-BENZ/E-CLASS/4%2520DR/RWD>)). The Mercedes-Benz E-Class has a 5-star overall NHTSA safety rating. In all crash tests done by the government organization, the automobile got five stars.

The Mercedes-Benz E-Class' newest model has anti-locking brakes, stability control, and overhead airbags.

According to the [national insurance crime bureau](<https://www.nicb.org/>), from 2009 to 2012 over 381 Mercedes-Benz E-Class cars were stolen (NCIB). The vehicle is number four of the Top 10 Most Stolen Luxury Cars on the NCIB list.
