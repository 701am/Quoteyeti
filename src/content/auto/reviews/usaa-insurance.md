## Who USAA is best for

Based on QuoteYeti's evaluation, USAA fits drivers who want low rates and don't need extensive hand-holding. Membership restricted to active-duty military is the most important caveat to consider.

## How to get the best rate from USAA

The biggest rate drivers at USAA are safepilot (telematics) and storage discount. Stacking these discounts can reduce premiums by 15–30% depending on eligibility.

For drivers comparing USAA against competitors, focus on three things: the rate after all eligible discounts, the actual NAIC complaint ratio (not just J.D. Power's marketing-friendly scores), and the specific add-ons each carrier offers for your situation. The cheapest base rate isn't always the best total value.
