## Who Allstate is best for

Based on QuoteYeti's evaluation, Allstate fits drivers who want an established carrier with broad coverage options. Above-average base rates for most driver profiles is the most important caveat to consider.

## How to get the best rate from Allstate

The biggest rate drivers at Allstate are drivewise (telematics) and multi-policy bundle. Stacking these discounts can reduce premiums by 15–30% depending on eligibility.

For drivers comparing Allstate against competitors, focus on three things: the rate after all eligible discounts, the actual NAIC complaint ratio (not just J.D. Power's marketing-friendly scores), and the specific add-ons each carrier offers for your situation. The cheapest base rate isn't always the best total value.
