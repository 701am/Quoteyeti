## Who GEICO is best for

Based on QuoteYeti's evaluation, GEICO fits drivers who want low rates and don't need extensive hand-holding. Below-average customer satisfaction for complex claims requiring human intervention is the most important caveat to consider.

## How to get the best rate from GEICO

The biggest rate drivers at GEICO are driveeasy (telematics) and multi-car. Stacking these discounts can reduce premiums by 15–30% depending on eligibility.

For drivers comparing GEICO against competitors, focus on three things: the rate after all eligible discounts, the actual NAIC complaint ratio (not just J.D. Power's marketing-friendly scores), and the specific add-ons each carrier offers for your situation. The cheapest base rate isn't always the best total value.
