California has one of the most complex business insurance environments in the country, driven by strict workers' comp rules, AB-5 employment classification, and high property values along coastal areas.

## Workers' compensation

California workers' compensation is run as a competitive market with the State Compensation Insurance Fund as the carrier of last resort. Rates are some of the highest in the country, especially for construction, healthcare, and entertainment industries.

## Professional liability and E&O

California has aggressive professional liability environments, especially for healthcare and legal services. Errors and omissions claims are filed at high rates relative to other states.

## Key state-specific considerations

AB-5 worker classification rules significantly affect contractor/freelancer relationships. Many small businesses misclassify workers and face audits. The Department of Industrial Relations is active on enforcement.

## Shopping for business insurance in California

The same fundamentals apply in California as elsewhere:

1. **Get a BOP** (Business Owner's Policy) if eligible — bundles GL + commercial property + business interruption at a discount
2. **Work with an independent commercial broker** who can shop multiple carriers and understand California-specific requirements
3. **Verify your industry classification** annually — misclassification is a common cause of overpayment
4. **Build a safety program** if you have employees — your workers' comp experience modifier compounds year over year
5. **Match coverage limits to your contracts** — buying too much liability is wasted; buying too little can disqualify you from work
