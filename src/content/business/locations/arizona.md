Arizona has a moderate business insurance market with broad carrier availability and lower workers' comp rates than coastal states.

## Workers' compensation

Arizona workers' comp is required for all employers with 1+ employees. The state has historically been one of the more affordable workers' comp markets. Competitive private market with State Compensation Fund as carrier of last resort.

## Professional liability and E&O

Standard competitive E&O market.

## Key state-specific considerations

Some commercial property pricing affected by wildfire exposure in forested areas. Monsoon storm and hail risk in metro areas affects property rates.

## Shopping for business insurance in Arizona

The same fundamentals apply in Arizona as elsewhere:

1. **Get a BOP** (Business Owner's Policy) if eligible — bundles GL + commercial property + business interruption at a discount
2. **Work with an independent commercial broker** who can shop multiple carriers and understand Arizona-specific requirements
3. **Verify your industry classification** annually — misclassification is a common cause of overpayment
4. **Build a safety program** if you have employees — your workers' comp experience modifier compounds year over year
5. **Match coverage limits to your contracts** — buying too much liability is wasted; buying too little can disqualify you from work
