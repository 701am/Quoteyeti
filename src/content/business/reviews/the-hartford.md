## Who The Hartford is best for

Based on QuoteYeti's evaluation, The Hartford fits small business owners who value strong customer service and are willing to pay a modest premium for it. Higher base rates than digital-first competitors is the most important caveat to consider.

## How to get the best rate from The Hartford

The biggest rate drivers at The Hartford are aarp member and multi-policy bundle. Stacking these discounts can reduce premiums by 15–30% depending on eligibility.

For small business owners comparing The Hartford against competitors, focus on three things: the rate after all eligible discounts, the actual NAIC complaint ratio (not just J.D. Power's marketing-friendly scores), and the specific add-ons each carrier offers for your situation. The cheapest base rate isn't always the best total value.
