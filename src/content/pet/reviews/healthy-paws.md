## Who Healthy Paws is best for

Based on QuoteYeti's evaluation, Healthy Paws fits pet owners who value strong customer service and are willing to pay a modest premium for it. No wellness or preventive care option is the most important caveat to consider.

## How to get the best rate from Healthy Paws

The biggest rate drivers at Healthy Paws are bundling and a clean claims history. Bundling with other products is the most reliable way to lower your premium.

For pet owners comparing Healthy Paws against competitors, focus on three things: the rate after all eligible discounts, the actual NAIC complaint ratio (not just J.D. Power's marketing-friendly scores), and the specific add-ons each carrier offers for your situation. The cheapest base rate isn't always the best total value.
