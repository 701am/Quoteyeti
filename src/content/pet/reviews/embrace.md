## Who Embrace is best for

Based on QuoteYeti's evaluation, Embrace fits pet owners who want an established carrier with broad coverage options. Higher base rates than budget competitors is the most important caveat to consider.

## How to get the best rate from Embrace

The biggest rate drivers at Embrace are multi-pet and healthy pet deductible. Stacking these discounts can reduce premiums by 15–30% depending on eligibility.

For pet owners comparing Embrace against competitors, focus on three things: the rate after all eligible discounts, the actual NAIC complaint ratio (not just J.D. Power's marketing-friendly scores), and the specific add-ons each carrier offers for your situation. The cheapest base rate isn't always the best total value.
