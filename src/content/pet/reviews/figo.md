## Who Figo is best for

Based on QuoteYeti's evaluation, Figo fits pet owners who want an established carrier with broad coverage options. Higher premium for 100% reimbursement option is the most important caveat to consider.

## How to get the best rate from Figo

The biggest rate drivers at Figo are multi-pet and annual payment. Stacking these discounts can reduce premiums by 15–30% depending on eligibility.

For pet owners comparing Figo against competitors, focus on three things: the rate after all eligible discounts, the actual NAIC complaint ratio (not just J.D. Power's marketing-friendly scores), and the specific add-ons each carrier offers for your situation. The cheapest base rate isn't always the best total value.
