Pet insurance has exploded in popularity — and so have the gotchas. Pre-existing condition exclusions, lifetime caps, breed-specific carve-outs, and annual deductibles can turn an "85% reimbursement" plan into far less in practice. QuoteYeti's pet vertical compares carriers on the things that actually matter at claim time.
