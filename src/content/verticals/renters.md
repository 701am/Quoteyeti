Renters insurance is one of the best deals in insurance: a few dollars a month for tens of thousands in coverage and liability protection. But the carriers vary wildly on what they cover, how they pay claims, and whether they'll cover replacement cost or just depreciated value. QuoteYeti's renters vertical helps you pick a policy that actually pays when you need it.
